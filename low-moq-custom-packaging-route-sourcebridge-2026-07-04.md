## RFQ inputs buyers should prepare

- Product type and packed product dimensions.
- Target quantity and whether the request is one design or multiple designs.
- Preferred packaging structure or reference image.
- Material direction, print coverage and finish references.
- Artwork status and whether dielines or mockups are needed.
- Target market, sales channel and any buyer-supplied document request.
