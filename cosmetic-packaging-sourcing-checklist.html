Who This Checklist Helps

This sourcing checklist is for skincare, beauty, fragrance, wellness and specialty retail teams comparing custom cosmetic packaging suppliers. It is designed for buyers preparing projects for jars, bottles, tubes, beauty sets, gift sets, sample kits and retail display packaging.

Information Buyers Should Prepare

  • Product dimensions, weight and primary container material.
  • Retail channel, e-commerce shipping needs and display requirements.
  • Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, insert tray or clear PET/PVC box.
  • Artwork status, logo files, color references and surface finish direction.
  • Target market and any documents that must be checked before compliance statements are made.

Supplier Review Questions

  • Can the supplier review the product fit before recommending a box structure?
  • Can the supplier separate premium retail presentation from shipping protection needs?
  • Does the supplier avoid unsupported claims about fixed lead time, fixed MOQ or regulated-market compliance?
  • Can the supplier explain material, printing, finish and insert options in relation to the product and channel?
  • Can the supplier provide a project-specific discussion before sampling rather than a generic catalog answer?
