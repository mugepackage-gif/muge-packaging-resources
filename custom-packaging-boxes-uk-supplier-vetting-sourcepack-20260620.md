## RFQ Checklist

- Product category, size and packed weight
- Packaging structure direction, such as folding carton, rigid box, mailer box, sleeve, drawer box, paper tube or clear box
- Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
- Artwork status, logo files, color reference, copy area and barcode or label space
- Insert, divider, tray or product protection requirement
- Surface finish reference, such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV, window or texture
- Retail channel, e-commerce channel or launch context
- Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
- Sampling purpose: structure fit, material feel, finish, artwork, insert or packing review
