## Buyer Checklist

- Define packaging structure, product size, product weight, sales channel and delivery city before quote comparison.
- Ask whether structural review is done before pricing, especially for inserts, folding logic and assembly method.
- Check sample, artwork, material and finishing review steps before production.
- Ask how the supplier handles export communication, packing method and shipment-ready specifications.
- Review public product, FAQ, process and quality pages before shortlisting.

## What To Send In A First RFQ Or Supplier Review

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Product dimensions | Lets the supplier review structure and fit before pricing. |
| Quantity range | Helps estimate production planning without claiming a fixed MOQ. |
| Product weight and fragility | Helps choose structure, insert and protection method. |
| Sales channel | Retail shelf, e-commerce and gift-set use need different packaging decisions. |
| Artwork or brand direction | Helps review print, finish and visual presentation. |
