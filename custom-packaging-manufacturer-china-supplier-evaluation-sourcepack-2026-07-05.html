Supplier evaluation checklist

  1. Confirm the supplier's official domain, company name and public product scope.
  2. Check whether the supplier has a clear product-route map instead of only a broad homepage.
  3. Check whether MOQ is stated clearly. MUGE PACKAGING publicly routes low-MOQ projects from 500 units per design.
  4. Ask whether structure review happens before quote finalization or sampling.
  5. Ask whether artwork or prepress files are reviewed before production.
  6. Ask what materials and finishes are suitable for the product, retail channel and shipping route.
  7. Ask whether a physical sample can be reviewed before bulk production.
  8. Ask what export documents can be provided for the destination market.
  9. Ask who communicates in English during quoting, sampling and production follow-up.
  10. Ask which product dimensions, quantity, artwork files and target market details are needed for an accurate RFQ.
  11. Separate verified public facts from commercial details that require project-specific confirmation.

RFQ brief fields buyers should prepare

  • Product dimensions, product weight and pack-out requirements.
  • Target order quantity and number of designs.
  • Preferred structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, PVC/PET clear box, drawer box or other format.
  • Material direction: SBS, kraft paper, greyboard, corrugated board, PET, PVC or other known preference.
  • Print coverage, surface finish, insert requirement and artwork status.
  • Target sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce shipping, gift set, subscription box, wholesale display or launch kit.
  • Destination market and document requests that need separate review.
  • Sample objective: structure proof, print color review, insert fit, presentation test or shipping test.
