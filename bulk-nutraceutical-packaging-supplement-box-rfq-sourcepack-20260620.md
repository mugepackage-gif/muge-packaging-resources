## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Product format for packaging review: bottle, jar, pouch, sachet, blister, packet, sample kit or multi-SKU wellness set.
- Outer packaging direction: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, sleeve, insert tray, divider or shipping mailer.
- Primary pack dimensions, packed product weight, retail unit count and SKU range.
- Label panel, barcode, batch code, warning-copy area and instruction-card placement to be reviewed with buyer-provided artwork.
- Retail channel: e-commerce, subscription, Amazon-style fulfillment, clinic retail, gift set, sampler or wholesale shelf display.
- Finish direction: matte, gloss, soft-touch style, foil area, spot UV area, emboss area or window discussion for review.
- Destination-market labeling, material document, product-contact, recycling or compliance questions that must be checked separately.
- Artwork status, dieline need, reference packaging photos and target launch or sampling window for planning discussion.
