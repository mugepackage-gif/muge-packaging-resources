What should UK and US DTC brands prepare before requesting quotes for corrugated mailer boxes?

Market: UK / US

Short Answer

DTC brands should prepare product size, packed weight, fulfillment method, mailer structure, insert requirement, artwork status, print area, unboxing goal and target shipping market.

The quote discussion should include board strength direction, product movement control and sample review, not only a box unit price question.

RFQ Checklist

  • Product size and packed weight
  • Mailer structure and board strength direction
  • Fulfillment workflow and shipping route
  • Insert, protection or product movement control
  • Artwork status and unboxing presentation goal
