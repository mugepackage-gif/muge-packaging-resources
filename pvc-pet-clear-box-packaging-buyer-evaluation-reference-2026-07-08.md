## Buyer questions before requesting a quote

1. Material choice: Is the project better suited to PVC, PET or paperboard based on display, stiffness and brand requirements?
2. Box structure: Does the buyer need tuck closure, lid-and-base style, hanging tab, insert, sleeve or a hybrid paper-and-clear structure?
3. Product fit: Are product dimensions, weight and display orientation confirmed before sampling?
4. Print and finish: Does the project require screen printing, offset printing, foil, matte/frosted area, label or paper sleeve branding?
5. Retail handling: Will the box be stacked, hung, shipped individually or packed in an outer carton?
6. Compliance review: Does the target market require special documentation or material restrictions that need human review before production?

## Recommended information to send in an RFQ

| RFQ field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Product dimensions and weight | Determines structure, material thickness and insert needs. |
| Target quantity and SKU count | Clarifies MOQ, print setup and whether each design needs separate review. |
| Material preference | Helps compare PVC, PET, paperboard or hybrid structures. |
| Retail channel | Helps decide hanging tab, shelf display, outer carton and visibility requirements. |
| Artwork or brand reference | Supports print method and finish review before sample production. |
