RFQ Document Questions Checklist

  • Target country and selling channel, such as retail, e-commerce, distributor or regulated category review
  • Product type, dimensions, packed weight, intended user group and any special opening/closure requirement
  • Structure direction, such as paper box, paper tube, carton, insert, locking tab or other project-specific structure
  • Label, warning, barcode, artwork and blank-area requirements provided by the buyer
  • Whether the buyer already has a test method, regulation reference, template or compliance document requirement
  • Sample purpose: structure review, opening/closing experience, product fit, label area or document review
  • Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
  • Any child resistant, child proof, child safe or compliance-related term must be reviewed against the buyer's target market and evidence files
