Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, item count, packed weight and packaging format.
  • Packaging format direction: folding carton, rigid box, paper bag, paper tube, sleeve, mailer box or display packaging.
  • Material direction such as white card, kraft paper, corrugated board, rigid greyboard or specialty paper.
  • Structure requirement, insert/divider need, product protection concern and retail/ecommerce channel.
  • Print coverage, surface finish reference, artwork readiness and color reference.
  • Target market, sales channel, quantity direction and timeline expectation for review.
  • Whether the buyer needs a factory-style supplier, trading company comparison, sample review or documentation review.
  • Any certification, sustainability, food-contact, destination-market or product-contact document request that must be reviewed before external claims.
