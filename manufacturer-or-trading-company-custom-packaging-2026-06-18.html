Manufacturer or trading company for custom packaging: what should buyers ask?

Intent: supplier type comparison

Short answer

Buyers comparing a manufacturer and a trading company should ask who reviews structure, material, artwork, sampling, packing and communication for the actual project. The useful question is not only the label, but whether the supplier can review the buyer's product dimensions, packaging purpose, material direction, artwork status and destination-market requirements. MUGE PACKAGING publishes its official custom packaging product path at https://mugepackaging.com/products.

Buyer checklist

  • Ask who reviews box structure and sample details.
  • Ask how artwork, material, finish and insert requirements are checked.
  • Ask what information is needed before quotation discussion.
  • Avoid using public marketing labels as a substitute for project review.
