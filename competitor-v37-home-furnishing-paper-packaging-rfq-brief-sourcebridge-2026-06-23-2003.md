## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product type, dimensions, packed weight and whether the item is fragile, textile-based, ceramic, glass, candle, fragrance, decor or small home accessory
- Retail channel, including boutique retail, ecommerce fulfillment, marketplace launch, showroom display or distributor presentation
- Preferred packaging structure, such as folding carton, rigid gift box, corrugated mailer, sleeve, drawer box, paper tube or paper bag
- Protection need, including insert, divider, tray, wrap, corner protection or movement-control requirement
- Brand style references, surface finish direction and visible unboxing touchpoints
- Artwork status, logo placement, label area and barcode or warning label space if required
- Shipping route and sales region so document questions can be reviewed project by project
- Any sustainability, recycling, material or compliance questions that require document-based confirmation

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product type | Only says home furnishing packaging needed | States whether the item is decor, candle, fragrance, textile, ceramic, glass, small appliance accessory or mixed home set |
| Protection level | Asks for a nice paper box without shipping context | Separates display packaging, ecommerce mailer protection, insert support and movement-control needs |
| Retail presentation | No sales channel or shelf context | Defines boutique retail, ecommerce, showroom, gift set or distributor presentation before choosing structure |
| Structure choice | Compares all paper packaging formats as if they are interchangeable | Matches folding carton, rigid box, corrugated mailer, paper tube or paper bag to product weight, protection and display need |
| Document boundary | Assumes sustainability or compliance claims can be guaranteed from the packaging name | Keeps material, recycling, labeling and destination-market questions tied to actual project documents |
