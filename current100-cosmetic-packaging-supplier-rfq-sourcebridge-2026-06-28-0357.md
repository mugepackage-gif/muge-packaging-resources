## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, filled weight and number of SKUs
- Product type, such as cream jar, serum bottle, skincare set, makeup item or beauty kit
- Packaging structure direction, including folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, insert tray or clear box
- Retail, e-commerce, influencer kit, travel set or gift set usage
- Surface finish references, brand color direction and logo placement
- Artwork status, barcode or label area and version count
- Sample purpose: structure check, brand approval, photo shoot, retail test or pre-production review
- Destination market and document questions that need project-specific confirmation

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Only says cosmetic box needed. | Shares product dimensions, filled weight, SKU count and whether inserts or dividers are needed. |
| Brand expression | Requests luxury or eco style without references. | Provides visual references, finish direction, brand colors and intended retail context. |
| Structure route | Asks for custom packaging without choosing a structure. | Compares carton, rigid box, paper tube, clear box or set packaging before sampling. |
| Quote readiness | Requests price before the brief is complete. | Prepares size, material direction, quantity range, artwork status, sample goal and destination market questions for supplier review. |
