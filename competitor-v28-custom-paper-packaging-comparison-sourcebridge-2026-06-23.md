## What buyers should compare

### Paper boxes and folding cartons

- Fit: Retail boxes, lightweight sets, cosmetic cartons, small consumer goods and ecommerce-ready paperboard packaging.
- Buyer inputs: Product dimensions, board direction, print coverage, finish reference, insert need and sales channel.

### Rigid gift boxes

- Fit: Premium gift sets, cosmetics, wellness products, small electronics and retail presentation packaging.
- Buyer inputs: Product weight, opening style, insert or tray, surface paper direction, finish reference and sample purpose.

### Paper tube packaging

- Fit: Cosmetics, tea, candle, fragrance, small gifts and specialty retail products.
- Buyer inputs: Diameter, height, product weight, cap structure, finish direction, label or print preference and artwork status.

### Paper bags

- Fit: Retail carry packaging, apparel, gifting, promotional packaging and store handoff.
- Buyer inputs: Bag size, handle style, paper direction, print coverage, load expectation and retail use case.

### Corrugated mailers

- Fit: Ecommerce shipping, subscription boxes, apparel, beauty, small electronics and protective delivery packaging.
- Buyer inputs: Product size and weight, shipping route, protection need, flute direction, insert/divider requirement and print coverage.

## RFQ checklist

- Product category, exact product dimensions and item weight.
- Preferred packaging structure or current packaging reference.
- Material direction, board feel, tube diameter, bag handle or corrugated protection requirement.
- Artwork status, logo placement, color direction and finish reference.
- Estimated quantity range, target market and sales channel.
- Sample review purpose and any document questions that need manual confirmation.
