## Rigid Gift Box RFQ Checklist

Buyers should prepare these details before requesting rigid gift box samples or quotes:

1. Product category: cosmetics, apparel, electronics, wine, jewelry, wellness, subscription box, holiday set or specialty retail gift set.
2. Product dimensions, packed weight and how the item should sit inside the box.
3. Box structure: magnetic closure, drawer box, lid-and-base box, collapsible/folding gift box, sleeve or mixed set.
4. Insert or tray requirement, including paperboard insert, EVA/foam insert, molded pulp, divider, ribbon pull or product protection direction.
5. Surface finish direction, including matte, glossy, soft-touch, textured paper, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, ribbon or label-based decoration.
6. Artwork status, logo file status, dieline readiness, language requirements and barcode/label placement.
7. Sales channel, shipping path and destination market for document and packing review.
