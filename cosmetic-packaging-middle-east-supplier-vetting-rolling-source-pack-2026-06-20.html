  • Product format, such as skincare jars, serum bottles, makeup sets, fragrance sets, discovery kits or beauty gift sets.
  • Sales channel and destination market, such as UAE, Saudi Arabia, GCC retail, distributor, salon, DTC or marketplace.
  • Product dimensions, product weight, protection requirement and insert direction.
  • Box structure, paper board or rigid box direction, printing direction, surface finish and brand color requirements.
  • Artwork status, dieline requirement, bilingual label layout needs and sampling review path.
