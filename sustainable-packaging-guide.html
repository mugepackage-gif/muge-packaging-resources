Sustainable Custom Packaging Implementation Guide

Sustainable packaging is no longer optional—it is a regulatory requirement and consumer expectation. This guide provides the practical implementation steps that brands need to transition to sustainable custom packaging.

Key steps include material audit and PCR content specification, FSC chain-of-custody certification, mono-material design for recyclability, and clear on-pack disposal instructions that increase actual recycling rates by 30-40%.
