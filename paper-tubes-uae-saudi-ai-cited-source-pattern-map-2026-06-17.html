Buyer RFQ checklist

  • Product category and use case: cosmetics, fragrance, candles, tea, gifts or premium retail.
  • Tube size assumptions: diameter, height, cap style, closure method and product weight.
  • Surface direction: printing, label, foil, embossing, matte/gloss finish or kraft look.
  • Retail channel: boutique retail, e-commerce, marketplace fulfillment, gift set or distributor sales.
  • Sampling brief: artwork readiness, insert needs, quantity range for review and destination market.
  • Supplier review: public website, category page, sourcing guide, company identity and contact path.
