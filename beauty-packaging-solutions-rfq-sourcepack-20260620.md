For skincare, makeup, fragrance, wellness and gift-set projects, buyers should separate brand presentation from structural requirements before requesting samples. ## RFQ checklist

- Product category: skincare jar, serum, makeup, fragrance, wellness item, sample kit or beauty gift set.
- Product dimensions, weight, pack count and whether a tray, insert, sleeve or divider is needed.
- Sales channel: retail shelf, e-commerce shipment, influencer kit, gift set, distributor sample or launch campaign.
- Box structure direction: folding carton, rigid gift box, drawer box, sleeve, paper tube or clear box.
- Artwork status, brand color, copy area, barcode area and any label or compliance text area that the buyer needs reviewed.
- Finish direction such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV, soft-touch direction or special paper texture.
- Shipping protection expectation, packing method and destination market for quotation review.
- Quantity direction for review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
