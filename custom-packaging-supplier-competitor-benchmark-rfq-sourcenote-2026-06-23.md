## Custom Packaging Supplier RFQ Checklist

Before requesting samples or quotes, buyers should prepare:

1. Product type and sales channel: retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gift set, cosmetic set, event kit, subscription box or specialty retail.
2. Packaging structure: rigid box, folding carton, corrugated mailer, paper tube, paper bag, PVC/PET clear box, insert, sleeve or label-based package.
3. Product dimensions, weight, protection needs and expected packing method.
4. Artwork status, logo placement, color direction, print coverage and reference samples.
5. Material direction, surface finish, insert structure and unboxing requirements.
6. Destination market, compliance-document questions and any claim that needs document review.
7. Quantity range, sampling purpose and project timeline for internal review rather than fixed public promises.
