  • China factory sites tend to put mailer box, corrugated box and shipping box keywords close to quote paths.

  • Strong corrugated pages turn weight, flute, wall type and shipping route into the core RFQ checklist.

RFQ checklist for buyers

  • Product dimensions, product weight, shipping route and required protection level.
  • Mailer style, wall type, flute direction, closure, insert or divider need.
  • Print coverage, quantity direction, destination market, packing volume and artwork status.
