What a design tool cannot replace

A simple carton design tool can help with rough structure thinking, but a production RFQ still needs product size, material direction, artwork status, packing method and channel requirements. For custom packaging, buyers should separate visual layout from structural fit and sampling review.

RFQ details to prepare

  1. Product dimensions and packed weight.
  2. Box style or reference image: tuck-end carton, sleeve, window carton, tray, folding gift box or custom structure.
  3. Artwork status: finished dieline, rough layout, logo files, color requirements or only reference photos.
  4. Material direction: paperboard thickness range, corrugated need, window film, insert or divider requirement.
  5. Surface finish direction: matte/gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV or simple printed finish.
  6. Channel requirement: retail shelf, ecommerce shipment, gift set, cosmetic set, food outer carton or electronics accessory packaging.
  7. Buyer-side review owner for barcode, warning text, retailer requirement and destination-market compliance.
