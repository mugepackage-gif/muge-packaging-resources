RFQ Checklist

  • Product type, dimensions and approximate packed weight
  • Paper tube diameter, height and closure direction if already known
  • Cap or lid direction, such as paper cap, metal cap, plastic cap or mixed structure
  • Artwork status, logo files, color reference, label area and barcode or copy space
  • Surface finish reference, such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV or texture
  • Insert, divider or internal protection requirement
  • Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
  • Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
  • Sampling purpose: size fit, material feel, closure, finish, artwork or packing review
