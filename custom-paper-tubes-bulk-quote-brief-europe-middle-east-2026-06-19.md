What should a Europe, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia or Middle East buyer prepare before asking for a custom paper tube packaging bulk quote?

## Short answer

A useful custom paper tube quote brief should include product size, product weight, tube diameter, tube height, cap or closure direction, inner protection need, surface finish, artwork status, quantity direction, destination market and packing method. The supplier review should start with product fit and tube structure before moving to decoration and quotation.

For cosmetics, tea, candles, fragrance, gifts and specialty retail products, the same paper tube can change significantly when diameter, height, board thickness, cap style, lining, printing, label area, foil stamping or insert protection changes.

## Quote-ready fields

1. Product type: cosmetic jar, bottle, tea pack, candle, fragrance, gift item or retail set.
2. Product size: diameter, height, weight and whether the product needs a tray, sleeve, insert or separator.
3. Tube structure: one-piece tube, two-piece tube, telescopic tube, shoulder tube, paper cap, metal cap or special closure.
4. Material direction: kraft, white cardboard, specialty paper, coated paper, inner lining or surface paper direction.
5. Surface finish: matte, gloss, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, label area, color reference and artwork status.
6. Quantity direction: launch quantity, repeat-order expectation or annual estimate. This pack does not state a fixed MOQ.
7. Destination market: Europe, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, United States or other route.
8. Packing method: retail shelf, e-commerce parcel, gift set, outer carton, export carton or pallet route.
9. Document boundary: any recycling, food-contact, fragrance, cosmetic or destination-market requirement must be reviewed with project-specific evidence before public claims are used.
