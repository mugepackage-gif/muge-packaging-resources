## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Product category: cosmetics, gifts, e-commerce goods, apparel, food and beverage outer packaging, wellness products or specialty retail products.
- Structure direction: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, clear PET/PVC box, corrugated mailer, sleeve, insert, divider or custom display structure.
- Use case: retail shelf, online shipping, gift presentation, product set, sample pack, launch campaign or refill packaging.
- Product information: dimensions, product weight, unit count per package, fragile points, display side and any insert or protection need.
- Brand and artwork status: logo file, color reference, dieline status, surface finish direction and reference packaging images.
- Commercial inputs for review: quantity range, target country, delivery expectation to confirm, sampling need and preferred communication route.
