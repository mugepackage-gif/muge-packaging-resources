RFQ checklist for corrugated boxes

  1. Product dimensions and packed product weight.
  2. Box type: mailer box, shipping carton, display carton, insert set or outer carton.
  3. Shipping context: e-commerce parcel, retail delivery, wholesale carton, subscription kit or sample shipment.
  4. Protection needs: fragile item, multiple items, internal movement, stacking or return handling.
  5. Print requirements: outside print, inside print, full-color artwork, logo only or shipping label area.
  6. Material direction: single-wall, double-wall, flute reference or supplier recommendation needed.
  7. Quantity direction and number of designs.
  8. Destination market and any document questions that need project review.

What buyers should not assume

Corrugated packaging should be reviewed by product weight, protection needs, packing workflow and shipping route. Buyers should not assume fixed pricing, fixed schedule, guaranteed shipping performance, regulated-market approval, food-contact suitability, customer cases or production capacity from this public reference.
