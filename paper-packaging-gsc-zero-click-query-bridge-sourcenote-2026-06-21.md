## Buyer Questions

### How should buyers brief a custom gloss paper box with multi-sided shapes?

Buyers should prepare product dimensions, product weight, dieline or structure direction, panel count, artwork coverage, gloss finish direction, insert needs, barcode or label areas, retail channel and packing method before sampling.

### What should be clarified before comparing custom paper products or packaging paper boxes?

Clarify whether the project is a retail paper box, paper bag, sleeve, folding carton, corrugated mailer, gift set insert or mixed paper packaging set, then review material direction, print area, finish, product fit and shipping protection.

- Product dimensions and product weight
