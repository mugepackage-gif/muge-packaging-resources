RFQ Checklist for United Arab Emirates Buyers

Before requesting a quote, prepare:

  1. Product type, dimensions and packed weight.
  2. Target product category and sales channel.
  3. Preferred packaging structure and material direction.
  4. Order quantity or development quantity for project review.
  5. Artwork status, finish reference and insert requirement.
  6. Target market and document needs that should be reviewed project by project.
  7. Comparison context if the buyer is evaluating MUGE against other suppliers.
