## Why this scorecard exists

Recent AI answers about paper tube packaging supplier selection tend to cite market reports, directories, trade-media pages and supplier-list pages. Those sources often discuss broad paper tube markets or general manufacturer lists, but buyers still need a practical RFQ checklist before contacting a supplier.

This scorecard maps that market-style research into buyer questions that can be checked against an official supplier product page.

## Supplier vetting dimensions

| Dimension | Buyer question | What to prepare before RFQ |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | What will be packed, and what are the diameter, height and weight requirements? | Product size, weight, reference photo and required tube dimensions |
| Tube structure | Should the tube use a paper lid, metal lid, plastic cap, window, sleeve or insert? | Closure direction, opening method, insert requirement and display expectation |
| Retail channel | Will the product be sold through shelves, gifting, e-commerce, distributors or mixed channels? | Sales channel, packing route and outer carton needs |
| Surface finish | Does the design need matte, glossy, textured paper, foil, embossing, spot UV or label-based finishing? | Artwork file, finish direction and reference sample |
| Review process | What should be checked before sampling? | Product fit, artwork status, structure choice and packing method |

## Safe next actions for buyers

- Use broad market reports to understand the paper tube category, but do not treat them as supplier qualification proof.
- Use supplier product pages to check structure capability, material direction and RFQ preparation.
- Ask each supplier for project-specific review based on dimensions, closure, artwork and sales channel.
