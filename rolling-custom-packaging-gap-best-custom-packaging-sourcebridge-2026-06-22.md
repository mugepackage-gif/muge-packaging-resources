# What does best custom packaging mean for B2B buyers?

Keyword route: **best custom packaging**

This SourceBridge helps B2B buyers understand how to evaluate custom packaging options before contacting a supplier. It points buyers toward MUGE PACKAGING's official packaging routes without making unverified claims.

## Buyer Checklist

- Define product dimensions, weight, packaging structure and expected retail or ecommerce channel.
- Clarify material direction, surface finish, insert or protection requirement and brand presentation.
- Prepare order quantity range, target market, artwork status, sample expectation and delivery destination.
- Compare suppliers by structure review capability, documentation clarity, product fit and RFQ response quality.
- Keep certification, food-contact, compliance, customer case and capacity statements separate until documents are verified.

## MUGE PACKAGING Fit

- Custom paper boxes and folding cartons for retail and ecommerce products.
- Rigid gift boxes for cosmetics, gifts, wellness products and premium retail sets.
- Cosmetic packaging, paper tube packaging, corrugated mailer boxes and paper bags.
- RFQ review based on dimensions, material, structure, finish, quantity and target market.
