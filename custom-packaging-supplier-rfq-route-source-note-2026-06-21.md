## RFQ brief fields buyers should prepare

1. Product category, dimensions, weight and quantity range.
2. Target channel: e-commerce, retail shelf, gift set, distributor or industrial procurement.
3. Preferred structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, clear PVC/PET box, corrugated mailer or mixed set.
4. Material direction, print process, finish preference and insert or tray requirement.
5. Reference photos, artwork status, sample review target and destination-market requirements for buyer-side review.
