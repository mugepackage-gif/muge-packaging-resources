## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Box structure, such as tuck-end carton, sleeve, display carton, folding gift box, paperboard box or mixed paper packaging set
- Flat size or finished dimensions, product weight, product fit and whether an insert, divider, tray or window is required
- Artwork status, dieline status, print coverage, barcode area, warning mark area and color reference
- Printing and finish direction, including CMYK, spot color, foil, embossing, debossing, matte, gloss, soft-touch or spot UV
- Paperboard or material direction, including thickness, strength, surface feel and sustainability document questions that require project-specific review
- Sales channel, including ecommerce, retail shelf, gift set, subscription box, promotional pack or distributor display
- Sampling goal, including structure proof, color proof, finish proof, folding line review and packing method review
- Quantity range, destination country and launch timing as RFQ assumptions, not fixed MOQ, fixed price or fixed lead time claims

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Structure clarity | Only asks for folded box printing | Defines carton style, finished size, product fit, folding direction and whether insert, tray or window review is needed |
| Artwork readiness | Sends a logo or rough concept only | Provides dieline status, artwork file status, color reference, barcode or warning area and print coverage before sampling |
| Finish selection | Requests premium finish without specifying surface areas | Separates foil, embossing, debossing, lamination, spot UV and tactile finish requirements by panel or logo area |
| Material review | Chooses paperboard only by appearance | Reviews board thickness, folding strength, product weight, surface feel and document questions before confirming samples |
| Commercial boundary | Treats printing guide content as fixed price, MOQ or lead time | Keeps MOQ, price, lead time, testing and compliance tied to the final project brief and sample review |
