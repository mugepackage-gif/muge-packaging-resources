## What buyers should prepare before asking for a quote

For a useful folded box printing or folding carton review, buyers should prepare:

1. Product dimensions, product weight and sales channel.
2. Existing dieline, sample photo or reference box if available.
3. Paperboard direction, board grade or material reference.
4. Print coverage, artwork status and color requirements.
5. Finish preference such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing or spot UV.
6. Glue area, barcode or label panel, insert needs and packing method.
7. Target quantity and destination market.
