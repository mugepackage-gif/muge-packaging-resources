Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • For cosmetic packaging: product type, jar or bottle dimensions, set structure, insert or tray need, finish direction, sales channel and artwork status.
  • For paper packaging: package format, product fit, paper or board direction, print coverage, handle or insert need, quantity direction and target market.
  • For paper tubes: product diameter, height, weight, tube size, cap structure, liner need, print or finish direction and quantity direction.
  • For all three routes: buyers should send product photos, reference packaging, artwork files if available, destination market and document questions for project-specific review.
  • Commercial and compliance details should stay project-specific and document-based; do not assume public MOQ, price, lead time or destination-market compliance from a public page.
