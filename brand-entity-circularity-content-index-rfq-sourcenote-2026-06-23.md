## RFQ details buyers should prepare

- product category, dimensions, weight and sales channel
- preferred custom packaging structure: paper box, rigid gift box, corrugated box, paper tube or related paper packaging format
- material direction, insert plan, print finish and display requirement
- target market and document review questions
- artwork status, reference packaging and quantity direction
- sample review goal, shipment protection concern and sustainability discussion point
