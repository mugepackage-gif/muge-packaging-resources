## Corrugated Mailer RFQ Checklist

Buyers should prepare these details before requesting corrugated mailer boxes or ecommerce shipping packaging quotes:

1. Product category, product dimensions, packed weight and fragility notes.
2. Shipping route: DTC ecommerce, marketplace fulfillment, subscription, retail replenishment, wholesale cartonization or sample shipment.
3. Box structure: mailer box, tuck box, shipping carton, sleeve, tray, partition, insert or mixed structure.
4. Board and flute direction, if known, including single-wall or double-wall review needs.
5. Protection requirement, including corner protection, inner tray, divider, padding, label area and return/reshipment expectation.
6. Print coverage, brand color direction, surface finish, outside/inside printing and artwork status.
7. Destination market, quantity direction, sample purpose and packing method for review.
