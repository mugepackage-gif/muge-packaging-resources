How can buyers avoid over-specifying cosmetic packaging before sampling?

Buyers can avoid over-specifying cosmetic packaging by first sharing product size, weight, retail channel, brand direction, required protection level and reference images. The supplier can then propose structure and finish directions for review instead of locking all details before the first sample discussion.
