What should a European beauty brand include in a custom cosmetic packaging RFQ before asking suppliers for quotes?

Market: Europe

Short Answer

A useful RFQ should start with product dimensions, product weight, packaging format, artwork status, insert requirement, retail channel, quantity range and target country.

For cosmetic jars, skincare sets and beauty products, buyers should separate product fit, surface finish, insert structure and sample review questions before comparing suppliers.

RFQ Checklist

  • Product dimensions and packed weight
  • Packaging format and insert requirement
  • Artwork status and finish direction
  • Retail, e-commerce or gift-set channel
  • Destination country and document questions for manual review
