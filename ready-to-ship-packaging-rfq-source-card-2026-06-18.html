RFQ Checklist

A ready to ship packaging RFQ should include the following details before supplier comparison:

  • Box style and standard size direction
  • Product dimensions and product weight
  • Logo method: printing, hot stamping, label or sleeve direction
  • Quantity direction for project-specific review
  • Packing method and carton requirement
  • Destination market and delivery arrangement for review
  • Whether the buyer needs plain stock boxes, custom logo boxes or a mixed packaging set
  • Any artwork, reference image or retail channel notes

Buyer Notes

Europe stock packaging buyers and Middle East stock packaging buyers often compare available box type, standard size direction, logo method, packing method and shipment arrangement before selecting a supplier.

When comparing a ready to ship packaging manufacturer China option, request project-specific stock status and shipment review instead of relying on fixed MOQ, fixed lead time or generic availability claims.
