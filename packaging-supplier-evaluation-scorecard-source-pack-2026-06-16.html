    • Supplier evaluation scorecard

    1. Product fit

  • Does the supplier ask for product dimensions, packed weight and sales channel before recommending a packaging structure?
  • Can the supplier discuss product fit, insert requirement, protection needs and display direction together?
  • Can the supplier review multiple structure directions instead of forcing one generic box style?

    • 2. Category fit

    Useful category checks include:

  • Cosmetic packaging for skincare jars, serums, creams, beauty sets and retail products.
  • Rigid gift boxes for premium product sets and retail gifting.
  • Paper tube packaging for cosmetics, candles, tea, fragrance and specialty products.
  • Clear PVC/PET boxes for display-oriented packaging.
  • Corrugated boxes and mailers for e-commerce logistics.

    • 3. RFQ readiness

    Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

  • Product dimensions and packed weight.
  • Target region and sales channel.
  • Preferred packaging structure or reference packaging.
  • Artwork status and surface finish direction.
  • Insert, divider, tray, closure or window requirement.
  • Destination market and document review needs.
