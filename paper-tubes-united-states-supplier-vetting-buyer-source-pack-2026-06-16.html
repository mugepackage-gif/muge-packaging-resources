    • United States buyer checklist for custom paper tube packaging

    Before asking for a quote, a United States buyer should normally prepare:

  • Product type, target channel and intended use, such as candles, fragrance, cosmetics, tea, small gifts or specialty retail.
  • Tube diameter, height, product weight and fit tolerance.
  • Closure direction, such as rolled edge, paper cap, metal cap, plastic cap or project-specific review.
  • Artwork status, printing direction and surface finish preference.
  • Insert or protection requirement if the product is fragile or sold as a set.
  • Quantity range for project review, without treating any public number as a fixed MOQ claim.
  • Destination country and documentation expectations for review before production.
