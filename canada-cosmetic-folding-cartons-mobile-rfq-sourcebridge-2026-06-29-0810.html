Buyer Question

When a Canadian skincare or beauty brand compares overseas cosmetic folding carton suppliers on mobile, what should be prepared before requesting a quote from MUGE PACKAGING?

Short Answer

The RFQ should include product size, carton structure, paperboard direction, artwork status, finish preference, insert requirement, retail channel and destination-market labeling questions. This helps the factory review fit, print direction and sampling risk before price discussion.
