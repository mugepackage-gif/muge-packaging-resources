## RFQ checklist

- Product dimensions, weight and whether the item needs a snug fit or loose display space.
- Tube diameter, height, wall thickness direction and cap or closure style.
- Product category and sales channel: cosmetics, candles, tea, gifts, retail sets, sample kits or e-commerce.
- Printing coverage, artwork status, label area and barcode or compliance copy area.
- Surface finish direction such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing or special paper texture, if relevant.
- Insert, divider, inner wrap or product-stability requirement.
- Packing method, shipping protection expectation and destination market.
- Approximate quantity direction for review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
