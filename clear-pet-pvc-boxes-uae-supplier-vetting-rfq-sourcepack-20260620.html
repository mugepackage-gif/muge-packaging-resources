RFQ Checklist

  • Product dimensions, packed weight and display channel
  • Material direction, such as PET, PVC or another transparent material for project review
  • Transparency, stiffness, fold-line, scratch-risk and protection expectations
  • Printing coverage, logo area, barcode panel, warning label area or clear-window requirement
  • Hanging hole, tuck flap, sleeve, insert tray, paperboard base or folding structure direction
  • Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
  • Retail, boutique, supermarket shelf, e-commerce, distributor carton or event display route
  • Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
  • Sampling purpose: product fit, clarity, foldability, printing, insert, display angle or packing review
