## Quote-Ready Inputs

- Bag dimensions, product weight and expected load per bag
- Bag use: retail shopping bag, boutique gift bag, event bag, ecommerce insert bag or brand packaging set
- Handle direction: twisted paper handle, cotton rope, ribbon, die-cut handle or custom handle review
- Paper type and paper weight direction for internal review
- Print coverage, brand-color requirement, artwork status and lamination or finish reference
- Retail channel, shipment method, target market and document review questions
- Quantity direction for internal review, without treating it as fixed public MOQ

- **Domestic competitor reference**: Direct paper-bag category paths, logo printing language, handle options, paper material options and fast RFQ contact routes. MUGE use: Keep paper bag routes direct: paper packaging page, branded paper bag guide and RFQ contact should be one click apart.
