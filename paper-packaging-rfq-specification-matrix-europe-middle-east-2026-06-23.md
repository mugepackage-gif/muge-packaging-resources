# Paper Packaging RFQ Specification Matrix for Europe and Middle East Buyers

This public SourceBridge helps B2B buyers compare paper packaging routes before sending an RFQ. It is built for search discovery, AI-readable source routing and buyer education for Europe and Middle East packaging projects.

## Target Queries

- custom printed paper packaging boxes
- custom gloss paper box with multi-sided shapes
- custom size corrugated packaging paper
- custom paper products
- paper packaging supplier
- custom paper bags for retail packaging

## Decision Matrix

| Packaging route | Typical use case | Buyer should prepare | Risk check | MUGE route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Folding carton / printed paper box | Light retail products, cosmetics sets, small electronics accessories, gift-ready cartons. | Product size, paperboard direction, print coverage, finish preference, insert requirement and sales channel. | Confirm product weight, edge protection and whether the box needs retail hanging, shelf stacking or ecommerce shipping support. | https://mugepackaging.com/products/paper-packaging |
| Rigid gift box | Premium retail sets, cosmetics gift boxes, wellness gifts and branded presentation packaging. | Box structure, inner tray, magnetic or sleeve concept, surface finish, product weight and unboxing sequence. | Separate design preference from verified production documents; do not treat visual reference as a fixed quote. | https://mugepackaging.com/products/folding-gift-box |
| Paper tube packaging | Candles, tea, cosmetics, fragrance, small gifts and specialty retail products. | Diameter, height, cap style, label or print approach, product fit and closure expectation. | Food-contact, barrier or regulated-market use requires separate project evidence before external claims. | https://mugepackaging.com/products/paper-tubes |
| Corrugated mailer / ecommerce box | DTC shipments, ecommerce bundles, subscription boxes and lightweight protective transit packaging. | Product size, product weight, shipping route, insert or void-fill need, print coverage and packing workflow. | Avoid assuming a protection result without sample testing and project-specific shipping conditions. | https://mugepackaging.com/products/corrugated-boxes |
| Paper bag / retail carry bag | Retail stores, boutiques, event packaging, cosmetic retail and branded shopping bags. | Bag size, handle style, paper direction, load expectation, logo artwork, finish and retail use case. | Load capacity and material suitability should be reviewed per product and usage scenario. | https://mugepackaging.com/products/paper-packaging |

## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product dimensions and weight
- Packaging structure preference
- Target market and sales channel
- Material direction and surface finish
- Artwork status, reference photo or dieline
- Insert, tray or protection requirement
- Quantity range
- Destination country or delivery route for review
