    • Paper tube supplier vetting checklist

    1. Product fit

    Buyers should define the product size, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Paper tube packaging can be relevant for cosmetics, candles, fragrance, tea, small gifts and specialty retail products; UK buyers should review product fit, tube diameter, tube height, closure style, insert direction, retail display route, artwork status and document review requirements before sampling.

    2. Structure questions

    Useful supplier questions include:

  • What product category, diameter, height and closure direction should be reviewed for the packed product?
  • Does the buyer need a paper lid, metal lid, plastic cap, window, insert, sleeve or label direction?
  • Will the product be displayed upright, shipped in cartons, used for gift packaging, sold through e-commerce or placed in UK retail channels?
  • Does the design require matte, glossy, textured paper, foil, embossing, label-based finishing or artwork file review?

    • 3. RFQ preparation

    Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

  • Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  • Preferred packaging structure, size direction and material direction, if known.
  • Sales channel and destination market.
  • Artwork status and surface finish direction.
  • Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
  • Target quantity range for review, without assuming a preset order minimum.

    • 4. Comparing supplier types

    When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
