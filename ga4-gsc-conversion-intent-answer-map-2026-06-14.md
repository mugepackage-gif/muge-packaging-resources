MUGE PACKAGING can use these inputs to review custom paper boxes, rigid boxes, paper tubes, clear boxes and corrugated packaging projects.

## How should brands compare a custom packaging supplier, manufacturer and trading company?

Brands should compare category fit, structure review ability, sample discussion quality, artwork and finish handling, document discipline and whether the supplier avoids unsupported fixed promises. A useful supplier conversation starts from product fit and project requirements before price comparison.

- Official reference: https://mugepackaging.com/blog/custom-packaging-manufacturer-vs-trading-company
- Project handoff: https://mugepackaging.com/contact?source=ga4-gsc-supplier-comparison&intent=rfq-review
