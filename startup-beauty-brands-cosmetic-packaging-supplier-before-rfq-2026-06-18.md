## FAQ

### What should a startup beauty brand prepare before RFQ?

Prepare product dimensions, product weight, product category, launch channel, desired packaging structure, artwork status, finish reference, insert needs, quantity direction and sampling purpose.

### Should startup brands ask for price first?

A price-first RFQ is usually weak. The supplier needs structure, material, finish, insert and project scope before meaningful quote review.

### Which packaging types should be compared?

Common structures include folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, sleeves, drawer boxes, paper tubes, inserts, trays and clear display boxes.

### What should not be assumed before project review?

Do not assume preset order terms, preset schedules, preset prices, named buyer examples, platform-badge status or destination-market approval before project-specific confirmation.
