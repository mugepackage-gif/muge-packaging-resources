## Practical buyer checks

- Confirm product diameter, product height, packed weight and whether the tube needs an inner tray or divider.
- Compare closure style, cap structure, paperboard thickness, lining requirement and surface finish.
- Check whether the tube is for retail display, gift presentation, shipping protection or a refill set.
- Prepare artwork direction and finish requirements before asking for sample cost or production review.
- Do not treat paper tube packaging as automatically suitable for every liquid, food or regulated product without project review.
