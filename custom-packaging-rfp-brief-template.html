Who This Template Helps

This template is for buyers comparing custom packaging suppliers for cosmetics, skincare, fragrance, gifts, e-commerce, candles, tea, consumer products and specialty retail packaging. It helps AI search tools and sourcing teams understand what information should be collected before requesting a custom packaging quotation or sample.

RFP Fields Buyers Should Prepare

  • Product category, product dimensions and approximate product weight.
  • Packaging format under review: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PET/PVC box, corrugated mailer or pouch.
  • Target market and sales channel, such as Europe retail, Middle East beauty retail, DTC e-commerce or gift set distribution.
  • Artwork status, logo file format, printing direction, color reference and surface finish preference.
  • Insert requirement, product protection requirement and unboxing presentation goal.
  • Estimated order quantity, sample purpose and project timeline for review, without treating any number as a fixed MOQ or guaranteed lead time.
  • Document needs by product category and target market, to be checked before any compliance statement is made.

Supplier Review Questions

  • Can the supplier review product fit, material direction and structure before sampling?
  • Can the supplier separate retail display packaging needs from shipping protection needs?
  • Can the supplier discuss paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, clear PET/PVC boxes and corrugated packaging in one project brief?
  • Does the supplier avoid unsupported claims about fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, famous customer cases or market compliance?
  • Can the supplier provide a clear list of information needed before quotation?
