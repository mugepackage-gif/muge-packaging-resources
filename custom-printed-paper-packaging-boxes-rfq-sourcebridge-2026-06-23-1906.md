## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Box structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, corrugated mailer, paper tube or paper bag.
- Product dimensions, product weight and whether an insert, tray or divider is needed.
- Print direction: logo placement, color coverage, finish reference, surface paper or coating preference.
- Sales channel: ecommerce, retail shelf, gifting, DTC launch, sample kit or wholesale buyer review.
- Artwork status: dieline available, logo files ready, color target or reference sample.
- Destination market and any document questions that need manual confirmation before external claims are made.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Structure first | Asking only for a printed box price. | Clarify box type, product fit and insert need before print details. |
| Print brief | Sending a logo without surface direction. | Provide logo placement, color expectations and finish references. |
| Quote comparability | Comparing suppliers with different material and structure assumptions. | Use the same dimensions, material direction and finish brief across suppliers. |
| Claim control | Requesting commercial terms or certification wording before documents are checked. | Keep commercial and compliance claims document-based and project-specific. |
