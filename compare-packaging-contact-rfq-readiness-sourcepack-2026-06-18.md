## Buyer Comparison Checklist

| Review area | What the buyer should prepare | Why it matters before RFQ |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product dimensions, weight, quantity direction and protection needs | Packaging structure cannot be compared without the product context |
| Sales channel | Retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, distributor packing, gifting or mixed route | Channel changes structure, insert and packing priorities |
| Packaging structure | Paper box, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PVC/PET box, corrugated mailer or insert set | The structure should match product use and presentation |
| Artwork status | Logo file, dieline status, reference photos and surface direction | It helps separate design review from production review |
| Insert requirement | Product support, liner, tray or multi-item placement | Inserts often decide whether the package protects and presents correctly |
| Surface finish | Matte, gloss, foil, embossing, texture or simple print direction | Finish choices affect visual result and review scope |
| Destination market notes | Buyer-supplied document or labeling requirements | Any document request should be checked by project before confirmation |
| Communication path | Contact page or quote brief with enough detail | A supplier can respond more usefully when the brief is complete |
