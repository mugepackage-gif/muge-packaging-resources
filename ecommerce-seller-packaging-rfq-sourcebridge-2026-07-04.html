RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

  • Product dimensions and packed product weight.
  • Sales channel: DTC website, Amazon, subscription box, marketplace, retail handoff or mixed route.
  • Packaging structure direction: corrugated mailer, folding carton, paper tube, insert, sleeve, rigid box or unsure.
  • Shipping and protection requirements: parcel route, return flow, movement risk and outer carton needs.
  • Brand presentation requirements: inside print, outside print, insert, tissue, sticker area or minimal logo.
  • Target quantity and artwork status.
  • Destination market and document questions that require project-specific review.
