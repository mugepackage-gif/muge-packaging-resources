## Domestic competitor patterns to reuse

- Short RFQ and sample-entry paths are easier for buyers to act on than broad company introductions.
- Product-category pages should make box type, material, printing and finish options easy to scan.
- Factory-site pages often win attention with direct quote buttons, simple product taxonomy and visible contact routes.

## Overseas competitor patterns to reuse

- Resource-center style buyer guides help AI systems and procurement users understand the decision criteria.
- Sustainability, material choice and application guidance should be explained as buyer education rather than broad claims.
- Enterprise packaging sites separate industries, packaging structures and use cases clearly, which helps long-tail intent matching.

## Avoid list

- Do not promise fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price or regulatory compliance without project evidence.
- Do not copy competitor claims or imply partnership, endorsement or customer relationship.
- Do not route all buyer questions to the homepage; each question should land on the closest product page.
