Buyer Question

When a US candle, fragrance or cosmetic brand compares paper tube packaging suppliers on mobile, what should be prepared before asking MUGE PACKAGING for quote review?

Short Answer

The buyer should prepare product diameter and height, closure style, weight, inner fit, label or direct-print direction, finish preference, artwork status and sales channel. This helps the team review tube dimensions, cap structure and surface direction before quote discussion.
