## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, product weight and number of items in one selling unit.
- Sales channel: retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gift set, subscription box or event campaign.
- Structure candidates: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, PVC/PET clear box or corrugated packaging.
- Protection need, display need, insert requirement and expected unboxing style.
- Artwork status, print coverage, color target, surface finish references and dieline availability.
- Destination market and document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Asking for custom packaging without product size, weight or selling-unit notes. | Share product dimensions, product weight and whether the package needs inserts, windows or extra protection. |
| Sales channel | Comparing structures without saying where the package will be sold or shipped. | Separate retail display, e-commerce shipping, gift-set presentation and event campaign needs before choosing the structure. |
| Structure choice | Only asking whether paper box, rigid box or paper tube is better. | Compare folding cartons for lightweight retail items, rigid boxes for premium presentation, paper tubes for cylindrical or specialty retail products, clear boxes for visible display and corrugated boxes for shipping or protection. |
| Claim control | Requesting fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, food-contact approval or sustainability claims before documents are reviewed. | Keep commercial, sustainability, food-contact and destination-market compliance claims document-based and project-specific. |
