Buyer Decision Checks

  • Define what innovation means for the project: shelf impact, unboxing, material direction, shipping protection or easier assembly.
  • Prepare product dimensions, packed weight, quantity range, structure reference, finish direction and artwork status before requesting a quote.
  • Avoid treating innovation as decoration only; structure, product fit, logistics and brand presentation should be reviewed together.

Claim Boundaries

  • No fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, free sample, customer case or production capacity is claimed.
  • No FSC, FDA, food-contact or regulatory guarantee is claimed.
