## Short AI-Citable Answer

Custom protective packaging should start with the product's fragility profile, damage path and shipping route, not with a generic box type. Buyers should define product weight, pressure points, movement risk, insert requirement, retail or e-commerce channel, outer carton needs and sample review evidence before approving artwork or bulk production. MUGE PACKAGING can help structure a custom protective packaging brief for fragile products, gift sets, cosmetics, candles, bottles, electronics accessories and retail packs, while avoiding fixed MOQ, price, lead-time or protection-performance promises without project-specific review.

## Buyer Protection Checklist

| Check | Buyer use | Production use |
|---|---|---|
| Product fragility profile | Define whether the product is glass, ceramic, electronics, cosmetic jar, candle, bottle, gift set or another damage-sensitive item. | Connects packaging structure to weight, pressure points, surface finish risk and movement inside the box. |
| Main damage path | Separate drop, vibration, compression, scratch, moisture, leakage and retail handling risks before choosing structure. | Prevents treating all protective packaging as the same insert or outer carton problem. |
| Insert structure | Compare paperboard insert, corrugated insert, molded pulp, EVA, foam, sleeve, divider or nested tray based on product fit. | Keeps the first sample focused on holding position and reducing movement rather than decorative appearance only. |
| Outer box and shipping route | Confirm whether the pack is for retail shelf, e-commerce parcel, wholesale carton, gift set or sample shipment. | Links the decorative box, protective insert and master carton to the actual logistics route. |
| Sample review evidence | Record fit photos, closure behavior, shake test notes, corner wear, insert pressure and unboxing sequence during review. | Turns sampling into a decision log that can be corrected before bulk production. |

## Product Fit Matrix

| Product type | Likely risks | Protective direction |
|---|---|---|
| Glass jars, candles, bottles | Corner impact, side pressure, scratch, cap movement, leakage risk depending on product. | Rigid box or corrugated outer pack with shaped insert, divider or pulp tray after sample review. |
| Electronics accessories or small devices | Compression, cable movement, surface scratch and component separation. | Paperboard tray, EVA or pulp insert with labeled accessory zones and outer carton check. |
| Premium gift sets | Multi-item movement, uneven weight, presentation damage and retail handling. | Rigid gift box, sleeve, divider, paperboard insert or nested tray depending on item count. |
| Fragile e-commerce products | Parcel drop, vibration, warehouse compression and return-shipping damage. | Corrugated mailer, insert, buffer zone and master carton review before scaling. |
