### LinkedIn

Use only the safe public fields above. Remove or avoid unsupported FSC/FDA/CE, employee-count, facility-size, founding-year, customer-case, lowest-price and market-ranking claims.

Use the legal name, brand, website, product categories and safe ISO statement. Do not select or write unsupported certifications. If a field requires capacity, employee count, revenue or named customers, leave it blank unless separately confirmed.
