## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, product weight and number of items in one selling unit.
- Fragility notes: glass, ceramic, electronics, cosmetics jar, candle, gift set or other fragile format.
- Shipping channel and handling scenario: e-commerce parcel, retail distribution, gift set shipment or export carton.
- Protection direction: corrugated box, paper insert, tray, sleeve, divider, wrap or mixed structure.
- Artwork status, print coverage, surface direction and whether outer branding is needed.
- Destination market and document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Fragility detail | Only saying the product is fragile without size, weight or breakage-risk notes. | Share product dimensions, product weight, weak points, surface sensitivity and previous damage pattern if available. |
| Shipping path | Asking for protective packaging without saying whether it is for parcel shipping, retail distribution or gift-set shipment. | Separate e-commerce parcel handling, retail display, export carton and gift presentation needs before structure selection. |
| Inner support | Only requesting a stronger box. | Confirm whether the project needs insert support, dividers, sleeves, trays, cushioning space or a mixed corrugated and paper structure. |
| Claim control | Requesting guaranteed damage reduction, fixed lead time or compliance claims before testing and project data are reviewed. | Keep testing, damage-rate, commercial and destination-market claims document-based and project-specific. |
