Buyer Decision Checks

  • Identify the product category before selecting the packaging structure.
  • Prepare product dimensions, product weight, quantity range, material direction, print coverage and artwork status.
  • Separate retail presentation, ecommerce shipping, gifting, and protective needs before asking for price.
  • Do not promise fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, or unverified compliance claims.

Claim Boundaries

  • No fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, free sample, customer case or production capacity is claimed.
  • No FSC, FDA, food-contact or regulatory guarantee is claimed.
