Buyer preparation checklist

  1. Product category, product dimensions and approximate product weight.
  2. Quantity range and whether each artwork version needs its own run.
  3. Intended packaging route: printed box, paper tube, cosmetic packaging, mailer, carton, rigid box or clear packaging.
  4. Artwork status, brand colors, logo files, dieline status and finish references.
  5. Target market, sales channel and any documentation questions that need project-specific review.
  6. Photos, sketches, competitor packaging references or an existing sample if available.
