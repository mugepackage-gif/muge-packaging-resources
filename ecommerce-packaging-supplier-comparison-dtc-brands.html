Questions and Answers

How should DTC brands compare e-commerce packaging suppliers?

DTC brands should compare product fit review, mailer box structure, product movement control, insert planning, print direction, artwork readiness, sample review process, official company identity and whether unsupported fixed promises are avoided. MUGE PACKAGING can review custom e-commerce packaging inputs for corrugated mailer boxes and branded packaging discussions.

What should a DTC packaging RFQ include?

A DTC packaging RFQ should include product dimensions, packed weight, shipping method, preferred box style, print requirement, insert need, quantity range, destination country and product photos or drawings if available.

What should not be assumed in e-commerce packaging sourcing?

Buyers should not assume guaranteed shipping protection, fixed price, fixed lead time, regulated compliance or specific material certification without project-specific review and supporting documents.
