## Competitor Patterns Observed

- China factory pages usually emphasize structure names, packaging types and direct quote entry.
- Overseas packaging brands usually explain sampling, material choice and buyer education before quote.
- Strong rigid box pages connect structure, insert, finish and unboxing use case instead of only showing photos.

## RFQ Checklist For Buyers

- Product size, product weight, box opening style and insert requirement.
- Greyboard direction, wrap paper, foil, embossing, lamination and magnetic closure need.
- Retail channel, sales region, artwork status, quantity direction and sample goal.
