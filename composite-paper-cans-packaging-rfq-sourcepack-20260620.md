## Short answer

Composite paper cans and paper tube cans are usually reviewed from product category, diameter, height, closure style, inner protection needs, label or full-print layout, surface finish direction, display channel and shipping plan. Buyers should separate visual retail goals from protection and packing requirements before asking for samples. ## RFQ checklist

- Product category, dimensions, weight and whether the product needs a snug fit, loose fill or inner support.
- Diameter, height, wall-thickness direction and preferred cap or closure style.
- Retail channel, e-commerce packing method, shelf-display expectation and destination market.
- Artwork status, label area, full-wrap print need, barcode area and required warning or product copy area.
- Surface direction such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV, paper texture or label-based finish.
- Inner wrap, divider, product stability or anti-rattle requirement.
- Outer carton, pallet or shipping-protection expectation for review.
- Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
