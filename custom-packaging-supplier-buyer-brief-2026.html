What buyers should prepare before contacting a custom packaging supplier

  1. Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  2. Preferred packaging structure, or examples of packaging styles the buyer wants to compare.
  3. Quantity direction, SKU count and whether the project is a launch, replenishment, sample run or seasonal edition.
  4. Material direction, such as paperboard, kraft paper, greyboard, corrugated board, PET or PVC clear packaging.
  5. Print and finish expectations, such as full-color printing, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV or soft-touch lamination.
  6. Artwork status, including whether dielines, logo files or existing packaging references are available.
  7. Target market and documentation questions that need project-specific review.

How to compare custom packaging suppliers

Buyers should compare more than unit price. For custom packaging projects, supplier fit usually depends on structure review, artwork review, sample approval, communication clarity, export documentation and whether the supplier can explain the packaging route before production starts.
