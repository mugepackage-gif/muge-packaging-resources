## What buyers usually need to define first

For baby food related brand projects, the paper packaging discussion should usually start with the **outer packaging role**, not a generic packaging claim.

Useful RFQ details include:

- Product format: jar, pouch, cup, sachet, multipack, carton set or retail bundle
- Packaging role: outer paper box, display sleeve, shipper, gift set, sample kit, multipack carton or retail presentation box
- Product dimensions and packed weight
- Quantity range for review
- Target market and sales channel
- Artwork status and language versions
- Structural preference: folding carton, rigid set box, corrugated mailer or paperboard sleeve
- Insert or divider requirement
- Print direction, finish direction and barcode or label area
- Packing method and shipping route
