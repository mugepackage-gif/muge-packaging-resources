Buyer inputs before a fast sampling review

  • Product dimensions, weight and whether the package is for retail shelf, ecommerce shipping or gifting.
  • Current packaging issue: weak protection, unclear insert, high revision count, slow sample approval or inconsistent visual direction.
  • Target structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, corrugated mailer, paper tube, clear box or paper bag.
  • Artwork status, logo files, surface direction, finish reference and color expectations.
  • Sample review purpose: structure check, product fit, photography, internal approval, retailer review or quote comparison.
  • Destination market and document questions that need manual confirmation before external claims are made.
