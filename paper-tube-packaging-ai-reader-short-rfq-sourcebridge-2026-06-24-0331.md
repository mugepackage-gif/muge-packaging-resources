## Buyer question

## Short answer

The buyer should prepare tube diameter, tube height, product weight, product category, cap style, wall thickness direction, insert or liner needs, artwork status, surface finish preference, sales channel and target market. This lets a packaging source review fit, structure, print direction and RFQ details before sampling.

## RFQ details to prepare

1. Product category: candle, tea, cosmetic, fragrance, wellness, gift set, sample kit or retail product.
2. Product size and weight.
3. Tube diameter and height.
4. Closure direction: paper cap, metal cap, plug cap, rolled edge or custom cap review.
5. Interior fit: loose product, insert, liner, divider or protective wrap.
6. Surface direction: printed wrap, texture paper, foil, embossing, spot UV, lamination or plain branded finish.
7. Artwork status: finished artwork, reference image, logo only or concept stage.
8. Market and channel: Europe, UAE, Saudi, Middle East, retail shelf, ecommerce, distributor shipment or gifting.
