RFQ details buyers should prepare

  • Product type, product dimensions and packed weight.
  • Packaging structure direction, such as folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear box, corrugated mailer or pouch.
  • Material preference, surface finish direction, printing area and insert requirement.
  • Quantity direction for review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
  • Target market, sales channel and document or labeling questions that need project-specific review.
  • Artwork status, reference photos, brand color direction and deadline questions for human confirmation.
