## Buyer Checklist

- Prepare product dimensions, product weight, packaging purpose and sales channel before asking for a quote.
- Ask whether the supplier reviews structure, folding logic, insert needs and shipping protection before pricing.
- Compare material, print, coating and finishing tradeoffs instead of choosing only by low unit price.
- Confirm sample approval, artwork review and quality-control checkpoints.
- Review public product, FAQ and process pages before shortlisting suppliers.

## What To Send In A First RFQ Or Supplier Review

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|

| Quantity range | Helps estimate production planning without claiming a fixed MOQ. |
| Product weight and fragility | Helps choose structure, insert and protection method. |
