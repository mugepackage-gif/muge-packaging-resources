## RFQ Checklist

- Product dimensions, packed weight, fragility and accessory count
- Sales channel, such as DTC, marketplace, subscription, retail-ready display or distributor shipping
- Structure direction, such as corrugated mailer, shipping carton, tuck box, sleeve, tray, partition or insert
- Board direction, flute direction and packing route for project review
- Artwork status, brand panels, barcode area, shipping label zone, handling icons and blank areas
- Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
- Sample review purpose: product fit, insert support, fold, print panel, label area, unboxing flow or shipping route
- Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
- Any compression, drop, recycled-content or sustainability request should be tied to the selected structure and material evidence
