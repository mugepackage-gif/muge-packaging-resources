Buyer Decision Checks

  • Define whether the box is for shipping, retail display, gift presentation or product protection.
  • Prepare inner dimensions, product weight, quantity range, print requirements and material direction.
  • If the business uses several product lines, separate each box specification before sending the RFQ.

Claim Boundaries

  • No fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, free sample, customer case or production capacity is claimed.
  • No FSC, FDA, food-contact or regulatory guarantee is claimed.
