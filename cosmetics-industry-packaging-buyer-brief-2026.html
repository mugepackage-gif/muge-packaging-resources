Industry RFQ checklist

  1. Product type: skincare jar, serum bottle, tube, palette, fragrance item, makeup set or beauty kit.
  2. Primary package dimensions and how the product should sit inside the secondary packaging.
  3. Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, sleeve, tray, divider or insert set.
  4. Retail context: e-commerce, store shelf, gift set, launch kit, subscription set or wholesale pack.
  5. Material direction and surface finish reference.
  6. Artwork status, dieline readiness and number of SKUs.
  7. Quantity direction and whether the project is a new launch, refill order or test run.
  8. Target market and document questions that need project review.

Buyer fit notes

Cosmetics and beauty packaging projects often need early structure review because insert tolerance, outer box strength, surface finish, color consistency and retail presentation affect both sampling and production planning. The best first brief includes product dimensions, reference packaging images, artwork status and target quantity direction.
