Buyer brief checklist

  1. Product dimensions and product weight.
  2. Preferred box structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, drawer box, sleeve box, two-piece box, corrugated mailer or other paper structure.
  3. Target order quantity and number of designs.
  4. Material direction: SBS, kraft paper, corrugated board, greyboard or specialty paper, if known.
  5. Print coverage: logo only, single-color, full-color, inside printing or multi-panel artwork.
  6. Finish requirements: lamination, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, soft-touch or textured paper.
  7. Insert, tray, divider or product-fit requirements.
  8. Target sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce shipping, gift set, subscription box, wholesale display or event launch.
  9. Destination market and delivery route.
  10. Artwork status: finished dieline, logo files, reference packaging or design concept.
  11. Any document or labeling request that needs project-specific review.

What the supplier should review before sampling

  • Whether the structure fits the product size and weight.
  • Whether the material choice supports the retail or shipping channel.
  • Whether the dieline, glue area and folding sequence are practical.
  • Whether inserts, trays or dividers are required.
  • Whether print and finish expectations match the target price level.
  • Whether destination-market labeling or document requests need separate confirmation.
