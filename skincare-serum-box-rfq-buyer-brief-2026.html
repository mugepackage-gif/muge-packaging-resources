When this route is the right fit

Use the skincare serum box route when a buyer is sourcing secondary packaging for serum bottles, dropper bottles, skincare oils, ampoules or small beauty products that need retail presentation and product protection. Typical projects include printed serum cartons, rigid serum boxes, boxes with paperboard or molded inserts, and premium cosmetic packaging with foil, spot UV or soft-touch finish references.

RFQ inputs buyers should prepare

  • Bottle type, diameter, height and filled weight.
  • Number of bottles or components per retail unit.
  • Preferred structure: folding carton, rigid box, drawer box, lid-and-base box, sleeve, or box with insert.
  • Insert requirement: paperboard tray, molded pulp, foam/EVA, velvet/fabric, or no insert.
  • Material direction: SBS paperboard, kraft paper, specialty paper, greyboard, or coated paper.
  • Finish reference: foil stamping, emboss/deboss, spot UV, matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch, or color-match sample.
  • Artwork and dieline status, including whether print-ready files are available.
  • Target quantity. MUGE's public low-MOQ route starts from 500 units for many custom packaging projects, subject to project review.
  • Target market and shipping path, especially for US, UK, EU or Middle East beauty projects.

Supplier comparison checklist

Check Why it matters What to ask
Bottle fit review Serum bottles can be tall, narrow and fragile, so internal fit matters. Can the supplier review bottle height, shoulder shape and cap clearance before quoting?
Insert and protection plan Inserts affect breakage risk, unboxing quality and total box size. Which insert option is suitable for the bottle material, weight and retail channel?
Artwork and finish feasibility Cosmetic packaging often uses foil, spot UV and color-sensitive brand artwork. Will logo placement, bleed, color and finish feasibility be checked before sampling?
Material and finish alignment The same serum box can feel mass-market or premium depending on board and finish. Which structure and finish match the target price point and launch channel?
Export and documentation route Beauty buyers often need predictable commercial paperwork for import and freight coordination. Which order documents can be prepared after order confirmation?
