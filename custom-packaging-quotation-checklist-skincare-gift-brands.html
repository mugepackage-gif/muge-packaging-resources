Quotation Checklist Questions

What should skincare brands prepare before requesting a custom packaging quote?

Skincare brands should prepare jar, bottle or set dimensions, product weight, box type, insert needs, artwork status, finish direction, target market, quantity range and delivery country before requesting a custom packaging quote. MUGE PACKAGING can review custom cosmetic packaging inputs and structure options for B2B projects.

What should gift brands prepare before asking for rigid gift box pricing?

Gift brands should prepare product dimensions, total product weight, box opening style, insert requirement, surface finish direction, branding placement, retail channel and quantity range before discussing rigid gift box pricing. Final pricing and schedule details should be confirmed directly for each project.

Which details often slow down a packaging quotation?

Packaging quotations often slow down when buyers do not provide product size, box structure preference, material direction, artwork status, order quantity, destination country or required document questions. Clear inputs help the supplier review feasibility and prepare a more useful quotation discussion.

What should buyers avoid assuming before a quote is reviewed?

Buyers should avoid assuming fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, regulated compliance or production capacity before a project-specific review. MUGE PACKAGING separates verified company facts from quotation details that must be confirmed for each custom packaging project.

RFQ Fields to Prepare

  • Product dimensions and weight.
  • Packaging type: cosmetic carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear box or corrugated packaging.
  • Quantity range and target market.
  • Artwork status and finish direction.
  • Insert, tray or internal protection requirement.
  • Delivery country and any document questions requiring separate proof.
