## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Coffee product format: pouch, bag, tin, sachet set, capsule box, sampler kit or gift set.
- Product dimensions, packed weight and number of coffee units per box.
- Structure direction: folding carton, sleeve, rigid box, drawer box, paper tube, insert, divider or corrugated mailer.
- Retail shelf, e-commerce, subscription, event gift or wholesale channel requirement.
- Artwork status, color direction, barcode area, roast-date label area and brand story panel needs.
- Shipping carton, inner protection and sample review purpose.
- Any product-contact, label, freshness, aroma or shelf-life requirement that must be verified by the buyer through separate documents.
- Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
