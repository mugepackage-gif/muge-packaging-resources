Buyer Question

What should an organic brand prepare before asking for custom sustainable packaging?

Practical RFQ Inputs

Organic brands should prepare the product category, dimensions, packed weight, sales channel, retail or ecommerce use case, packaging structure preference, artwork status, material direction, insert requirement, sample question and any retailer document questions.

For paper-based packaging, the structure may involve a folding carton, rigid gift box, paper sleeve, paper tube, insert, divider, corrugated mailer, paper bag or combined retail-and-shipping package. Structure, material, print coverage and surface finish should be reviewed together because sustainability wording alone does not define a complete packaging specification.
