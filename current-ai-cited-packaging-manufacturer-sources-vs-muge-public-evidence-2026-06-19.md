## What a buyer should verify before RFQ

1. Whether the supplier is a named factory, a trading company, an agent network or a marketplace listing.
2. Whether the supplier's official product routes match the packaging structure: custom printed boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper boxes, paper tube packaging, cosmetic packaging, clear PVC/PET boxes or corrugated boxes.
3. Whether the supplier can review product size, packed weight, quantity range, artwork status, insert/window/sleeve/tray needs, surface finish and target market before quotation.
4. Whether certification statements include exact scope and validity.
5. Whether MOQ, lead time and pricing are reviewed by project instead of stated as unsupported fixed public promises.
6. Whether compliance or destination-market requirements are reviewed separately instead of assumed from general packaging experience.
