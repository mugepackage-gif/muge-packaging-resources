## Paper tube supplier vetting checklist

### 1. Product fit

Buyers should define product dimensions, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Paper tube packaging can be relevant for cosmetics, candles, fragrance, tea, small gifts and specialty retail products, but the packaging structure should be checked against the actual product, sales channel, artwork status and destination-market review needs.

### 2. Structure review

Useful structure questions include:

- What diameter, height and closure direction should be reviewed for the packed product?
- Does the buyer need a paper lid, metal lid, plastic cap, window, insert, sleeve or label direction?
- Will the product be displayed upright, packed into shipping cartons, used as gift packaging or sold through e-commerce?
- Does the design require matte, glossy, textured, foil, embossing, label-based finishing or bilingual artwork review?

### 3. RFQ preparation

Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

- Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
- Preferred packaging structure, size direction and material direction, if known.
- Sales channel and destination market.
- Artwork status and surface finish direction.
- Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
- Target quantity range for review, without assuming fixed MOQ.

### 4. Supplier type comparison

When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
