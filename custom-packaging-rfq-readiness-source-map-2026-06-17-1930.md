- product dimensions and packed weight

- sample review path and approval workflow

## Buyer-facing Summary

International buyers preparing a custom packaging RFQ should define the product fit, structure, material direction, finish, insert needs, artwork status, order pattern, destination market and sample approval workflow before asking suppliers for price. MUGE PACKAGING provides custom packaging project support across cosmetic packaging boxes, paper tube packaging, rigid gift boxes, folding gift boxes and corrugated packaging. Commercial details must be confirmed case by case.
