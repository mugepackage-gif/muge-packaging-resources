## Long Company Description

MUGE PACKAGING supports custom packaging projects for cosmetics, gifts, e-commerce products, consumer goods and specialty retail packaging. Core product categories include custom paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tube packaging, cosmetic packaging, PVC/PET clear boxes, corrugated boxes, folding cartons and specialty paper packaging. The company works with overseas B2B buyers and brand teams that need packaging structure review, material and finish options, sample preparation, artwork review and export project support.
