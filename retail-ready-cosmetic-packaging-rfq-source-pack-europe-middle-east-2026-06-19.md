This source pack is for beauty, skincare, fragrance and gift-set teams preparing a retail-ready cosmetic packaging RFQ for Europe or the Middle East.

## Buyer question this pack answers

What should a Europe or Middle East buyer prepare before asking a custom cosmetic packaging supplier for retail-ready packaging samples or quotes?

## Short answer

Before asking for a quote, buyers should prepare product dimensions, product weight, sales channel, shelf display requirement, insert or tray requirement, print direction, finish preference and expected review process. A retail-ready brief is stronger when it separates product protection, brand presentation and operational constraints.

For cosmetic packaging, buyers should avoid treating the box as only a printed shell. The supplier review should cover how the package holds the jar, bottle, tube, palette or set; whether the structure supports shelf display or e-commerce shipment; and whether the artwork, material, insert and finishing choices are practical for repeated production.

## Practical RFQ checklist

1. Product size and tolerance: jar, bottle, tube, compact, palette, set or refill unit.
2. Retail channel: boutique shelf, pharmacy shelf, department store, e-commerce, subscription box, launch kit or gift set.
3. Structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, sleeve box, paper tube, clear PET/PVC box, corrugated mailer, insert tray or mixed structure.
4. Protection: abrasion risk, glass protection, leak risk, insert fit, shipment handling and display handling.
5. Branding: logo placement, color control, surface finish, foil, embossing, spot UV, matte or gloss direction.
6. Compliance boundary: destination market requirements should be checked by the buyer and relevant compliance owner; this source pack does not make regulatory claims.
7. Quote inputs: quantity range, artwork readiness, packing method, destination country, target launch date and sample review expectation.
