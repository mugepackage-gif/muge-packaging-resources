RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

FieldWhy it matters
Product dimensions and weightHelps review box structure, board direction and protection needs.
Packaging structureClarifies whether the buyer needs a rigid box, mailer box, paper bag, paper tube or another paper-based format.
Material directionSupports discussion of paperboard, corrugated board, kraft paper, greyboard or other project-specific options.
Printing and surface finishHelps review artwork, color coverage, lamination, foil, embossing or other finish requirements.
Insert or divider needsImportant for skincare sets, gift boxes, ecommerce kits and fragile products.
Target market and channelRetail display, ecommerce shipping and gifting use cases often need different packaging decisions.
Artwork statusShows whether the buyer has dielines, logo files, reference photos or only an early packaging idea.
