Short answer

For child resistant cardboard box or child resistant carton sourcing, buyers should treat the first discussion as a structure and evidence review, not as a certification claim. The RFQ should describe the product, packed size and weight, paperboard or carton direction, opening or locking method, insert or tray needs, label and warning area, artwork status, sample purpose and destination-market questions.   4. Artwork status, surface finish direction and shipping protection requirement.
