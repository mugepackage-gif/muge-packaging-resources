## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Finished artwork or print size, paper thickness, frame or no-frame status and whether the pack must stay flat during shipping
- Required mailer structure, such as flat corrugated mailer, rigid paperboard mailer, sleeve, insert, backing board or protective wrap
- Quantity range, destination country, sales channel and whether the order is for ecommerce, gallery retail, subscription, event merchandise or brand fulfillment
- Print and branding direction for the mailer itself, including outside print area, inside print, label area, barcode area and color reference
- Protection review needs, including edge protection, bend resistance, surface scuffing risk, moisture exposure and packing method
- Artwork file and dieline status, including whether the buyer needs a structure proof, print proof or packing trial before confirming production
- Document questions such as material statement or sustainability wording, kept project-specific and not treated as a broad compliance guarantee

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Flatness and bend control | Only asks for artwork mailers | Defines print size, product thickness, flatness requirement, shipping route and whether backing board or insert review is needed |
| Mailer structure | Chooses a mailer only by appearance | Compares flat corrugated mailer, rigid paperboard mailer, sleeve and insert options against product weight and handling risk |
| Branding and print | Sends a logo without layout details | Provides print area, color reference, label or barcode area and whether inside printing is required |
| Sampling purpose | Requests a sample without saying what to verify | Separates structure proof, print proof, bend-resistance review and packing trial before confirming the final brief |
| Commercial boundary | Treats a guide as fixed price, MOQ or lead time | Keeps MOQ, price, lead time, testing and document statements tied to the final project brief |
