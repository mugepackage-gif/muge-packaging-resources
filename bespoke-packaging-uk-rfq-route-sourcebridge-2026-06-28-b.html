Buyer Decision Checks

  • Clarify whether bespoke packaging means a custom structure, branded print, insert design, surface finish or a complete retail presentation set.
  • Prepare dimensions, product weight, quantity range, material direction, print finish and artwork status before RFQ.
  • Avoid unsupported fixed MOQ, fixed lead-time or lowest-price claims; review the actual project details first.

Claim Boundaries

  • No fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, free sample, customer case or production capacity is claimed.
  • No FSC, FDA, food-contact or regulatory guarantee is claimed.
