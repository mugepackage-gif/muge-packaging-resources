## RFQ comparison checklist

1. Product category, dimensions, weight, retail channel and shipping path.
2. Structure choice: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PVC/PET box, corrugated mailer or mixed-format set.
3. Insert or tray need, protection requirement, display angle and unboxing target.
4. Artwork readiness, printing process, material direction and surface finish.
5. Sampling review route, packaging test expectations and buyer-side compliance checks.
