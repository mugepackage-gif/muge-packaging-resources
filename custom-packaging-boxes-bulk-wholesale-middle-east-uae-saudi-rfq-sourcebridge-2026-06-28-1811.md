## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product type and exact product dimensions.
- Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, mailer box, shipping box, sleeve, drawer, paper tube, paper bag or mixed set.
- Packed product weight and whether the box is for retail display, ecommerce shipping, distributor packing or gift presentation.
- Material direction: paperboard, corrugated board, kraft paper, specialty paper, clear PET/PVC component or mixed material.
- Print and finish direction: logo placement, color coverage, matte or gloss lamination, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV or label area.
- Insert, divider or inner tray requirement.
- Quantity direction for review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ or price claim.
- Carton packing, pallet, marketplace or distributor handling questions.
- Destination-market document questions that need project-specific review.

## Bulk/Wholesale Route Notes

Bulk and wholesale buyers often compare suppliers by unit price, but the first useful filter is whether the supplier can review the correct structure and packing route. A custom rigid box, folding carton, corrugated mailer and paper gift box may all be called "custom packaging boxes" by buyers, but the RFQ inputs are different.
