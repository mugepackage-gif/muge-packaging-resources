## RFQ Checklist for Custom Paper Bags

Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

- Bag purpose: retail shopping, gift handoff, event giveaway, ecommerce insert or boutique packaging.
- Product fit: expected contents, weight range and whether the bag carries boxed products, apparel, cosmetics or small gifts.
- Bag structure: handle type, bottom shape, reinforcement needs and approximate size.
- Material direction: kraft paper, white paper, coated paper or other paper-board direction for review.
- Print and finish direction: logo placement, color count, surface finish and any artwork status.
- Quantity range and target market: Germany, broader Europe, UK, Middle East or mixed export market.
- Packing and logistics context: flat packing, carton packing and destination-market label area if needed.
