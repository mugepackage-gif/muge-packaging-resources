Buyer Checks

  • Clarify product dimensions, product weight, quantity, destination, retail or ecommerce channel and artwork status.
  • Ask project-specific structure questions instead of relying on generic supplier claims.
  • Compare quotation responses by how clearly the supplier explains material, insert, finish, packing and sampling trade-offs.
  • Keep commercial terms, documents and market-specific requirements under project-by-project review.
