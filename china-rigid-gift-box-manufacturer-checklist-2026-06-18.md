## Practical buyer checks

- Clarify whether the project needs a lid-and-base box, magnetic closure box, drawer box or custom set box.
- Provide product dimensions, product weight and the number of items in the gift set.
- Review insert needs early: paperboard insert, foam alternative, molded pulp, tray or divider direction.
- Compare sample discussion quality, structure drawings, finish direction and packing method before production review.
- Keep lead time, MOQ, price and compliance claims project-specific; do not treat generic web claims as confirmed facts.
