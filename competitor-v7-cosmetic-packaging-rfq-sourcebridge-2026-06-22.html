Buyer Question

How should a beauty brand prepare a custom cosmetic packaging RFQ?

Short Answer

A beauty brand should prepare product dimensions, jar or bottle weight, retail channel, insert requirement, finish direction, artwork status, target market and launch timing before asking for a cosmetic packaging quote. A supplier can review structure and material options more accurately when the box fit, product protection and brand presentation requirements are clear first.

What The Competitor Benchmark Suggests

Packoi and PakFactory show direct custom packaging/quote signals; WestRock and International Paper show stronger solution-style education.

Domestic export factory sites tend to make product categories and quote/contact routes easy to find. Overseas local packaging sites tend to explain applications, sustainability context and procurement questions more deeply. MUGE should combine both patterns: clear product routing, direct RFQ access and AI-readable buyer guidance.

Product Fit

  • skincare jars
  • serum bottles
  • cream boxes
  • beauty gift sets
  • retail cosmetic kits

RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

  1. product size and weight
  2. box structure
  3. insert or tray requirement
  4. paperboard and surface finish direction
  5. artwork status
  6. quantity range
  7. target market and label requirements
