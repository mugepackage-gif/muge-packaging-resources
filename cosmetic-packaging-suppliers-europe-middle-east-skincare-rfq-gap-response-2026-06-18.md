## Supplier Comparison Checklist

- Define the skincare product type, primary container size and product weight before comparing suppliers.
- Compare whether each supplier can review box structure, insert or tray protection, retail display and e-commerce shipping needs.
- Prepare artwork status, finish reference, quantity direction and destination market before requesting a sample or quote.
- For Europe and Middle East projects, list any document request for project review instead of assuming a supplier certificate applies to every item.
- Avoid selecting only by low price; compare fit, structure, surface finish, packing route, communication clarity and RFQ response quality.
