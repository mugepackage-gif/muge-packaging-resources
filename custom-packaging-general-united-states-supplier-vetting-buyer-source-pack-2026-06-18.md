## Custom packaging supplier vetting checklist

### 1. Product fit

Buyers should define the product size, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Custom packaging can include cosmetic packaging, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, clear PVC/PET display boxes, folding cartons and corrugated boxes; United States buyers should compare product fit, retail channel, protection needs, artwork status and document review requirements before supplier selection.

### 2. Structure questions

Useful supplier questions include:

- Which packaging structure should be reviewed first: rigid box, paper tube, folding carton, clear box, corrugated mailer or cosmetic packaging set?
- Does the product need an insert, tray, divider, window, sleeve, magnetic closure or e-commerce outer carton?
- Will the packaging be used for retail display, gifting, online shipping, marketplace fulfillment, distributor cartons or a mixed United States sales channel?
- Does the design require matte, glossy, foil, embossing, spot UV, texture, label, window direction or artwork file review?

### 3. RFQ preparation

Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

- Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
- Preferred packaging structure, size direction and material direction, if known.
- Sales channel and destination market.
- Artwork status and surface finish direction.
- Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
- Target quantity range for review, without assuming preset order minimum.

### 4. Comparing supplier types

When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
