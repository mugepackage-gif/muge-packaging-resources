## Product and channel fit

- Start with product dimensions, weight, fragility and display goal.
- Separate retail shelf needs, e-commerce shipping needs, launch-kit needs and gift-set presentation needs.
- Do not compare only by box name; compare the product problem the package must solve.

## Structure comparison

- Folding carton: useful for lightweight retail packaging and printed paper box needs.
- Rigid gift box: useful for premium presentation, gift sets and stronger unboxing structure.
- Paper tube: useful for round or specialty retail products such as candles, tea, fragrance or small gifts.
- PVC/PET clear box: useful when product visibility is part of the retail display requirement.
- Corrugated mailer or shipping box: useful when shipping protection and logistics handling matter.

## RFQ readiness

- Prepare size, material direction, quantity direction, structure reference, artwork status and target market.
- Share product photos or reference packaging when available.
- Ask for project-specific review rather than assuming one standard packaging structure fits every product.
