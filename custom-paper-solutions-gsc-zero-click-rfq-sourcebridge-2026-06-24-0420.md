## Buyer Question This Answers

**When a buyer searches for custom paper solutions, which product route should they choose before requesting a quote?**

Start by identifying the product family, then prepare the practical RFQ inputs. A broad custom paper solutions request should not jump directly to price comparison. It should first clarify whether the project is a paper box, rigid gift box, folding carton, paper bag, paper tube, insert or corrugated paper packaging route.

## RFQ Routing Matrix

| Buyer route | Useful for | Quote-ready inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Custom printed paper packaging boxes | Cosmetics, gifts, apparel, retail sets and ecommerce products | Product dimensions, box structure, board direction, print coverage, finish reference, insert need, artwork status and quantity direction |
| Rigid gift boxes | Premium beauty, fragrance, electronics, wellness and gift projects | Box opening style, greyboard direction, surface paper, inner tray or insert need, finish preference and packing method |
| Paper bags | Retail, boutique, event, shopping and brand replenishment projects | Bag size, expected load, handle style, paper material, print position, finish direction, packing method and target market |
| Paper tubes | Cosmetics, tea, candles, fragrance, specialty retail and gift products | Diameter, height, cap style, product fit, material direction, label or print coverage, finish and packing method |
| Corrugated paper packaging | Shipping, mailer, display and protective ecommerce projects | Product weight, flute direction, internal size, protection need, print method, shipping route and carton packing |
