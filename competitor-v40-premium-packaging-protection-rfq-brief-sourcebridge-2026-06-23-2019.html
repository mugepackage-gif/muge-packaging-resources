Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, packed weight, material fragility and whether the item is glass, ceramic, cosmetic jar, fragrance, electronics accessory, gift set or home decor
  • Sales channel and handling path, including boutique retail, ecommerce shipping, distributor transport, sample kit, showroom display or gift campaign
  • Presentation structure, such as rigid box, folding carton, corrugated mailer, sleeve, drawer box, paper tube or mixed packaging set
  • Protection requirement, including insert, divider, tray, wrap, corner support, movement-control need or drop/handling review question
  • Unboxing and visual requirements so protection does not conflict with premium presentation
  • Surface finish direction and material feel references
  • Artwork status, label area and any mandatory warning or handling mark area
  • Target market and document questions that require project-specific review
