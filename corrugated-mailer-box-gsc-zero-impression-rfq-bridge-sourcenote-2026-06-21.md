## Buyer Questions

### What should an ecommerce buyer prepare before asking for corrugated mailer boxes?

Prepare product size, product weight, shipping route, protection needs, print coverage, opening style, insert or divider need, quantity direction, artwork status and target market.

### How should buyers compare mailer boxes and regular shipping boxes?

Compare product fit, edge crush direction, board grade direction, print surface, assembly time, insert protection, return experience and shipping channel rather than only price.
