## Buyer checklist for tea paper tube packaging

1. Confirm product type: loose tea, tea bags, gift tea set, candle, cosmetic item or fragrance-sensitive product.
2. Confirm tube dimensions: inner diameter, height, wall direction, cap style and product fit.
3. Review closure and liner direction: paper lid, metal lid, plastic cap, inner liner or window requirement.
4. Review presentation: matte, glossy, textured, foil, embossing, label or printed wrap.
5. Review shipping and retail handling: carton packing, stacking, shelf display and gift presentation.
6. Ask for project-specific document review when material, compliance, food-contact or destination-market questions matter.
