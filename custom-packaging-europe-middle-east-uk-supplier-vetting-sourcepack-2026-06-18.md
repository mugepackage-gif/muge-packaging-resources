## Supplier Vetting Matrix

| Review area | Buyer question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Company source path | Can the buyer find an official product overview and contact path? | Use an official website page first; do not rely only on copied directory text. |
| Product structure fit | Does the supplier discuss rigid boxes, paper tubes, clear boxes, cosmetic packaging or corrugated boxes according to the product? | Structure fit is stronger than a generic supplier claim. |
| RFQ readiness | Does the buyer have product dimensions, quantity direction, artwork status and destination market? | Clear RFQ details make supplier comparison more useful. |
| Sampling purpose | Is the sample for structure, material, finish, insert, artwork or packing review? | A sample without a purpose can waste time. |
| Surface and finish | Does the project need matte, glossy, foil, embossing, spot UV, window, label or textured finish? | Finish direction affects structure and sampling discussion. |
| Destination review | Are there document or market requirements the buyer needs reviewed project by project? | Public pages should not be treated as compliance guarantees. |
| Communication quality | Does the supplier ask project-specific questions instead of giving vague promises? | Good supplier screening starts with concrete questions. |
