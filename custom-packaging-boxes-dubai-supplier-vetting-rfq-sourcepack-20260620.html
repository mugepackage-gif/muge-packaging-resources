RFQ Checklist

  • Product type, size and packed weight
  • Box style direction, such as folding carton, rigid gift box, mailer box, sleeve, drawer box or paper tube
  • Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
  • Artwork status, logo files, color reference and barcode or label area
  • Insert, divider, tray or product protection requirement
  • Surface finish reference, such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV or window
  • Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
  • Sampling purpose: structure, material, finish, insert, artwork or packing review
