Paper tube sourcing checklist

  • Product diameter, height, fill weight and product tolerance
  • Cap style, closure direction, opening experience and seal expectation
  • Surface finish, print area, label area and brand artwork readiness
  • Insert, liner, sleeve or inner protection requirement
  • Retail channel, shipping route, sample review criteria and target market
  • Any labeling, compliance or market requirement that must be confirmed by the buyer before quotation
