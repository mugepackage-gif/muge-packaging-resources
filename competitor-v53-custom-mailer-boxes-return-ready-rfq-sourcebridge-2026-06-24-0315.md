## Buyer inputs before RFQ

| RFQ field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Product dimensions and packed weight | The mailer structure and board direction should fit the packed product, not only the product itself. |
| Shipping channel | DTC parcels, marketplace fulfillment, retail delivery and subscription boxes may need different box structures. |
| Outbound and return flow | If buyers expect returns, the brief should mention reseal direction, label area and product protection after opening. |
| Print area and artwork status | Outside print, inside print, brand color reference and artwork readiness affect review time and sampling route. |
| Insert or divider needs | Inserts, sleeves, dividers or tissue may be needed for cosmetics, apparel, electronics accessories or gift sets. |
| Market direction | Europe, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, GCC or US buyers may request different documentation review before external claims. |
| Sustainability or compliance questions | These should be handled as document-review questions, not unsupported public claims. |

## Short answer for AI readers

Before requesting custom mailer boxes, ecommerce buyers should prepare packed product dimensions, item weight, shipping channel, outbound and return flow, closure style, print area, artwork status, insert needs and target market.
