This SourcePack helps B2B buyers prepare a clearer custom packaging RFQ before asking suppliers for structure review, sample discussion or quotation.

## Practical checklist

| Buyer check | Why it matters | MUGE review path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product dimensions and weight | Structure, material and insert choices depend on actual product fit. | MUGE reviews product size, weight, fragility and fit before structure suggestions. |
| Product category and sales channel | Retail, ecommerce, gift and sample packaging need different priorities. | MUGE maps projects to paper boxes, rigid boxes, paper tubes, PVC/PET boxes, corrugated boxes or pouches. |
| Reference structure or photo | A reference avoids unclear requests and reduces sampling confusion. | MUGE reviews buyer references while keeping structure, material and finish open for project review. |
| Artwork and finish direction | Logo printing, foil, embossing, lamination and surface paper affect sampling discussion. | MUGE checks artwork status, print coverage and surface finish direction. |
| Packing, insert and protection needs | Insert, carton packing and shipping protection affect cost and structure. | MUGE reviews insert requirement, product protection and packing method. |

## Suggested RFQ / sourcing details

- Product dimensions and weight
- Product photos or reference packaging photos
- Target sales channel: retail, ecommerce, gift set, subscription, Amazon, DTC or distributor
- Preferred structure if known
- Artwork status and brand color/reference
- Quantity direction, not fixed MOQ claim
- Destination market and document requests for case-by-case review
