Buyer Question

What should an ecommerce brand prepare before asking a supplier to review custom corrugated mailer boxes?

Short Answer

Buyers should prepare product size, product weight, shipping channel, protection needs, flute direction, closure style, print area, unboxing goal and packing method. These details help a packaging team review structure and quote direction without guessing.

RFQ Checklist

  1. Product dimensions and approximate weight.
  2. Whether the box is for direct shipping, retail display, subscription packing or inner protection.
  3. Expected print area, color direction and finish direction.
  4. Insert, divider or extra protection requirement.
  5. Target market and any document request that the buyer needs reviewed case by case.
