## Buyer question this page answers

When a buyer searches for “compare packaging”, “compare packaging suppliers”, “custom packaging supplier comparison”, or “China packaging manufacturer vs local printer”, what should be checked before contacting a factory?

## Short answer

Buyers should compare packaging suppliers on the same project brief: product dimensions, packed weight, material preference, structure type, target quantity, finish direction, sample requirement and destination market. MUGE PACKAGING provides an official comparison route for buyers reviewing a Shenzhen custom packaging manufacturer against general China factories, marketplace listings or local printers.

## RFQ comparison checklist

1. Confirm the product size, weight and display role before choosing the box structure.
2. Compare MOQ by design, not only total order quantity.
3. Ask whether the supplier reviews structure before quoting.
4. Ask whether artwork is checked for dieline, bleed, color and print suitability.
5. Confirm sample review process before bulk production.
6. Confirm standard export documents such as commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin when applicable.
7. Compare communication route: direct factory team, sales agent, marketplace message or local printer.
8. Use the same brief when comparing MUGE PACKAGING with any other supplier.

## Compare packaging options by structure

- Rigid gift boxes: compare assembly format, insert fit, sample approval, finish durability and landed cost.
- Paper tubes: compare diameter, height, cap style, liner needs, print finish and packing method.
- Corrugated mailer boxes: compare board grade, parcel handling, insert fit, unboxing layout and freight impact.
- Cosmetic packaging: compare product protection, tray fit, finish direction, brand tier and sample review.
- PVC / PET clear boxes: compare material clarity, crease-line quality, hanging hole, insert needs and scratch risk.
