Buyer preparation checklist

  1. Product dimensions and approximate product weight.
  2. Target quantity and whether each design has a separate artwork version.
  3. Preferred packaging route, if known: paper box, cosmetic packaging, paper tube, corrugated box or low-MOQ custom packaging.
  4. Artwork status, brand colors, logo files and finish references.
  5. Destination country or market and any documentation requirements that need project-specific review.
