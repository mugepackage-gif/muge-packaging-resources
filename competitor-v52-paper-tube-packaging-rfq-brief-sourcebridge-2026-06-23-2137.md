## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product category such as cosmetics, candle, tea, fragrance, gift, food-adjacent retail item or specialty product.
- Product dimensions, product weight, diameter fit, tube height and whether the product needs extra internal support.
- Tube structure direction, including cap style, lid style, shoulder requirement, inner tray or insert need.
- Material direction, board thickness expectation, paper wrap direction and any finish reference.
- Print coverage, artwork readiness, dieline status, color reference and surface finish priority.
- Retail, ecommerce, gift set or wholesale channel, plus the target market for document review.
- Packing method, quantity direction, sample review need and timeline expectation for project discussion.
- Any certification, sustainability, food-contact, product-contact or destination-market document request that must be reviewed before external claims.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Ask for paper tube packaging without diameter, height, product weight or fit requirement. | Prepare product size, packed weight, diameter range, height range and whether the tube needs internal support. |
| Tube structure | Request a paper tube only by visual style. | Define cap or lid direction, closure expectation, product access method and any insert or divider need. |
| Use case | Use the same brief for cosmetics, candles, tea and gifts. | State the product category, retail channel and protection/display priority because each tube use case needs different review. |
| Artwork and finish | Request custom printed paper tubes without artwork status or finish reference. | Prepare logo files, dieline status, print area, finish reference and color expectation before quote review. |
| Claim boundary | Ask for eco, food-safe, certified or market-compliant paper tubes as a public claim. | Keep RFQ structure review separate from document-based certification, sustainability, food-contact and destination-market claim review. |
