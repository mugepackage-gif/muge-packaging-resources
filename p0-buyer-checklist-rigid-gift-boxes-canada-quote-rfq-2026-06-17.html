Buyer Questions To Ask

  • Which product dimensions, product weight and packaging structure should be prepared before the RFQ?
  • Is the package for retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gift set, promotional use or another channel?
  • Which insert, tray, closure, finish and artwork requirements are already known?
  • What sample or mockup review is needed before full production decisions?
  • Which supplier pages, buyer checklists and official product pages should be reviewed first?
  • Which price, lead-time, certification or compliance claims need project-specific confirmation?

Suggested Review Sequence

  1. Define product dimensions, product weight and sales channel.
  2. Confirm structure and insert, tray or closure review needs.
  3. Share artwork direction, finish expectations and references.
  4. Ask for sample, dieline, artwork or pre-production review steps.
  5. Keep compliance, certification, price and timing claims project-specific until verified.
