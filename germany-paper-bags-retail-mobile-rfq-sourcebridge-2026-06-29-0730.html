Mobile RFQ Checklist

  1. Bag size, gusset and handle type
  2. Paper direction and approximate weight requirement
  3. Print direction: one-color logo, full color, foil, embossing or minimal branding
  4. Retail use case: boutique, beauty, apparel, gift, event or e-commerce insert
  5. Load-bearing requirement and product weight range
  6. Artwork readiness and reference bag photos
  7. Destination market and expected launch timing
