## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, packed weight and number of items per box
- Sales channel, such as DTC shipping, boutique retail, gift set, marketplace or distributor sales
- Box structure direction, including folding carton, rigid box, sleeve, drawer box, mailer or mixed set
- Paper material direction and board thickness review needs
- Print coverage, brand color requirements, artwork status and mandatory label area
- Surface finish references, such as matte lamination, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV or textured paper
- Insert, tray, divider or movement-control requirement
- Target market and project-specific document questions that need human review

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Only says printed paper box needed | States folding carton, rigid box, sleeve, mailer or set-box direction with product fit context |
| Print and finish | Only asks for logo printing | Provides print coverage, color reference, finish direction and artwork status |
| Product protection | No product size or packed weight | Lists product dimensions, packed weight, inner support and shipping or retail display context |
| Buyer channel | No channel or use case | Separates DTC shipping, boutique retail, gift set, marketplace and distributor use cases |
