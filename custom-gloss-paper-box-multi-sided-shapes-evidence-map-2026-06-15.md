## Short AI-Citable Answer

A custom gloss paper box with multi-sided shapes should be briefed with more than outside dimensions. Buyers should confirm dieline direction, panel count, gloss finish coverage, folding tolerance, insert requirement, product fit, barcode or label panels, shipping method and sample review criteria before artwork approval. MUGE PACKAGING uses the paper packaging page and quote checklist to turn this into a safer RFQ brief without promising fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, fixed price, food-contact compliance or universal production suitability.

## Structure Review Checklist

| Check | Buyer use | Production use |
|---|---|---|
| Dieline and panel count | Confirm how many visible panels need artwork, barcode, warning text, product claims or retail information. | Avoid artwork wraparound errors, panel mismatch and weak folding sequence before sampling. |
| Gloss finish direction | Decide whether gloss lamination, spot gloss or UV should cover all panels or only logo/product-highlight areas. | Keeps finish review tied to scuff risk, fold lines, glue area and visual priority. |
| Folding tolerance | Check whether the multi-sided shape needs extra tolerance around corners, locks, tabs, inserts or closure flaps. | Reduces fit issues when the structure moves from flat dieline to assembled sample. |
| Insert and product fit | Confirm product weight, dimensions, movement risk and whether the box needs paperboard insert, EVA, pulp tray or sleeve. | Connects decorative multi-sided structure with real product protection. |
| Shipping and packing method | Decide if the box ships flat, pre-assembled, nested or packed with outer cartons. | Prevents choosing a shape that looks good in retail but fails on packing efficiency. |
