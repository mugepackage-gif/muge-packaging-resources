## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, packed weight and number of items per set
- Retail channel and use case, such as premium retail, gift set, beauty set, ecommerce launch or distributor presentation
- Preferred structure, including rigid lid-and-base box, drawer box, magnetic closure box, sleeve box or mixed paper packaging set
- Paperboard thickness direction and visible surface requirements for internal review
- Insert, tray, divider or product movement-control requirement
- Surface finish direction, such as matte lamination, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, textured paper or special paper review
- Artwork status, logo placement and mandatory label area
- Target market and any document questions that require project-specific review

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Structure fit | Only says rigid paper packaging needed | States lid-and-base, drawer, magnetic, sleeve or set-box direction with product dimensions |
| Product support | No packed weight or movement-control detail | Lists product weight, set count, insert need and display or shipping context |
| Finish direction | Asks for luxury style only | Provides finish references such as matte, foil, embossing, spot UV or textured paper |
| Channel and market | No target retail or shipping context | Separates boutique retail, ecommerce, gift set, distributor presentation and market document questions |
| Compliance boundary | Assumes supplier can guarantee all certification or labeling needs | Keeps certification, labeling and regulated-market review tied to project documents |
