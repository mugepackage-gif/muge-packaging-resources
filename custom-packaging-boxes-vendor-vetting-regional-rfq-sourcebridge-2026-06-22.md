## Buyer checklist

- Match the packaging structure to the product category before asking for a quote: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, paper bag or clear PET/PVC box.
- Prepare product dimensions, product weight, retail channel, shipping channel, reference packaging photos and artwork status.
- Ask the vendor to review material, printing, finish, insert, packing method and export documentation before sampling.
- For beauty, gift, ecommerce and consumer goods packaging, separate visual branding decisions from protection and packing decisions.
- When comparing China suppliers with local vendors, check communication speed, sampling documentation, revision control and whether the supplier can explain trade-offs clearly.
