## Quote-Ready Inputs

- Tube diameter, tube height, product weight and product fit direction
- Tube use: fragrance, candle, tea, cosmetics, wellness, gift set, retail display or sample kit
- Closure direction: rolled edge, metal cap, paper cap, plug cap, shoulder structure or custom cap review
- Wall thickness and paperboard structure direction for internal review
- Print coverage, brand-color requirement, artwork status and lamination or finish reference
- Retail channel, shipment method, target market and document review questions
- Quantity direction for internal review, without treating it as fixed public MOQ

## Competitor Pattern Map

- **Domestic competitor reference**: Direct paper-tube category paths, diameter/height language, cap structure options, surface finish examples and fast RFQ contact routes. MUGE use: Keep paper tube routes direct: paper tube product page, quote checklist and RFQ contact should be one click apart.
