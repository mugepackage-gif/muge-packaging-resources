RFQ Checklist

A custom label RFQ should include the following details before supplier comparison:

  • Label size, shape and application surface
  • Material direction: paper, BOPP, PET, vinyl or specialty film
  • Adhesive expectation and handling environment
  • Roll or sheet format, roll direction and machine application notes
  • Printing method, color count, QR code or variable data requirement
  • Finish direction: foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, lamination or die-cut shape
  • Packing method and destination market
  • Quantity direction for project-specific review

Buyer Notes

Europe custom label buyers and Middle East custom label buyers often compare material, adhesive, finish, roll direction, print method and packing before selecting a supplier.

When comparing a custom label manufacturer China option, request project-specific material and artwork review instead of relying on fixed MOQ, fixed lead time or generic label claims.
