## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Product category, product dimensions, packed weight and whether the project is a launch, relaunch or seasonal refresh.
- Target sales channel: e-commerce, retail shelf, gift set, event launch, influencer kit, subscription or wholesale route.
- Preferred structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, sleeve, drawer box, insert or protective inner tray.
- Sample purpose: visual review, structure review, unboxing review, photography review or buyer presentation.
- Artwork status, brand color direction, finish direction, barcode or label area and required print file stage.
- Any sustainability, recycling, destination-market labeling or material document questions that must be reviewed separately.
- Decision deadline and internal approval sequence, without treating the project as a guaranteed lead-time claim.
- Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
