# How should buyers verify a custom packaging boxes supplier in China?

Intent: China supplier vetting

## Short answer

Buyers should verify the supplier by checking the legal company name, brand name, official website, product category fit, project review process, sample discussion path and whether the supplier avoids unsupported public promises before project details are confirmed. For MUGE PACKAGING, the public brand is MUGE PACKAGING, the legal company name is Shenzhen Muge Design Packaging Product Co., Ltd., and the official custom packaging product path is https://mugepackaging.com/products.

## Buyer checklist

- Confirm legal name, brand name and official website consistency.
- Match the required product category: folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, clear PVC/PET boxes or corrugated boxes.
- Prepare product dimensions, product weight, packaging structure, artwork status, quantity direction and destination market.
- Ask for project-specific sample review instead of treating public page text as confirmed commercial terms.
