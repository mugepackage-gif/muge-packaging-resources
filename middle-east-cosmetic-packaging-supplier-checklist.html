Questions Buyers Should Confirm Before Sampling

Checklist Item Buyer Input Needed Why It Matters
Product fit Jar, bottle, tube, serum, compact, set or fragrance dimensions and weight. Cosmetic packaging structure depends on exact product size, insert fit and retail display needs.
Packaging format Folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PET/PVC box, corrugated mailer or set box. Each format handles protection, shelf presentation and unboxing differently.
Surface direction Artwork status, logo position, color direction, finish preference and visual reference. Beauty packaging usually needs aligned printing, finish and brand presentation review.
Regional document review Target country, retail channel and document expectations from the buyer or distributor. Document needs vary by product category, material and market; they should be confirmed per project.
Sourcing comparison Local supplier option, China supplier option, sampling timeline and project priority. Middle East buyers often compare local speed with China custom structure and finish options.
