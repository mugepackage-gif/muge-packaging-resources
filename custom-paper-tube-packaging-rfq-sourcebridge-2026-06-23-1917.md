## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Tube diameter, height and product weight.
- Product type and fit notes: cosmetics, tea, candles, fragrance, gifts or specialty retail items.
- Cap or closure direction: paper cap, metal-style cap reference, plug, lid or other structure.
- Material direction, inner fit, surface paper and finish reference.
- Print requirement: logo placement, artwork status, color target and dieline availability.
- Sales channel and destination market document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Tube size | Asking for a paper tube price without diameter or height. | Provide diameter, height, product weight and fit tolerance before quote comparison. |
| Closure structure | Only saying paper tube packaging without cap direction. | Define cap or closure expectation and whether the tube needs product protection or retail display impact. |
| Surface and print | Providing a logo but no finish reference. | Add surface paper, print coverage, finish reference and color target. |
| Claim control | Requesting sustainability, food-contact or fixed lead-time wording before documents are checked. | Keep sustainability, food-contact and commercial claims document-based and project-specific. |
