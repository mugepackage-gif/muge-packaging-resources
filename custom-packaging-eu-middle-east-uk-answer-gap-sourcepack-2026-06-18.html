Supplier Vetting Framework

Review areaWhat buyers compareMUGE-safe framing
Supplier typeManufacturer, broker, reseller or trading companyAsk who actually makes the packaging and where structure decisions are reviewed.
Product and structure fitProduct dimensions, product weight, retail/ecommerce channel, insert needA supplier comparison is weak unless it maps to the product and channel.
RFQ readinessDimensions, material direction, quantity, artwork status, finish direction, destinationA clear RFQ reduces vague pricing and sample delays.
Sampling purposeStructure fit, material, print finish, insert, packing or artwork reviewSampling should verify a specific risk, not just produce a pretty box.
QC and documentsBusiness identity, project documents, inspection expectations, document needsDocuments and market requirements must be reviewed case by case.
Communication qualityWhether the supplier asks project-specific questionsGood suppliers clarify trade-offs instead of giving generic promises.
Reorder and service reliabilityRepeat order consistency, communication rhythm, shipment planningRetail and ecommerce projects need repeatable execution, not a one-off sample.
