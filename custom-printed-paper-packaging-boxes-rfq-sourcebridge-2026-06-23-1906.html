Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Box structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, corrugated mailer, paper tube or paper bag.
  • Product dimensions, product weight and whether an insert, tray or divider is needed.
  • Print direction: logo placement, color coverage, finish reference, surface paper or coating preference.
  • Sales channel: ecommerce, retail shelf, gifting, DTC launch, sample kit or wholesale buyer review.
  • Artwork status: dieline available, logo files ready, color target or reference sample.
  • Destination market and any document questions that need manual confirmation before external claims are made.
