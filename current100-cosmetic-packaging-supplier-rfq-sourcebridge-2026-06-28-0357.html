Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, filled weight and number of SKUs
  • Product type, such as cream jar, serum bottle, skincare set, makeup item or beauty kit
  • Packaging structure direction, including folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, insert tray or clear box
  • Retail, e-commerce, influencer kit, travel set or gift set usage
  • Surface finish references, brand color direction and logo placement
  • Artwork status, barcode or label area and version count
  • Sample purpose: structure check, brand approval, photo shoot, retail test or pre-production review
  • Destination market and document questions that need project-specific confirmation
