How should champagne and spirits brands prepare a gift box packaging RFQ for bottle and flute sets?

Keyword route: champagne flute gift box packaging

This SourceBridge supports buyers researching gift box packaging for champagne, wine, spirits, bottle-and-glass gift sets, duty-free gift sets, and premium retail presentation.

Short Answer

A buyer should define the bottle size, glass or accessory layout, rigid box structure, insert type, finish direction, sales channel, and review requirements before asking for a champagne flute gift box packaging quotation.

For bottle plus flute sets, the practical RFQ question is not only the outer box style. Buyers also need to confirm cavity layout, glass protection, insert material direction, artwork status, retail display expectation, and whether the pack is mainly for gifting, ecommerce, duty-free shelves, or event presentation.

RFQ Checklist

  • Bottle dimensions, glass or flute dimensions, accessory count, and total packed weight.
  • Required structure: magnetic rigid box, drawer box, book-style box, sleeve set, or display gift set.
  • Insert requirement: EVA, paperboard insert, molded pulp direction, satin liner direction, or mixed accessory bay.
  • Finish direction: foil stamping, debossing, textured wrap, soft-touch surface, printed story panel, or other decorative direction.
  • Sales channel: duty-free, retail shelf, ecommerce, corporate gift, launch kit, event gift, or distributor sample set.
  • Artwork status, reference photos, destination country, and whether document review is needed before production discussion.
