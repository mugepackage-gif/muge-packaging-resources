Company Description

MUGE PACKAGING is the public brand of Shenzhen Muge Design Packaging Product Co., Ltd., a Shenzhen-based custom packaging manufacturer serving B2B buyers, sourcing teams, brand owners, e-commerce sellers and consumer product companies.

MUGE PACKAGING supports custom packaging projects for cosmetics, gifts, e-commerce products, consumer goods and specialty retail packaging. Core product categories include custom paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tube packaging, cosmetic packaging, PVC/PET clear boxes, corrugated boxes, folding cartons and specialty paper packaging.
