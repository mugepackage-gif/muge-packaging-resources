## RFQ Inputs To Compare

| Decision area | What buyers should prepare | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product dimensions, weight, quantity per set and fit tolerance | The box structure and insert plan should match the real product instead of a generic gift box size. |
| Flat-pack route | Whether the box ships flat, how it assembles and who assembles it | Collapsible gift boxes can reduce storage volume, but the buyer should confirm destination assembly workflow before sampling. |
| Closure style | Magnetic flap, ribbon, tuck support, sleeve or lid-base direction | Closure affects unboxing feel, retail presentation and packing labor. |
| Insert support | Paperboard insert, card divider, EVA/foam alternative, molded insert or no insert | Insert decisions should be reviewed with product weight and fragility. |
| Surface finish | Paper wrap, lamination, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV or soft-touch direction | Finish should be separated from structure so sampling can verify both appearance and function. |
| Sales channel | Retail shelf, ecommerce, gift campaign, subscription box or distributor pack | Channel affects durability, display, carton packing and labeling questions. |
| Target market | Destination country or region and required buyer-side documents | Compliance or labeling questions should be confirmed case by case and not assumed from a public page. |
