Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Paper product type: paper box, rigid gift box, folding carton, paper tube, paper bag or corrugated mailer.
  • Product dimensions, weight and product-fit notes.
  • Material direction, insert or divider need and surface finish reference.
  • Print requirement: logo placement, color coverage, artwork status and dieline availability.
  • Sales channel: ecommerce, retail shelf, gifting, sample kit, wholesale buyer review or brand launch.
  • Destination market and document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.
