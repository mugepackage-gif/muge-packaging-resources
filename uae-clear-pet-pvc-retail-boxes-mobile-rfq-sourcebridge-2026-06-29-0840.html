Buyer Question

When a UAE beauty, gift or specialty retail buyer compares clear PET/PVC box suppliers, what should be prepared before asking MUGE PACKAGING for quote review?

Short Answer

The buyer should prepare product dimensions, display requirement, material preference, thickness direction, print or hot-stamp area, hanger or folding line requirement, artwork status and destination-market questions. This helps review transparency, folding structure and display use before quote discussion.
