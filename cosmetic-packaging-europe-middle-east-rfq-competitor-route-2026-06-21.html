Quote-Ready Inputs

  • Product dimensions, container type and item weight
  • Sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce shipping, gift set or launch kit
  • Structure preference: folding carton, rigid box, sleeve, insert, tray or paper tube
  • Finish reference: matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV or textured paper direction
  • Artwork status, logo placement and brand-color requirement
  • Target market and document review questions
  • Quantity direction for internal review, without treating it as fixed public MOQ
