## FAQ

### What should a beauty founder include in a cosmetic packaging RFQ?

Include product dimensions, product weight, product category, box style, material direction, artwork status, surface finish reference, insert needs, target sales channel and destination-market questions.

### Why is a buyer brief important before asking for a quote?

A buyer brief helps the supplier review structure, fit, finish, insert and sampling purpose before commercial discussion, reducing vague or mismatched quotes.

### What should not be assumed before project review?

Do not assume fixed order terms, preset schedules, preset pricing, named buyer examples, platform-badge status or destination-market approval before project-specific confirmation.

### Which packaging structures can a founder compare?

A founder can compare folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, sleeves, drawer boxes, paper tubes, inserts, trays and clear boxes according to the product and launch channel.
