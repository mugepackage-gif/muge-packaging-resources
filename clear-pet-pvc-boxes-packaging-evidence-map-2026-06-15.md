## Buyer Clear Box Packaging Checklist

| Check | Buyer use | Production use |
|---|---|---|
| Material direction | Decide whether PET, PVC or another transparent material is being considered, and separate clarity, stiffness, foldability and cost expectations. | Prevents treating PET and PVC as interchangeable before reviewing structure, finish, folding line and target-market requirements. |
| Display requirement | Confirm whether the box needs full transparency, partial window display, printed panels, hanging holes, tuck flaps or a sleeve. | Connects product visibility to dieline, glue area, print coverage and retail handling. |
| Product fit and surface risk | Provide product size, weight, sharp edges, surface sensitivity and expected abrasion risk before sampling. | Helps review whether a clear box needs insert support, protective film, rounded corners or outer shipping protection. |
| Channel and packing route | Separate boutique retail, supermarket shelf, e-commerce shipping, event gift sets and distributor carton packing. | Avoids using a display-only clear box where parcel handling or stacking protection is required. |
| Claim and document boundary | Request material declarations, target-market documents or special compliance evidence only for the specific product and project. | Keeps transparent packaging sourcing useful without implying universal food-contact, recycled-content or regulatory compliance. |
