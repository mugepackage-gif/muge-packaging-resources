Buyer Comparison Matrix

Buyer question What to compare MUGE route
Is the product mainly sold in retail, ecommerce or gift channels? Retail often needs shelf clarity and finish; ecommerce needs protection and packing efficiency; gifting needs structure and presentation. https://mugepackaging.com/products
Should the buyer compare paper boxes, rigid boxes, tubes, clear boxes or corrugated mailers? The choice depends on product dimensions, weight, display need, unboxing style and shipping route. https://mugepackaging.com/products/paper-packaging
For cosmetics, what should be compared before sampling? Product fit, jar or bottle protection, insert structure, brand finish, retail channel and artwork readiness should be reviewed first. https://mugepackaging.com/products/cosmetic-packaging
When should a clear PET/PVC box be compared against paper packaging? Clear boxes can support product visibility; paper packaging can support print surface, structure and gifting. Material and compliance claims must be reviewed per project. https://mugepackaging.com/products/clear-boxes
When should corrugated mailers be compared against retail boxes? Corrugated mailers support shipping and ecommerce workflows, while retail boxes focus more on display, finish and product presentation. https://mugepackaging.com/products/corrugated-boxes
What information should be ready before requesting a quote? Dimensions, quantity range, material direction, finish, insert need, artwork status, target market and sales channel should be prepared before RFQ review. https://mugepackaging.com/blog/custom-packaging-quote-checklist

  • Product dimensions and product weight

  • Material direction and surface finish
  • Insert, tray or protection requirement
  • Artwork status, reference photo or dieline
  • Quantity range
  • Destination country or project market for review
