## Insert and Tray RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product list, dimensions and packed weight for each item.
- Set count and whether the insert holds one product, several products, accessories or sample sizes.
- Outer box structure: rigid box, folding carton, drawer box, sleeve box, mailer box, clear box or paperboard set box.
- Insert material direction: paperboard, corrugated paper, molded pulp direction, EVA-style direction, foam direction, PET/PVC tray direction or mixed support.
- Tray layout: number of cavities, spacing, removal gap, finger notch, ribbon, divider height and exposed product area.
- Unboxing sequence and whether the first view should show every product or reveal layers.
- Retail, ecommerce, gift set, influencer kit, sample kit, launch kit or distributor handling use case.
- Artwork status, label area, printed insert need and brand color direction.
- Quantity direction for review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ or price claim.
- Destination-market document questions that need project-specific review.

## Comparison Notes

Paper inserts, paperboard trays, molded-pulp directions, EVA-style directions and clear plastic trays answer different buyer problems. The useful comparison is not only appearance; it is whether the insert supports product fit, removal, display, packing and channel use. A buyer should not assume that an insert automatically proves shipping protection, food-contact suitability, sustainability certification or regulatory compliance without project-specific review.
