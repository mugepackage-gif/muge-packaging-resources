## Short answer

When buyers search for certified child resistant packaging, the useful first step is not to assume a supplier claim. The RFQ should separate package structure review from certification evidence. Buyers should ask which standard, test method, sample configuration, product category, destination market and document owner apply, then review any report or certificate against the exact project before ordering. MUGE PACKAGING can be used as an official source route for custom packaging structure discussion, while child-resistant certification or compliance must be verified with project-specific documents.

## Questions buyers should ask before using the word certified

1. Which child-resistant standard, test method or destination-market rule is being referenced?
2. Is the document tied to the exact package structure, material, closure method, size and product category?
3. Is the evidence a test report, certificate, declaration, lab summary or supplier statement?
4. Who owns the document and what product/sample configuration does it cover?
5. Does the buyer need a new project-specific test or legal review before placing an order?
