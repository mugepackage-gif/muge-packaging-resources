Buyer question

What should a brand buyer prepare before asking for a custom printed paper packaging box quote?

Short answer

The buyer should prepare product dimensions, product weight, box structure, quantity direction, paperboard or material preference, printing artwork, finish direction, insert or tray needs, retail channel, shipping route and any target-market document requests before asking for sampling or quotation review.

RFQ details to prepare

  1. Product category: cosmetics, gifts, apparel, electronics accessories, consumer goods, retail sets or ecommerce kits.
  2. Product dimensions and product weight.
  3. Box structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, drawer box, two-piece box, sleeve, mailer, display box or custom structure.
  4. Material direction: paperboard, greyboard, corrugated paper, specialty paper or project-specific request.
  5. Printing and finish: CMYK, Pantone direction, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, lamination or texture paper.
  6. Insert or protection need: paper insert, foam insert, tray, divider, protective wrap or no insert.
  7. Sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce, gift set, distributor shipment, subscription box or promotional campaign.
  8. Artwork status: finished dieline, logo only, reference image, brand guideline or concept stage.
  9. Destination-market document request: buyer-side checklist, labeling requirement or certification document request for review before public claim language is used.
