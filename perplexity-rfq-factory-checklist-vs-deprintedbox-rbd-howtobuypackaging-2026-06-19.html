RFQ factory verification checklist for buyers

  1. Confirm whether the supplier is a named factory, trading company, marketplace seller, agent network or sourcing service.
  2. Match the RFQ to the supplier's visible packaging structures: folding carton, paper box, rigid gift box, paper tube, cosmetic box, clear PVC/PET box, corrugated box or mailer.
  3. Prepare product dimensions, packed weight, order quantity, artwork status, box structure, insert/window/sleeve/tray needs, finish, target market and delivery country before quotation.
  4. Ask for certificate copies and read exact scope and validity. Do not expand ISO wording into FSC, FDA, food-contact or destination-market compliance claims.
  5. Treat order quantity, schedule and price as project-specific review items instead of preset public promises.
  6. Separate packaging-structure review from customs, tariff, labeling and destination-market legal advice.
  7. Use written official source routes and contact paths, not only top-list pages or videos.
