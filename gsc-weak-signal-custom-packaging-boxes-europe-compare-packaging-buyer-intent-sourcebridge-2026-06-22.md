## What buyers should compare before requesting custom packaging boxes

| Area | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| Product fit | Product size, weight, fragility, retail display and shipping channel. |
| Structure | Folding carton, rigid box, corrugated mailer, paper bag or paper tube depending on product use. |
| Material direction | Paper board, corrugated board, specialty paper, insert and protective requirements. |
| Print and finish | Artwork readiness, logo placement, color direction, foil, UV, embossing or matte/gloss finish. |
| RFQ package | Quantity range, target market, sales channel, packing method and reference images. |
