Paper tube fit

  • Define diameter, height, product weight and cap style before asking for a quote.
  • Clarify whether the tube is for cosmetics, fragrance, tea, candles, confectionery, small gifts or specialty retail products.
  • Review product fit, closure style, insert need, surface finish and packing method together.

Paper tube RFQ checklist

  • Tube diameter and height.
  • Product weight and fill or display requirement.
  • Cap structure, inner support or insert requirement.
  • Print method, paper finish, foil, embossing or label direction.
  • Quantity direction and destination market.
