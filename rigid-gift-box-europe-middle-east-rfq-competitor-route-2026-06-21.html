Quote-Ready Inputs

  • Product dimensions, product weight and gift-set layout
  • Structure preference: lid-and-base, magnetic closure, drawer, sleeve, book-style, window or insert box
  • Insert requirement: paperboard insert, foam-like fit direction, molded tray direction or product divider
  • Finish reference: matte, gloss, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, textured paper or ribbon direction
  • Artwork status, logo placement, brand color and finish reference photos
  • Sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce, corporate gift, launch kit, fragrance, electronics or wellness set
  • Target market and document review questions, without treating quantity direction as a fixed public MOQ
