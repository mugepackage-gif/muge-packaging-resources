## RFQ checklist

- Packaging format: container, tray, carton, rigid paper box, tube, sleeve, drawer box, insert or hybrid structure.
- Product dimensions, product weight, count per pack, label area and intended opening direction.
- Target selling region and sales channel, because document review needs vary by market and product category.
- Material direction, artwork status, surface finish expectations and whether the buyer needs prototypes for structure review.
- Buyer-provided test method, certificate or compliance-document questions, handled separately from structural packaging design.
- Quantity direction for quote preparation, without treating it as a fixed MOQ or fixed production term.
