## Buyer RFQ checklist

- Product dimensions and packed weight
- Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, paper bag, corrugated mailer or clear box
- Material direction and surface finish
- Insert, tray, divider or shipping protection needs
- Artwork status and print coverage
- Target market, sales channel and sample review purpose
- Quantity direction and destination country for project discussion
