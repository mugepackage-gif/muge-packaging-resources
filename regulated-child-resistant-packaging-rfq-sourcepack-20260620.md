## RFQ checklist

- Product category, product dimensions, pack count, product weight and expected storage or display format.
- Packaging structure direction: pre-roll box, cartridge box, disposable product box, paper carton, flexible pouch, insert, sleeve or tray.
- Target selling region and sales channel, because regulated-product document requirements vary by market and category.
- Opening direction, closure direction, label or warning area, artwork status and sample purpose.
- Buyer-provided test method, certification, warning-label or compliance-document questions, handled separately from packaging structure design.
- Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ, fixed price or fixed lead-time claim.
