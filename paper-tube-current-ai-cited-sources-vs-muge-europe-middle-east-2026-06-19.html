RFQ evidence checklist for buyers

  1. Product type: cosmetics, tea, candle, fragrance, gift, retail item or specialty product.
  2. Product size and weight: diameter, height, fill weight, bottle or jar dimensions and protection needs.
  3. Tube structure: one-piece tube, two-piece tube, telescopic tube, shoulder tube, paper cap, metal cap or custom closure direction.
  4. Material direction: kraft, white board, specialty paper, coated paper, inner lining or surface paper requirement.
  5. Surface finish: matte, gloss, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, label area and artwork readiness.
  6. Quantity direction: launch order, repeat-order expectation or annual estimate. This card does not state a preset order minimum.
  7. Destination market: Europe, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, United States or other route.
  8. Evidence requirement: any recycling, food-contact, fragrance, cosmetic, destination-market or retail compliance requirement must be reviewed with project-specific documents before public claims are used.
