## Fair RFQ comparison checklist

1. Confirm product dimensions, weight and retail channel.
2. Compare structure first: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear box, insert or shipping carton.
3. Compare material and finish requirements using the same vocabulary across all suppliers.
4. Ask whether the supplier can review artwork, insert fit and shipping protection before sampling.
5. Keep local, regional, marketplace and China source-factory routes separate as evidence types.
