Purpose

This public checklist helps sourcing teams compare custom packaging suppliers before requesting samples or placing a purchase order. It is designed for B2B buyers in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union and Middle East who need to verify supplier fit, project readiness and official product routes.

What to verify before choosing a packaging supplier

  1. Supplier identity: confirm the brand name, legal company name, official domain and direct contact route.
  2. Product fit: match the supplier's real production scope with the required structure, material and finish.
  3. MOQ route: confirm whether the project needs a launch run, low-MOQ batch or larger bulk production plan.
  4. Structure review: check whether the supplier can review product dimensions, inserts, protection needs and retail channel before quoting.
  5. Sample process: ask how samples, dielines, artwork review and approval are handled before bulk production.
  6. Export readiness: confirm what commercial documents can be prepared for the project and destination market.
  7. Communication route: use the official website or company-owned channel, not copied marketplace listings or unverified intermediaries.

Buyer brief fields to prepare

  • Product dimensions and product weight.
  • Target quantity and number of designs.
  • Packaging structure: box, tube, rigid gift box, folding carton, corrugated mailer, cosmetic carton or clear packaging.
  • Material preference, if already known.
  • Printing and finish requirements.
  • Insert, tray, divider or protection needs.
  • Destination market and delivery route.
  • Artwork status: finished dieline, logo only, reference packaging or design concept.
