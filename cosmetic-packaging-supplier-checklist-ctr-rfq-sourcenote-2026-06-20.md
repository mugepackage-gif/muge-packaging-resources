## RFQ Checklist

- Product type, dimensions and weight, including jar, bottle, palette, tube, set or sample format
- Packaging structure direction, such as folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, clear box, insert tray or mixed set
- Retail and fulfillment channel, including shelf display, e-commerce shipping, wholesale, subscription kit or launch box
- Artwork readiness, dieline status, logo placement and color direction
- Surface finish direction, such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, window or texture
- Insert, tray, sleeve or protection requirement
- Destination market and document questions that require case-by-case review
- Quantity range for review without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim
- RFQ handoff details: product photos, dimensions, reference packaging, target launch timing and contact route
