Buyer brief checklist

  1. Product category: skincare, makeup, fragrance, beauty tool, wellness product or gift set.
  2. Product dimensions, product weight and container type.
  3. Preferred packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, PVC/PET clear box or corrugated mailer.
  4. Insert or tray requirement, including product count and fit direction.
  5. Target order quantity and number of designs.
  6. Artwork status: logo files, dieline, brand colors, print copy and barcode or labeling area.
  7. Finish direction: matte, gloss, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, soft-touch or textured paper.
  8. Target sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce, subscription box, gift set, distributor or event launch.
  9. Destination market and any document request that needs project-specific review.
  10. Sampling purpose: size fit, material feel, opening experience, insert fit, print finish or packing review.

What the supplier should review before sampling

  • Whether the package structure fits the product container and sales channel.
  • Whether the insert or tray holds the product safely and presents it clearly.
  • Whether the material and surface finish match the brand position.
  • Whether print coverage, dieline and finish effects are practical before sampling.
  • Whether destination-market labeling or document requests need separate confirmation.
  • Whether the first sample should focus on structure, material feel, artwork proofing or final finish.
