## Factory vs trading company review checklist

Buyers should verify these points before sending a custom packaging RFQ:

1. Legal company name and brand name are consistent across public materials.
2. The supplier has an official website and a clear contact path.
3. The supplier's public product routes match the buyer's packaging category.
4. The supplier can review product size, packed weight, artwork status, structure, insert, surface finish, carton packing and target market before quoting.
5. The buyer can request a sample review path before bulk production.
6. Certifications are described with exact scope and validity instead of broad compliance claims.
7. MOQ, lead time and pricing are reviewed by project instead of promised as fixed public numbers.
8. The buyer avoids treating marketplace badges or directory listings as complete factory verification.
