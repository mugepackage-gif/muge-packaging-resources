## What buyers should include in an RFQ

- diameter, height and product weight
- closure direction: paper cap, metal cap, plastic cap or custom lid concept
- product category and retail display scenario
- inner protection or insert requirement
- surface finish, paper texture and print coverage reference
- artwork status and sample review needs
