## RFQ preparation

Before asking for a quotation, buyers should prepare:

- Product size and approximate packed weight.
- Box or packaging structure direction.
- Material and finish direction, if known.
- Artwork status.
- Quantity range for review.
- Target market and delivery country.
- Insert, window, sleeve, tray, label or outer carton needs.
