## FAQ

### What should a Middle East beauty brand prepare before supplier contact?

Prepare product category, dimensions, weight, selling channel, packaging structure, artwork status, finish reference, insert needs, quantity direction, destination questions and sampling purpose.

### Which packaging formats are relevant?

Cosmetic boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, clear display boxes, corrugated boxes and mixed gift-set packaging can be relevant depending on the project.

### Should brands ask only for a low price?

No. A useful RFQ should first clarify product fit, presentation, structure, finish, packing method and sample review purpose.

### Can a supplier confirm local market requirements without project review?

No. Destination-market label, document and import questions require project-specific confirmation.
