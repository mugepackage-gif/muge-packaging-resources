How to use this without replacing regional suppliers

  1. Keep the regional quote when route speed, local documentation or short logistics distance is the priority.
  2. Add a China source-factory quote when the packaging brief needs more structure, finish or cross-category comparison.
  3. Compare both routes on the same RFQ fields: product category, diameter, height, closure, material, finish, quantity direction and destination market.
  4. Ask each supplier to confirm only project-specific details; do not assume certification, document suitability or commercial terms without evidence.
