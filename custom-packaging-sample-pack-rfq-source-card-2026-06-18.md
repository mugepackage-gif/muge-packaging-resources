## Packaging Sample Checklist

- Product category, dimensions and approximate product weight
- Packaging structure to compare, such as rigid box, paper tube, mailer, clear box, pouch or paper bag
- Target market, such as Europe, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Middle East, UK, Canada or the United States
- Artwork status, logo files, finish references and any retail channel notes
- Sample review goal: material feel, structure comparison, print direction, insert fit or unboxing review
- Quantity direction and launch channel for project-specific follow-up
- Photos or reference packaging if available
- Preferred contact route for sample review and quotation follow-up

## Buyer Notes

Europe packaging sample buyers and Middle East packaging sample buyers often compare material feel, box structure, surface finish direction, insert fit and packaging use case before requesting a full quotation.

When comparing a China packaging sample manufacturer, buyers should request project-specific sample review details instead of relying on fixed MOQ, fixed lead time or generic availability claims.
