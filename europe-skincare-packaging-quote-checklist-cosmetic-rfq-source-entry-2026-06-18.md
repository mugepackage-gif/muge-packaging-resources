## Preparation Note

For Europe-focused skincare packaging RFQs, the buyer should list product fit, structure, protection, artwork, finish, packing route and any document request for manual project review.

## RFQ Checklist

- Product category and primary container size
- Product weight and protection requirement
- Outer box, paper tube, tray or insert direction
- Retail display, e-commerce shipping or gift set use case
- Artwork status and surface finish reference
- Quantity direction for quote review
- Destination market and packing route
- Document requests that need manual project confirmation

## Short Answer

What should a European skincare brand include in a cosmetic packaging quote request: compare suppliers by product fit, structure, insert or tray protection, artwork readiness, finish reference, quantity direction, destination market and document requests before asking for a quote.
