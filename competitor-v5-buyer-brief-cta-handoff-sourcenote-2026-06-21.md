## Required Buyer Brief Inputs

### Corrugated mailer and shipping box brief

- product size, weight and packing quantity
- mailer, display box, shipping carton or subscription kit direction
- single-wall, double-wall or flute preference if already known
- print coverage, finish direction, artwork status and reference photos
- target market, shipping method and handling or stacking notes

### Broad custom paper packaging structure and material brief

- product dimensions, weight, sales channel and target market
- preferred structure or reference packaging photos
- paper board, kraft, specialty paper, corrugated or greyboard direction
- printing surfaces, finish options, insert needs and artwork status
- quantity direction plus any labeling or document review notes
