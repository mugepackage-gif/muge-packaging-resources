## Short answer

For the query `best custom packaging food safety compliance`, the practical sourcing answer is: do not choose a packaging supplier from a generic compliance phrase. First decide whether the project is direct food-contact packaging, secondary retail packaging, shipping packaging or a gift/display package. Then send the supplier enough product, material, artwork and document-review information to route the quote safely.

## RFQ information buyers should prepare

- Product category and whether packaging directly touches the product.
- Target market or sales region.
- Finished size, product dimensions and product weight.
- Preferred material, board, flute, tube, carton or rigid-box direction.
- Printing, coating, lamination, insert and finishing requirements.
- Artwork status, reference photos or dieline status.
- Quantity direction and project timing for internal quotation planning.
- Document questions that require review before any compliance wording is discussed.
