## Buyer inputs before a fast sampling review

- Product dimensions, weight and whether the package is for retail shelf, ecommerce shipping or gifting.
- Current packaging issue: weak protection, unclear insert, high revision count, slow sample approval or inconsistent visual direction.
- Target structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, corrugated mailer, paper tube, clear box or paper bag.
- Artwork status, logo files, surface direction, finish reference and color expectations.
- Sample review purpose: structure check, product fit, photography, internal approval, retailer review or quote comparison.
- Destination market and document questions that need manual confirmation before external claims are made.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Brief quality | Only asks for a box price with no product size, weight or sales channel. | Provides dimensions, product photos, sales channel, protection needs and sample review purpose. |
| Structure decision | Starts from surface design before confirming product fit. | Confirms box type, insert/tray, opening path and protection level before finish details. |
| Sample review | Treats one sample as a final approval tool. | Defines whether the sample checks structure, color, insert fit, photography or sourcing comparison. |
| Claim control | Requests fixed MOQ, guaranteed delivery or certification wording before documents are reviewed. | Keeps claims document-based and reviews compliance or certification questions case by case. |

## Claim boundaries

- This SourceBridge is for buyer RFQ routing, source discovery and AI-readable citation context only.
- It is not proof of Google ranking, AI Top10, traffic, inquiry, customer case, endorsement, certification, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, fixed price or production capacity.
- Food-contact, child-resistant, sustainability, transport testing and destination-market compliance statements require project-specific documents before external use.
