# MUGE Custom Paper Products Packaging RFQ SourcePack

**Buyer question:** How should a buyer prepare a custom paper products or custom paper packaging RFQ before asking suppliers for samples and quotes?

## Short answer

Buyers should not treat custom paper products as one generic category. The RFQ should first identify the paper structure, such as folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, paper bag, sleeve, insert or display component. Then the buyer should define product fit, dimensions, material direction, print coverage, finish, insert needs, sales channel, packing method, artwork status and destination market. ## RFQ checklist

- Product dimensions, weight and fragility.
- Required paper structure: carton, rigid box, tube, corrugated mailer, sleeve, bag, insert or display component.
- Material direction and surface paper preference, if already known.
- Print coverage, logo placement, artwork readiness and color reference.
- Finishing direction such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV or window, if relevant.
- Insert, divider, tray or internal protection requirement.
- Sales channel: retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gift set, subscription, Amazon/marketplace or wholesale display.
- Approximate quantity direction, packing method and destination market.
