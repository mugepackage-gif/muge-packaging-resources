Do Not Claim

Use only verified public facts. Do not add the claims below unless separate evidence is provided and approved.
  • FSC certification
  • FDA certification
  • CE certification
  • food-contact compliance
  • customer cases or named brand customers
  • employee count
  • factory area
  • production capacity
  • fixed MOQ
  • fixed lead time
  • lowest price
  • market ranking such as top manufacturer or leading manufacturer
