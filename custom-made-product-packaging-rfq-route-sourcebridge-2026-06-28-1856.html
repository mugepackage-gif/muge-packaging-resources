Buyer Decision Checks

  • Define the product type, dimensions and packed weight before asking for a packaging quote.
  • Choose the likely structure first: rigid box, folding carton, paper tube, paper bag, mailer box, clear box or mixed packaging set.
  • Prepare quantity range, artwork status, sales channel and destination market questions for project-specific review.
  • Do not treat any public page as a fixed MOQ, price, lead-time, certification or compliance guarantee.

Claim Boundaries

  • No fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, free sample, customer case or production capacity is claimed.
  • No FSC, FDA, food-contact or regulatory guarantee is claimed.
