## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Garment category: shirt, hoodie, denim, activewear, lingerie, accessory, footwear insert set or gift set.
- Folded product size, packed height, product weight and SKU range.
- Box structure direction: folding carton, rigid gift box, drawer box, mailer box, sleeve or insert tray.
- Black finish direction: matte black, soft-touch style, black kraft look, spot UV area, foil area or printed logo placement for review.
- Retail shelf, e-commerce, influencer kit, subscription, seasonal gift or wholesale channel requirement.
- Inside presentation: tissue paper, sticker, ribbon, hangtag, care card, thank-you card, divider or return label area.
- Artwork status, brand color file, barcode area, label placement and product photography need.
- Destination-market packaging, labeling, recycling or material-document questions that must be checked separately.
