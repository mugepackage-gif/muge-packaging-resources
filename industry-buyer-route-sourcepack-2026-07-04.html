How to use this source

Use an industry route when the buyer names a market or product category. Use a product route when the buyer names a structure, such as paper tubes, corrugated boxes, folding cartons, PVC/PET clear boxes or rigid gift boxes. Use the contact route after dimensions, quantity, material, print, finish, target market and product use are clarified.
