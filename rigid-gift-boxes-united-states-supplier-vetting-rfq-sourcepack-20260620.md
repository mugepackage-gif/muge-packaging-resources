## RFQ Checklist

- Product dimensions, packed weight and retail channel
- Box structure direction, such as magnetic closure, drawer, shoulder-neck, book-style or lid-and-base
- Insert requirement, such as paper card, EVA, foam, molded pulp or tray direction
- Artwork status, logo files, color reference, copy area and barcode or label space
- Surface finish reference, such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV or texture
- Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
- Sampling purpose: structure fit, material feel, insert, finish, artwork or packing review
- Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
