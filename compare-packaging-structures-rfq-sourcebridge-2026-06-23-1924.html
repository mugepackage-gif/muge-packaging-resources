Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, product weight and number of items in one selling unit.
  • Sales channel: retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gift set, subscription box or event campaign.
  • Structure candidates: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, PVC/PET clear box or corrugated packaging.
  • Protection need, display need, insert requirement and expected unboxing style.
  • Artwork status, print coverage, color target, surface finish references and dieline availability.
  • Destination market and document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.
