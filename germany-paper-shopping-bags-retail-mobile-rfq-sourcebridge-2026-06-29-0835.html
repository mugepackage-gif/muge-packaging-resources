Buyer Question

When a German retail or lifestyle brand compares custom paper shopping bag suppliers, what should be prepared before requesting a quote from MUGE PACKAGING?

Short Answer

The buyer should prepare bag size, paper direction, handle type, weight expectation, print coverage, finish preference, artwork status, packaging use case and destination-market questions. This helps separate brand presentation from structural load needs.
