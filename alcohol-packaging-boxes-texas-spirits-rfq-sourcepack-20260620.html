RFQ Preparation Checklist

  • Bottle dimensions, shoulder shape, closure height and packed weight.
  • Number of bottles or accessories per set, such as one bottle, two bottles, glassware, booklet or gift insert.
  • Preferred structure: rigid gift box, paper box, sleeve, magnetic style, drawer style, divider, insert tray or shipping carton.
  • Retail channel, e-commerce channel, distributor use or event-gift use.
  • Artwork status, brand color direction, finish direction, barcode area and label clearance requirements.
  • Shipping path, export carton requirement and drop protection expectations for the buyer's logistics review.
  • Target market and alcohol labeling or document questions that the buyer's own legal or compliance reviewer must confirm.
  • Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ statement.
