Buyer Intent Covered

This evaluation brief is for buyers comparing rigid gift box manufacturers for cosmetics, wellness products, electronics accessories, candles, fragrance, gift sets, retail launch kits and premium e-commerce packaging. It focuses on supplier review questions that can be checked before sampling.

Project Inputs to Confirm

  • Product dimensions, total set weight and required insert support.
  • Box structure: lift-off lid, shoulder neck box, clamshell box, drawer box or magnetic closure box.
  • Board thickness direction, wrap paper, lining paper and surface finish preference.
  • Retail channel, shipping method, unboxing goal and shelf presentation requirement.
  • Artwork status, logo files, color references and any document review required for the target market.

Supplier Evaluation Questions

  • Can the supplier explain structure tradeoffs before recommending a premium box style?
  • Can the supplier review insert support and product fit before sample production?
  • Does the supplier avoid unsupported claims about guaranteed lead time, fixed MOQ or lowest price?
  • Can the supplier discuss finish options such as hot stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV and lamination in relation to the brand direction?
  • Can the supplier separate gift presentation requirements from shipping protection requirements?
