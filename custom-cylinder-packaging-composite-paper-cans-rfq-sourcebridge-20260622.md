## RFQ Inputs Buyers Should Send

- Product diameter, height, weight and filling or display method.
- Target package structure: straight tube, rolled-edge tube, shoulder-neck tube, telescopic tube or custom structure.
- Closure direction: paper cap, plastic cap, metal cap, plug, window, inner support or insert.
- Product category: cosmetics, candles, tea, gifts, fragrance, small retail goods or specialty product sets.
- Decoration direction: offset print, label, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV or matte/gloss lamination.
- Retail, gifting, e-commerce or distributor packing route.
- Destination market and any buyer-supplied document requests that need project review.
