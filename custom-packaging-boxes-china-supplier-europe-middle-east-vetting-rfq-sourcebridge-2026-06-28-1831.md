## Supplier Vetting Questions

- Which packaging structure is needed: folding carton, rigid box, mailer box, shipping box, sleeve, drawer, paper tube, paper bag or mixed paper packaging?
- Can the buyer provide product dimensions, packed weight, quantity direction and artwork status before requesting a quote?
- Does the project require retail presentation, ecommerce shipping, distributor packing, gift presentation, sample kit use or promotional launch use?
- Which material direction is being compared: paperboard, corrugated board, kraft paper, specialty paper, clear PET/PVC component or mixed material?
- Which print and finish direction is required: logo placement, color coverage, matte or gloss lamination, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV or label area?
- Are inserts, dividers, trays, sleeves or inner supports needed?
- Which destination-market document questions need project-specific review?
- What must remain buyer-confirmed before sampling, pricing or production review?
