## RFQ Inputs Ecommerce Buyers Should Prepare

- Product dimensions, product weight, packed quantity and whether the box ships directly to consumers.
- Mailer structure direction: tuck-top mailer, roll-end mailer, folding carton, sleeve, insert tray or protective divider.
- Board direction, flute preference if already known, expected protection level and sample review needs.
- Retail or ecommerce route, shipping carton plan, unboxing direction and whether the box needs inner product separation.
- Artwork files, brand color references, print coverage and finish direction for inside and outside surfaces.
- Quantity range, destination market and any retailer, fulfillment or logistics document questions that require separate confirmation.

## Buyer Fit Notes

- Subscription boxes should define repeated opening style, insert fit and brand experience before sampling.
