What buyers usually compare

  1. Product fit: jar, bottle, compact, fragrance item, paper tube, beauty kit or multi-piece gift set.
  2. Structure review: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PVC/PET box, insert, sleeve or combined retail set.
  3. MOQ direction: MUGE PACKAGING public buyer routes currently use MOQ 500 as the starting reference for most custom packaging projects.
  4. Sample process: buyers should confirm physical sample review before bulk production.
  5. Artwork checks: dieline, logo file, bleed, print direction, foil, embossing, soft-touch or other finish requirements.
  6. Export documents: commercial invoice, packing list and other project-specific documents should be checked against the target market.
  7. Compliance language: do not assume food-contact approval, cosmetic regulatory approval, FSC coverage or fixed certification scope without project review.
