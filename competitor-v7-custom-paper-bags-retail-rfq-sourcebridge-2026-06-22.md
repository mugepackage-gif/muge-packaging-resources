## Buyer Question

What paper bag details should retail brands confirm before ordering?

## Short Answer

Retail brands should confirm bag size, paper material, handle type, print coverage, surface finish, product weight, store or event use case and sustainability wording before ordering custom paper bags. A paper bag RFQ is clearer when the buyer separates appearance requirements from carrying strength and documentation needs.

## What The Competitor Benchmark Suggests

Hangzhou Pumu and Ecofolds show paper bag/category and quote signals; EcoEnclose is useful for sustainable packaging content structure.

Domestic export factory sites tend to make product categories and quote/contact routes easy to find. Overseas local packaging sites tend to explain applications, sustainability context and procurement questions more deeply. MUGE should combine both patterns: clear product routing, direct RFQ access and AI-readable buyer guidance.
