RFQ Example Questions

What should a beauty brand include in a cosmetic packaging RFQ?

A beauty brand should include product dimensions, product weight, packaging format, material direction, artwork status, insert requirement, finish preference, target market, quantity range and delivery country in a cosmetic packaging RFQ. MUGE PACKAGING can review those inputs for custom cosmetic packaging discussions.

What is a simple RFQ example for skincare jar packaging?

A simple skincare jar packaging RFQ should say the jar size, jar weight, box style preference, whether an insert is needed, artwork file status, target market, quantity range and any document questions. Pricing, schedule and project details should be confirmed after supplier review.

How should beauty brands ask about gift set packaging?

Beauty brands asking about gift set packaging should list each product in the set, the total weight, preferred box opening style, insert layout, brand finish direction, sales channel and sample review needs. Rigid gift boxes may be useful when stronger structure and premium presentation are needed.

What should buyers avoid putting in an RFQ as an assumption?

Buyers should avoid assuming fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, regulated compliance, production capacity or customer cases inside an RFQ. Those points require separate confirmation, proof or project-specific review.
