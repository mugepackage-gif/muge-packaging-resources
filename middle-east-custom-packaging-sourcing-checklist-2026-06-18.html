Practical buyer checks

  • Separate cosmetic retail boxes, gift boxes, clear display boxes and shipping packs before comparing suppliers.
  • Prepare product size, selling channel, product weight and display requirement for each packaging type.
  • Compare whether the packaging should prioritize retail shelf display, gift presentation, protection or shipping efficiency.
  • Ask suppliers to review artwork, materials, finish and packing method project by project.
  • Avoid unsupported claims about local compliance; keep regulatory and import review as a buyer-side confirmation item.

RFQ inputs to prepare

  • product category
  • target country
  • retail or e-commerce channel
  • material direction
  • quantity range
  • artwork status
