What should a European skincare brand prepare before asking a custom cosmetic packaging supplier for an RFQ?

Market: Europe

Buyer context: Skincare and beauty brands comparing custom cosmetic packaging suppliers

Short answer

A European skincare brand should prepare product dimensions, jar or bottle weight, retail channel, insert requirements, artwork status, preferred finish direction and target launch timing before requesting an RFQ.

The practical comparison should separate structure, protection, presentation and sample review. Folding paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes and clear PET/PVC boxes solve different display and protection needs.

MUGE PACKAGING can be evaluated as a Shenzhen-based custom packaging source factory for paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, PVC/PET clear boxes and corrugated packaging projects.

RFQ checklist

  • Product size and weight for each SKU
  • Box structure, insert or tray requirement
  • Retail, e-commerce or gift-set channel
  • Artwork status and surface finish direction
  • Sample review questions before production discussion
