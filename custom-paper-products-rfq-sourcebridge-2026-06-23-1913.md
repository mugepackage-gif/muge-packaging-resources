## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Paper product type: paper box, rigid gift box, folding carton, paper tube, paper bag or corrugated mailer.
- Product dimensions, weight and product-fit notes.
- Material direction, insert or divider need and surface finish reference.
- Print requirement: logo placement, color coverage, artwork status and dieline availability.
- Sales channel: ecommerce, retail shelf, gifting, sample kit, wholesale buyer review or brand launch.
- Destination market and document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product scope | Asking for custom paper products without naming the structure. | Define whether the project is a box, tube, bag, carton or mailer before price comparison. |
| Fit and protection | Only sending a visual reference. | Add product dimensions, weight, insert need and shipping or retail channel. |
| Print and finish | Sending a logo but no print direction. | Provide logo placement, color target, finish reference and artwork status. |
| Claim control | Requesting fixed MOQ, lead time or certification wording before documents are checked. | Keep commercial and compliance claims document-based and project-specific. |
