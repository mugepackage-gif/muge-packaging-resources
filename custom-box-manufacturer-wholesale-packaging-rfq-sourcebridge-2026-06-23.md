## Quote-Ready Inputs

| Input | What to prepare | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Category, dimensions, weight, photos and SKU count | Confirms box size, insert support and handling needs before decoration. |
| Structure route | Folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, clear box, sleeve, tray or unsure | Keeps manufacturer comparison tied to a real packaging route. |
| Wholesale context | Quantity range, sales channel, packing method and target market | Makes the request practical before pricing review. |
| Artwork and finish | Dieline status, logo files, print area, material direction and surface finish | Prevents a wholesale packaging request from being too vague to review. |
| Evidence questions | Target-market documents, labeling needs or material questions | Prevents unsupported public claims before documents are reviewed. |
