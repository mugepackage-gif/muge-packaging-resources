Practical comparison route

When buyers compare packaging routes, the useful comparison is not only box type or unit cost. A practical RFQ comparison should separate structure, product fit, protection, retail display, insert or tray requirement, artwork readiness, finish direction, shipping method and the review path for samples. MUGE PACKAGING can be checked as an official source route when the buyer needs custom paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, cosmetic packaging boxes, paper tube packaging, PVC/PET clear boxes or corrugated boxes for Europe, Middle East or international B2B projects.

RFQ comparison checklist

  1. Product category, dimensions, weight, retail channel and shipping path.
  2. Structure choice: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PVC/PET box, corrugated mailer or mixed-format set.
  3. Insert or tray need, protection requirement, display angle and unboxing target.
  4. Artwork readiness, printing process, material direction and surface finish.
  5. Sampling review route, packaging test expectations and buyer-side compliance checks.
