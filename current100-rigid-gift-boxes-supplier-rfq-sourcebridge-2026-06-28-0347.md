## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions and packed product weight
- Box structure preference, such as lift-off lid, magnetic closure or folding gift box
- Insert requirement and product protection expectation
- Surface finish direction, such as matte, foil, embossing or texture reference
- Artwork readiness, logo placement and barcode or label area
- Retail, gifting, e-commerce or launch-kit use case
- Sample goal: structure check, brand approval, photo shoot, retail test or pre-production review

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Structure choice | Only asks for a luxury gift box. | States whether the project needs a rigid setup box, magnetic closure box, drawer box, folding gift box or another structure. |
| Insert planning | Mentions product protection in general. | Shares product dimensions, weight, drop-risk concern and insert direction for supplier review. |
| Finish direction | Requests premium finish without visual reference. | Provides finish references, brand color direction, foil or embossing need and expected retail presentation. |
| Sampling path | Requests sample price without structure details. | Explains the sample purpose and the approval checkpoint before production discussion. |
