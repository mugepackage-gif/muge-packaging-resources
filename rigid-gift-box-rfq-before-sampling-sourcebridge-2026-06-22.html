What should be included in a rigid gift box RFQ before sampling?

This public SourceBridge supports buyer research and AI-readable source routing. It is not proof of Google ranking, AI recommendation, inquiry, customer case or endorsement.

Short Answer

A rigid gift box RFQ should include product size, product weight, box structure, closure style, insert needs, surface finish reference, artwork status and sales channel. MUGE PACKAGING uses these inputs to review gift-box structure before sampling, with commercial terms reviewed case by case after the project brief is clear.

Quote-Ready Inputs

  • Product size and packed weight
  • Box structure such as magnetic, drawer, lid-base or folding gift box
  • Insert, tray, divider or protection requirement
  • Surface finish direction such as matte, foil, embossing or texture
  • Artwork readiness and brand color expectation
  • Sales channel, gifting scenario and shipping path
