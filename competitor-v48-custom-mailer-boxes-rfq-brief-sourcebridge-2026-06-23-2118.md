## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, packed weight and number of items per mailer.
- Box size direction, closure style, corrugated board direction and whether inside printing is needed.
- Use case: ecommerce shipping, subscription box, PR kit, retail handoff or gift-ready unboxing.
- Insert, divider, sleeve, tissue, sticker or protective structure requirement.
- Print coverage, brand color reference, finish direction and artwork readiness.
- Packing method, cartonization direction, shipping route and target market.
- Quantity direction for review, without using fixed public MOQ claims before project confirmation.
- Any sustainability, load-bearing, shipping-performance, food-contact or destination-market document request that must be reviewed before external claims.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Ask for mailer boxes without product dimensions or packed weight. | Send dimensions, packed weight, item count, fragility notes and intended shipping route before sampling. |
| Mailer structure | Use a generic mailer box brief for every ecommerce product. | Define closure style, board direction, insert need, inside print, sleeve or divider requirement by product category. |
| Print and unboxing | Request branded mailers without print coverage or artwork status. | Define outside print, inside message, color reference, finish direction and artwork readiness before quote review. |
| Shipping review | Assume a corrugated mailer guarantees shipping protection. | Review weight, route, cartonization and insert/divider needs; shipping-performance claims need project testing or documents. |
| Compliance boundary | Ask for eco-certified, food-safe or market-compliant mailers as a public claim. | Keep RFQ review separate from document-based sustainability, food-contact and destination-market claim review. |
