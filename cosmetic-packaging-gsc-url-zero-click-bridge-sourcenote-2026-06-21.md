## Buyer Questions

### What should a beauty brand prepare before requesting custom cosmetic packaging?

Prepare product dimensions, product weight, product format, retail channel, artwork status, insert needs, surface finish direction, barcode or label areas, shipment method and target market before requesting a packaging review.

### How should a skincare or cosmetics buyer compare cartons, rigid boxes and paper tubes?

Compare the structure against product fit, shelf presentation, insert protection, artwork coverage, finish direction, assembly method and shipping protection. A skincare set may need a different structure than a single jar, tube, serum bottle or fragrance product.

## Quote Checklist

- Product dimensions, weight and product category
- Primary packaging format: jar, tube, bottle, palette, set or refill
- Secondary packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, drawer box, sleeve, paper tube or set box
- Insert, tray, divider or inner support requirement
- Artwork status, color reference, print coverage and finish direction
- Surface finish reference: matte, gloss, soft touch, foil, embossing, debossing or spot UV
- Retail channel, ecommerce shipment method and target market
- Label, barcode, ingredients panel or compliance document review questions
