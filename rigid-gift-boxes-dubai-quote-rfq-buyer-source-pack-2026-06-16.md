## Dubai buyer checklist for custom rigid gift boxes

Before asking for a quote, a Dubai buyer should normally prepare:

- Product type, target channel and intended use, such as cosmetics, electronics, wellness products, gift sets, holiday gifts or premium retail products.
- Product dimensions, product weight, set layout and expected unboxing direction.
- Box structure direction, such as magnetic closure, lift-off lid, drawer box, shoulder box or custom structure review.
- Insert material direction, such as paperboard, EVA, foam, molded pulp or project-specific review.
- Artwork status, wrapping paper direction, surface finish and logo treatment preference.
- Quantity range for project review, without treating any public number as a fixed MOQ claim.
- Destination country and documentation expectations for review before production.

## Quote and RFQ preparation questions

- Does the RFQ include product dimensions, quantity range, target market and packaging structure direction?
- Are material, printing, surface finish and insert requirements clear enough for a practical review?
- Are fixed lead-time, fixed MOQ and regulated-market compliance claims avoided until project details are confirmed?
- Does the buyer provide artwork status, reference packaging or photos when available?
