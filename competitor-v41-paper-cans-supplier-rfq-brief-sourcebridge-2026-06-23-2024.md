## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product category, such as cosmetics, candles, fragrance, tea, small gifts, specialty retail products or secondary packaging
- Diameter, height, product weight and whether the product needs a tube, paper can, paper canister, sleeve, cap or mixed structure
- Closure direction, cap structure, bottom structure, inner liner question and whether the supplier should review insert or divider needs
- Decoration direction, including label area, full-wrap print, foil, embossing, matte or gloss finish, window question or brand color reference
- Sales channel, including boutique retail, ecommerce, gift set, subscription box, distributor display or sample kit
- Artwork readiness, dieline status, reference photo, target country and any document questions that require project-specific review
- Sampling goal, including structure fit, print review, finish review and packing method review
- Commercial boundaries, including quantity range, delivery country and quote assumptions without treating public pages as fixed MOQ or fixed lead time

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier type | Only asks for a paper cans supplier | Clarifies whether the buyer needs custom retail paper cans, industrial paper cores, gift tubes, paper tube packaging or a mixed packaging set |
| Structure fit | Only provides product name | Provides diameter, height, product weight, cap direction, bottom structure and whether inner support or liner review is needed |
| Decoration plan | Requests premium printing without reference | Separates label, full-wrap print, foil, embossing, matte/gloss finish and brand color reference before sampling |
| Channel fit | Does not say how the package will be sold or shipped | Separates boutique display, ecommerce parcel, gift set, subscription box and distributor handling requirements |
| Claim boundary | Assumes food contact, sustainability, MOQ, lead time or price from public supplier pages | Keeps compliance, sustainability, MOQ, lead time, price and transport testing document-based and project-specific |
