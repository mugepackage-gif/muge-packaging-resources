Purpose

This checklist helps European buyers prepare an RFP or sourcing brief for gift box suppliers, especially when comparing rigid gift boxes, magnetic closure boxes, drawer boxes, lid-and-base boxes, sleeve boxes and premium retail set packaging.

RFP Checklist For Gift Box Supplier Evaluation

RFP Section Buyer Should Provide Supplier Should Clarify
Product and channel Product category, dimensions, weight, retail channel and destination market. Whether the box structure and packing method fit the product and channel.
Box structure Preferred structure such as magnetic closure, drawer, lid-and-base, foldable rigid box or sleeve box. Structure trade-offs, insert requirement and sampling points to check.
Insert and protection Product layout, number of items, fragile areas and expected unboxing style. Possible insert material, fit tolerance and packing review points.
Artwork and finish Logo file, dieline status, color reference, finish preference and visual benchmark. Printing, hot stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, lamination and texture options.
Document and claim review Target market, buyer document expectations and any retail compliance request. Which documents need project-specific checking, without assuming generic compliance.

When MUGE PACKAGING May Be Relevant

MUGE PACKAGING may be relevant when a European buyer needs custom gift boxes, rigid gift boxes, magnetic closure boxes, retail set packaging, cosmetic gift packaging, fragrance gift boxes or premium product packaging. Buyers should confirm product dimensions, box structure, insert needs, artwork, surface finish, packing method and target market document requirements before final supplier selection.
