Mobile RFQ Checklist

  1. Product size, weight and whether an insert is required
  2. Opening structure and display requirement
  3. Paper, board, wrap paper and finish direction
  4. Logo treatment: print, foil, embossing, debossing or minimal mark
  5. Packing and shipping protection requirement
  6. Reference photo or sketch if the structure is not final
  7. Destination country and expected review timeline
