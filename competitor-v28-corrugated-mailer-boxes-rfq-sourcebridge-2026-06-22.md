## What buyers should compare

### Product fit

- Buyer inputs: Product dimensions, item weight, fragility level, packed quantity and whether the box is for direct shipping, retail handoff or subscription use.

### Structure and protection

- Buyer inputs: Mailer style, tuck closure, insert or divider requirement, flute direction, board feel and expected packing method.

### Brand presentation

- Buyer inputs: Inside/outside print direction, color coverage, logo placement, unboxing reference and whether the box must work as both shipper and brand packaging.

### Logistics context

- Buyer inputs: Destination market, ecommerce channel, pallet or carton packing notes, sample review purpose and any document questions that require manual confirmation.

## Corrugated mailer RFQ checklist

- Product size, product weight and fragility level.
- Preferred mailer structure or current shipping box reference.
- Protection requirement, insert/divider need and packing route.
- Print coverage, logo placement, color direction and finish reference.
- Estimated quantity range, target market and ecommerce channel.
- Sample review purpose and any destination-document questions for manual review.
