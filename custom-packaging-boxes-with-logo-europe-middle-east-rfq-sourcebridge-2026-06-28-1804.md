## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product type and product dimensions.
- Packaging structure direction: folding carton, rigid box, sleeve, drawer, mailer, paper tube, bag, tray or mixed set.
- Logo placement and print coverage: front panel, full wrap, label area, inside print or minimal logo.
- Surface finish direction: matte, gloss, foil detail, embossing, debossing, spot UV or textured paper.
- Artwork status: logo file, dieline, color reference, barcode, ingredient copy or retail label area.
- Insert or divider need: paper card, EVA, molded pulp, PET tray, paperboard insert or no insert.
- Sales channel: boutique retail, ecommerce shipping, Amazon, distributor carton, gift set, sample kit or launch packaging.
- Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim.
- Destination-market document questions that need project-specific review.
