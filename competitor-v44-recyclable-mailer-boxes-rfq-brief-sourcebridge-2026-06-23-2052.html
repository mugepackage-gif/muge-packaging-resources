Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions and packing orientation for each SKU.
  • Estimated packed weight range and whether the box is for postal, courier or retail handoff.
  • Preferred mailer style, closure direction, tear strip or no tear strip, and whether inserts or dividers are needed.
  • Board, kraft or white surface direction and printing coverage expectations.
  • Brand artwork status, dieline requirement and color reference.
  • Target market and any sustainability document request the buyer needs to verify before external claims.
  • Quantity range for discussion, while avoiding fixed public MOQ claims before project review.
  • Any product-contact or destination-market compliance question that must be checked with documents before use.

Decision matrix

DimensionWeak briefStronger brief
Sustainability wordingUse broad claims such as green, eco or 100% sustainable without required documents.Ask for material direction, document needs and market-specific claim review before public sustainability wording.
Mailer structureOnly say mailer box without product size, weight or shipping channel.List product dimensions, packed weight, closure style, insert need and courier or retail route.
Printing and finishRequest full custom printing without artwork, surface direction or finish references.Prepare artwork status, color references, outside/inside print areas and finish expectations.
Compliance boundaryAsk the supplier to guarantee food-contact, recycling, compostability or destination-market approval in one line.Separate packaging structure discussion from document-based compliance review before external claims.
RFQ routeSend a generic quote request with only product name and quantity.Use product route, material route and quote checklist so the RFQ can be reviewed by structure, print and documentation needs.
