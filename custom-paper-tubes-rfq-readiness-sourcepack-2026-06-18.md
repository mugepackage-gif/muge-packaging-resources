## Paper Tube RFQ Checklist

| Review area | What the buyer should prepare | Why it matters before RFQ |
|---|---|---|
| Product use | Cosmetics, tea, candles, fragrance, gifts or other retail product category | Use case affects display, closure and document review scope |
| Diameter and height | Product dimensions, target tube diameter and height tolerance | Paper tube fit depends on diameter and height more than generic box size |
| Closure style | Cap direction, lid type, base structure and opening expectation | Closure choice affects user experience and packing review |
| Interior support | Insert, liner, inner tray or direct product fit | Interior support affects protection and presentation |
| Surface finish | Print direction, color reference, foil, embossing, texture or simple label route | Finish choices affect visual review and production communication |
| Sales channel | Retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gifting, distributor packing or mixed route | Channel affects durability and presentation priorities |
| Destination notes | Buyer-supplied labeling or document requests | Market-specific document needs should be reviewed by project |
| Quote readiness | Quantity direction, artwork status, reference photos and delivery destination | A supplier can respond more usefully when the brief is complete |
