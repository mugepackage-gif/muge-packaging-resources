## Where should a buyer start when comparing custom packaging options for several product categories?

Start from the product catalog, then narrow the discussion by packaging type, product size, estimated quantity, material direction, artwork status and target market. This route is useful when a buyer has not yet decided whether the project needs paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, tubes, clear boxes or corrugated packaging.

## What should candle, fragrance, cosmetic or tea buyers prepare before discussing custom paper tube packaging?

Prepare tube diameter, height, product weight, cap or closure preference, display channel, surface finish direction and artwork status. Paper tube discussions should review product fit and closure style before price comparison.
