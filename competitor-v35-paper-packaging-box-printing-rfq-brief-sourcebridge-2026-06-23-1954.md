## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Box structure and product dimensions before discussing print layout
- Artwork status, dieline availability and editable file format
- Print coverage, logo placement and mandatory label area
- Brand color reference, such as Pantone, CMYK or sample color target
- Surface finish direction, including matte lamination, gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV or textured paper
- Material and paperboard direction that may affect color and finish appearance
- Insert, tray or inner support details that influence visible print areas
- Target market and any document questions that require project-specific review

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Artwork readiness | Only sends a logo image | Provides editable artwork, dieline status, logo placement and required label area |
| Color control | Asks for brand color without reference | Provides Pantone, CMYK, physical sample or previous packaging reference |
| Finish selection | Only asks for premium look | Separates lamination, foil, embossing, spot UV and paper texture preferences |
| Structure and print fit | Discusses printing before confirming box structure | Confirms box type, dimensions, fold lines and visible surfaces before print review |
| Document boundary | Assumes the printer can decide all compliance wording | Keeps labeling, certification and destination-market review document-based |
