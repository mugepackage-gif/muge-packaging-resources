Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, packed weight and number of items per gift box.
  • Box structure direction such as lift-off lid, magnetic closure, drawer box, book-style box or sleeve box.
  • Rigid board thickness direction, paper wrapping direction and whether the box needs an inner tray or divider.
  • Use case: retail shelf, gift set, cosmetic set, electronics accessory, PR kit or premium unboxing.
  • Surface finish references such as matte, soft-touch, foil, embossing, spot UV or specialty paper direction.
  • Artwork readiness, dieline need, color reference and target market.
  • Quantity direction for review, without using fixed public MOQ claims before project confirmation.
  • Any sustainability, food-contact, product-contact, destination-market or certification document request that must be reviewed before external claims.
