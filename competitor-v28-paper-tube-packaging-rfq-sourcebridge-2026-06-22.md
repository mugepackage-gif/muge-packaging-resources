## What buyers should confirm

### Tube size

- Buyer inputs: Inner diameter, height, product weight, product fit tolerance and whether a liner, insert or label is required.
- Why it matters: Tube packaging quotes depend heavily on diameter and height, not only product category.

### Cap and closure

- Buyer inputs: Paper lid, metal cap, plastic cap, plug direction, shoulder style or other closure reference.
- Why it matters: Cap structure changes both buyer experience and production review requirements.

### Retail use case

- Buyer inputs: Cosmetics, tea, candle, fragrance, small gift, specialty retail or ecommerce set context.
- Why it matters: Use case affects surface finish, display expectations and protection notes.

### Artwork and finish

- Buyer inputs: Full print, label, logo placement, color direction, matte or glossy direction and reference image.
- Why it matters: The same tube structure can feel very different depending on artwork and finish choices.

## RFQ checklist

- Diameter, height, product weight and fit tolerance.
- Cap or closure reference.
- Product category and retail or ecommerce use case.
- Print, label, finish and logo placement direction.
- Estimated quantity range, target market and sample review purpose.
- Any document questions that need manual confirmation.
