Buyer Checks

  • State the UK destination early so packaging documents and shipping expectations can be reviewed project by project.
  • Define product type, diameter, height, cap style, product weight and retail or ecommerce channel.
  • Use sampling to check structure fit, closure style, print direction, surface finish and packing method.
  • Compare supplier communication by whether they ask project-specific questions instead of giving generic promises.
