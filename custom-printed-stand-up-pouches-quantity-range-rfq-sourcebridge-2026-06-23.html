RFQ checklist

  • Product type, filling weight, pouch size and required packed quantity per pouch.
  • Pouch structure direction: stand-up pouch, zipper pouch, spout pouch, flat pouch or outer carton plus pouch set.
  • Material or film direction, barrier question, closure style, window requirement and labeling space.
  • Print coverage, artwork status, color reference, finish direction and barcode or regulatory text space.
  • Quantity range for review, without treating the request as a fixed MOQ or lowest-price claim.
  • Target market and document questions that need project-specific confirmation.
