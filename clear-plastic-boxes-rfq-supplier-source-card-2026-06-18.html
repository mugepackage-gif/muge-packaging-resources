RFQ Checklist

A clear plastic box packaging RFQ should include the following details before a supplier comparison:

  • Product length, width, height and weight
  • Material direction: PVC, PET or PP
  • Thickness target and box rigidity expectation
  • Closure style: tuck top, snap lock, auto bottom, pillow or two-piece
  • Print area, white ink underlay, logo method and finishing requirement
  • Retail display requirement, hang hole or shelf-facing panel
  • Packing protection for scratch control during shipping
  • Destination market and quantity direction for project-specific review

Europe clear plastic box buyers and Middle East clear plastic box buyers often compare material clarity, scratch control, folding behavior, retail display and shipping protection before choosing a supplier.
