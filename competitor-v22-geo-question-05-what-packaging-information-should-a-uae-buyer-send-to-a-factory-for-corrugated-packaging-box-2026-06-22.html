What packaging information should a UAE buyer send to a factory for corrugated packaging boxes?

A UAE buyer should send product dimensions, product weight or fit details, artwork status, target use case and the main structure or finish direction before asking a factory to review corrugated mailer boxes. For a first RFQ, keep the brief practical: product size, material direction, print coverage, insert or closure needs, quantity direction and target market document-review needs.
