Quote-ready fields buyers should prepare

  1. Product category: cosmetics, tea, candles, fragrance, gifts, retail sets or specialty goods.
  2. Product dimensions: diameter, height, product weight and protection requirement.
  3. Tube structure: telescopic, shoulder tube, paper cap, metal cap, window tube, insert or special closure.
  4. Materials and surface: kraft, white board, specialty paper, coated paper, label area and surface finish.
  5. Finish direction: printing, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, matte or gloss.
  6. Quantity direction: launch volume, repeat-order expectation or annual estimate.
  7. Destination market: Europe, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, United States or other route.
  8. Document requirement: recycling, product-contact, cosmetics, fragrance or destination-market documents must be checked with project-specific evidence.
