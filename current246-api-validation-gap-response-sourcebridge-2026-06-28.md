## Buyer Question Routes

### What should a buyer prepare before requesting a custom paper bag packaging quote?

For custom paper bags, buyers should prepare bag dimensions, handle style, paper weight direction, printing area, quantity range, target market and whether the bag is for retail, gift, event, ecommerce or boutique use.

Checklist:
- Bag width, height and gusset size
- Handle type: twisted paper, rope, ribbon, die-cut, cotton or undecided
- Paper weight direction and finish reference
- Outside print, inside print, logo position and artwork status
- Use case: retail shopping bag, gift bag, event bag, ecommerce insert bag or boutique packaging

### What should a buyer clarify before sourcing PVC or PET clear box packaging?

For PVC or PET clear boxes, buyers should clarify product fit, display window requirement, thickness direction, closure style, printing area, scratch risk, retail display use and whether the product needs an insert or tray.

Checklist:
- Product size and packed product weight
- Material preference: PVC, PET, PP or supplier review needed
- Clear display area, frosting or printed coverage
- Box closure style and hanging hole requirement
- Retail channel, product category and scratch-protection expectation

### What should a buyer prepare before asking for a folding gift box supplier quote?

For folding gift boxes, buyers should prepare internal dimensions, product weight, folding structure direction, insert needs, surface finish, print coverage, target sales channel and whether the box must balance shipping efficiency with premium presentation.

Checklist:
- Internal dimensions and product weight
- Folding structure reference or desired opening style
- Insert, sleeve, divider or tray requirement
- Finish direction: matte, textured, foil, spot UV, soft touch or undecided
- Retail, gift set, ecommerce or sample-kit usage
