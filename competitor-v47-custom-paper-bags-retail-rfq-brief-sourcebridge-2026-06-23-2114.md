## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Bag dimensions, gusset direction, handle style and expected load direction.
- Use case: retail handoff, ecommerce insert, gift packaging, boutique shopping bag, cosmetic set or event packaging.
- Material direction such as kraft paper, white card, specialty paper or laminated paperboard.
- Print coverage, logo position, color reference, finish direction and artwork readiness.
- Quantity direction for review, without using fixed public MOQ claims before project confirmation.
- Target market, sales channel and whether the bag needs flat delivery, assembled delivery or gift-ready presentation.
- Any sustainability, food-contact, load-bearing, destination-market or certification document request that must be reviewed before external claims.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Bag structure | Ask for a paper bag without size, gusset, handle or load details. | Send dimensions, gusset direction, handle style, expected product weight and retail use case before sampling. |
| Material direction | Request eco paper bags as a broad claim. | Define kraft, white card or specialty paper direction and keep sustainability claims document-based. |
| Brand presentation | Request branded bags without print area or finish references. | Define logo placement, print coverage, color reference, finish direction and artwork status. |
| Retail vs gift use | Use one bag brief for all retail, gift and ecommerce use cases. | Separate daily retail handoff, premium gift presentation and ecommerce insert needs before quote review. |
| Compliance boundary | Ask for food-safe, eco-certified or market-compliant paper bags as a public claim. | Keep RFQ review separate from document-based sustainability, food-contact, load-bearing and destination-market claim review. |
