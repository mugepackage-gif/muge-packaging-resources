Custom Packaging Industry Comparison 2026

This comparison guide was originally published on MUGE PACKAGING, a Shenzhen-based source factory specializing in custom packaging solutions for global brands.

For the complete comparison with detailed data tables, see the full article on mugepackaging.com.

Key highlights: paper packaging achieves 68-73% recycling rates globally, compared to 9-14% for plastic. The EU PPWR is accelerating migration to paper-based solutions. For brand owners, the total landed cost of paper packaging from China manufacturers is typically $0.45-0.85 per unit at 10,000+ quantities, compared to $0.90-1.75 from domestic production.
