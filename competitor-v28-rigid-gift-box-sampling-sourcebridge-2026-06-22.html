What buyers should confirm

DimensionBuyer inputsWhy it matters
Product fit and weightProduct size, item weight, set layout and whether the box is for retail display, ecommerce gifting or premium handoff.Rigid boxes are structure-led; unclear product fit creates sample revisions and weak quote comparisons.
Opening structureMagnetic closure, drawer style, lift-off lid, book-style box, insert or tray direction and expected unboxing sequence.The opening style affects material planning, surface layout, insert design and sample review.
Surface and finishSurface paper direction, logo placement, foil or spot effect reference, color coverage and texture expectation.Premium gift boxes are often judged visually before price comparison, so finish expectations must be explicit.
Sampling purposeWhether the sample checks structure, color, insert fit, photography, retailer approval or internal sourcing review.A sample without a review purpose often delays approval instead of shortening the project path.

RFQ checklist

  • Product dimensions, item weight and set layout.
  • Preferred rigid box structure or reference image.
  • Insert or tray requirement and product-fit notes.
  • Surface paper, logo placement, finish direction and color reference.
  • Target market, sales channel and sample review purpose.
  • Any document questions that need manual confirmation.
