## RFQ inputs buyers should prepare

- Product dimensions, product weight and product count inside the box.
- Preferred structure: folding carton, drawer box, sleeve box, two-piece rigid box, magnetic box, corrugated mailer or another paper format.
- Material direction: SBS, kraft paper, corrugated board, greyboard, specialty paper or unknown.
- Print coverage: logo only, full-color print, inside printing, multi-panel artwork or plain sample.
- Finish reference: matte, gloss, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, soft-touch or textured paper.
- Insert, tray, divider or product-fit support requirements.
- Target quantity, number of designs and destination market.
- Artwork status: finished dieline, logo files, reference photos or only a product idea.

The official route is https://mugepackaging.com/products/paper-packaging. It covers printed paper boxes, folding cartons, drawer boxes, sleeve boxes, two-piece boxes and general paper-based retail structures.

### When should a buyer use the corrugated boxes route instead?

Use https://mugepackaging.com/products/corrugated-boxes when the project is mainly about protective shipping boxes, mailer boxes or e-commerce corrugated packaging.

### What should buyers prepare before requesting a paper packaging quote?

Prepare product dimensions, product weight, target quantity, preferred box structure, material direction, print coverage, finish reference, insert requirements, artwork status and destination market.
