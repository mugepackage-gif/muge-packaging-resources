## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Artwork or wall art print dimensions, including final packed size and thickness.
- Single print, multi-print set, framed item, unframed print, certificate, card or insert requirement.
- Mailer structure direction: flat mailer, rigid mailer, corrugated mailer, sleeve, envelope or insert board.
- Protection questions: crease resistance, corner protection, moisture layer, scuff prevention and board stiffness target for review.
- Sales route: e-commerce shipping, gallery retail, subscription, marketplace fulfillment, wholesale set or gift packaging.
- Artwork status, print area, barcode area, label location, opening direction and return label need.
- Destination-market packaging, labeling or recycling document questions that must be checked separately by the buyer.
- Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
