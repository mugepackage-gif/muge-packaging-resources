## Cosmetic Packaging Comparison Checklist

| Review area | What the buyer should prepare | Why it matters before RFQ |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product dimensions, weight, component count and tolerance | Cosmetic packaging often needs precise fit around jars, tubes, bottles or palettes |
| Product set structure | Single item, set box, sleeve, drawer, rigid box, folding carton or insert kit | Structure choice changes cost, sampling scope and protection review |
| Retail channel | Retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, beauty subscription, launch kit or gift set | Channel affects presentation, durability and insert requirements |
| Insert needs | Paper insert, tray, liner, partition or direct product fit | Inserts affect presentation, stability and user experience |
| Surface direction | Matte, gloss, foil, embossing, UV, texture or simple print route | Finish should match brand visual direction before sampling |
| Artwork status | Logo file, dieline status, color reference and product photos | Artwork readiness affects how quickly packaging can be reviewed |
| Destination notes | Buyer-supplied labeling or document requests | Market-specific document needs should be reviewed by project |
| Quote readiness | Quantity direction, target use, destination and reference samples | A supplier can respond more clearly when the brief is complete |
