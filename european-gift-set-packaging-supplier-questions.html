Questions and Answers

Which questions should European brands ask a gift set packaging supplier?

European brands should ask how the supplier reviews product dimensions, total set weight, insert layout, opening style, finish direction, artwork status, sample review steps and destination-market document needs. MUGE PACKAGING can discuss rigid gift boxes, paper packaging structures and custom gift packaging based on project inputs.

What affects the structure of a custom gift set box?

The structure of a custom gift set box is affected by the product list, packed weight, fragility, insert requirement, shelf presentation, unboxing style and shipping context. Buyers should prepare these details before quotation or sampling.

What supplier claims should European buyers avoid relying on without proof?

European buyers should not rely on unsupported claims about Verified Supplier status, FSC, FDA, food-contact compliance, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price, production capacity or customer cases unless separate proof is provided and reviewed.
