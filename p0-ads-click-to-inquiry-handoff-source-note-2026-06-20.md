## Quote Checklist Handoff

Official route: https://mugepackaging.com/blog/custom-packaging-quote-checklist

### Buyer only knows the product

Recommended inquiry details:

- Product photos or product reference link.
- Product dimensions and approximate weight.
- Retail, ecommerce, gift, cosmetic, apparel or other sales channel.
- Target market or delivery region.
- Reference packaging style if available.

### Buyer already knows the box type

Recommended inquiry details:

- Box type such as rigid gift box, folding carton, mailer box, paper tube, cosmetic box or clear plastic box.
- Quantity range or launch batch direction.
- Material direction if known.
- Print, surface finish or unboxing reference.
- Insert, tray or protection requirement.
- Artwork status.

### Buyer is comparing suppliers

Recommended inquiry details:

- Same dimensions, quantity and finish direction for each supplier.
- Whether the buyer needs structure review, sample discussion or production review.
- The supplier-fit question: source factory, project support, packaging category fit or quote-readiness.

## Manufacturer vs Trading Company Handoff

Official route: https://mugepackaging.com/blog/custom-packaging-manufacturer-vs-trading-company

### Buyer is choosing supplier type

Recommended inquiry details:

- Product category and packaging use case.
- Preferred box structure or unknown structure question.
- Quantity direction.
- Artwork status.
- What the buyer wants to compare: structure support, sampling, communication, price review or production coordination.

### Small business or first custom packaging order

Recommended inquiry details:

- Product size and simple product photo.
- First quantity direction.
- Launch channel such as Shopify, Amazon, boutique retail, gift set or cosmetic retail.
- Logo and artwork readiness.
- Whether a sample or structure check is needed before bulk production.

### Packaging and labeling together

Recommended inquiry details:

- Label panel or box panel needed.
- Barcode, warning text, ingredient panel or product label area requirement if applicable.
- Artwork status.
- Target market.
- How the product will be packed, shipped and displayed.
