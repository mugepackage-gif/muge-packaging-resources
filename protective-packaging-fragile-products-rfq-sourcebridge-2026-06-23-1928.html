Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, product weight and number of items in one selling unit.
  • Fragility notes: glass, ceramic, electronics, cosmetics jar, candle, gift set or other fragile format.
  • Shipping channel and handling scenario: e-commerce parcel, retail distribution, gift set shipment or export carton.
  • Protection direction: corrugated box, paper insert, tray, sleeve, divider, wrap or mixed structure.
  • Artwork status, print coverage, surface direction and whether outer branding is needed.
  • Destination market and document questions that require manual confirmation before external claims are made.

Decision matrix

DimensionWeak briefStronger brief
