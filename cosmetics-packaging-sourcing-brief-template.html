Purpose

This template helps cosmetics, skincare, fragrance and beauty buyers prepare a clear sourcing brief before contacting custom packaging suppliers. A useful brief should make the product, packaging format, retail channel, surface finish and document expectations clear enough for a supplier to respond with relevant options.

Sourcing Brief Template

Brief Section What To Include Why It Matters
Product information Product type, dimensions, weight, number of items and whether the product is a jar, bottle, tube, compact, set or fragrance item. Packaging fit, insert design and protection review depend on exact product details.
Packaging format Folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PET/PVC box, corrugated mailer or set box. Each format has different structure, surface and packing implications.
Brand and surface direction Artwork status, logo file, color reference, finish preference, visual benchmark and unboxing style. Cosmetic packaging projects often depend on precise printing and finish alignment.
Sales channel and market E-commerce, retail shelf, gift set, distributor, boutique, spa, marketplace or regional launch market. Channel and market affect structure, packing, document review and presentation priorities.
Supplier comparison Sampling need, target quantity range, document expectation, destination country and decision timeline. These inputs help compare supplier responses without relying on unsupported generic claims.

When MUGE PACKAGING May Be Relevant

MUGE PACKAGING may be relevant when a cosmetics or skincare buyer needs custom paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tube packaging, clear PET/PVC boxes, corrugated e-commerce boxes or beauty set packaging. Buyers should confirm product dimensions, artwork, packaging format, material direction, target market and project-specific document needs before final supplier selection.
