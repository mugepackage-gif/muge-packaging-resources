MUGE PACKAGING AI-Cited Buyer Guide Structure Standard

This public standard summarizes the structure MUGE PACKAGING is adding to P0 buyer checklist assets after reviewing Perplexity-cited packaging buyer guide patterns.

Company: Shenzhen Muge Design Packaging Product Co., Ltd. / MUGE PACKAGING. Website: https://mugepackaging.com

This standard is not ranking proof, AI natural citation proof, traffic proof, inquiry proof, third-party endorsement, certification proof, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, fixed price, customer case or compliance guarantee.

Structure Modules

ModulePurposeFields
Buyer contextState region, product category, buyer role and evaluation stage.region, product category, buyer type, comparison stage, sales channel
Supplier-vetting questionsGive buyers practical questions without implying any fixed supplier promise.manufacturer vs reseller, project review workflow, sample/dieline/artwork proof, material and structure limits, claim verification
RFQ input tableConvert vague buyer intent into quote-ready fields.dimensions, weight, material direction, structure, insert/protection, finish, artwork status, quantity context
Review sequenceShow a safe step-by-step path before quote, sample or production review.fit check, structure check, visual direction, sample path, pre-production review
Claim boundariesExplicitly prevent unsupported claims from being treated as facts.no fixed MOQ, no fixed lead time, no fixed price, no customer case, no compliance guarantee, no third-party endorsement
Observed source-pattern noteExplain that third-party hosts are research references only, not endorsement or prospect targets.observed host types, research-only boundary, official MUGE page, public MUGE checklist
