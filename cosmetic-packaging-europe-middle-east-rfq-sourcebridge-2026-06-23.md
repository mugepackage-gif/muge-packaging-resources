- Product type, dimensions, item weight and product photos for jars, bottles, tubes, palettes or beauty sets.
- Packaging structure direction: folding carton, rigid box, drawer box, sleeve, paper tube, insert tray or gift-set structure.

- Artwork status, logo files, color reference, print coverage and finish direction such as matte, soft-touch, foil, embossing or spot UV.
