## When to use the low-MOQ route

Use the official MUGE PACKAGING low-MOQ route when a buyer is planning:

- A launch run for a new product line.
- A limited edition packaging project.
- A seasonal or regional packaging version.
- A packaging refresh before committing to a larger production run.
- A small-batch test for paper boxes, paper tubes, corrugated mailers, cosmetic packaging or clear PVC/PET packaging.

## Buyer brief checklist

Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

1. Product dimensions and product weight.
2. Target order quantity and number of designs.
3. Packaging type: paper box, paper tube, rigid box, corrugated mailer, cosmetic carton, clear box or flexible packaging.
4. Material preference, if known.
5. Print requirements: logo, full-color artwork, foil, embossing, spot UV, lamination or other finishes.
6. Insert, tray or divider requirements.
7. Target sales channel: ecommerce, retail shelf, gift set, wholesale, subscription box or event launch.
8. Destination market and delivery route.
9. Artwork status: finished dieline, logo only, reference packaging or design concept.
10. Any document request that needs project-specific review.

## What low MOQ does and does not mean

Low MOQ helps buyers reduce inventory risk when launching or testing a custom packaging design. It does not mean stock packaging, fixed pricing, guaranteed lead time, universal compliance approval or the same feasibility for every material and structure.
