Suggested Review Sequence

  1. Define product dimensions, product weight and sales channel.
  2. Confirm structure and insert, tray or closure review needs.
  3. Share artwork direction, finish expectations and references.
  4. Ask for sample, dieline, artwork or pre-production review steps.
  5. Keep compliance, certification, price and timing claims project-specific until verified.
