## Paper Tube RFQ Checklist

Buyers should prepare these details before requesting a paper tube packaging quote:

1. Product category: cosmetics, candles, tea, fragrance, small gifts, wellness, accessories or specialty retail products.
2. Product size and packed weight.
3. Tube diameter, height and closure direction, if already known.
4. Cap or lid preference, including paper lid, metal lid, plastic cap, window, insert, sleeve or label direction.
5. Surface direction, including matte, glossy, textured, foil, embossing, label-based finish or bilingual artwork.
6. Sales channel and destination market for review.
7. Artwork status and whether a sample review is needed.
