Questions and Answers

How should buyers compare clear boxes and paper boxes?

Buyers should compare product visibility, shelf display, product protection, material direction, branding surface, packed weight, market document needs and unboxing style. Clear PVC/PET boxes may support visible retail display, while paper boxes may support stronger branding, print surfaces and a different presentation style.

What should a clear box vs paper box inquiry include?

The inquiry should include product dimensions, product weight, visibility requirement, retail channel, preferred packaging format, artwork status, quantity range, destination country and any document or compliance questions that require separate confirmation.

What safety boundaries matter when comparing clear and paper packaging?

Buyers should avoid assuming food-contact compliance, regulated-market approval, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price or sustainability claims unless separate project-specific documents support those claims.
