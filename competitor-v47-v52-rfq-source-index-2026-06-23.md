## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Product dimensions and weight | Helps review box structure, board direction and protection needs. |
| Packaging structure | Clarifies whether the buyer needs a rigid box, mailer box, paper bag, paper tube or another paper-based format. |
| Material direction | Supports discussion of paperboard, corrugated board, kraft paper, greyboard or other project-specific options. |
| Printing and surface finish | Helps review artwork, color coverage, lamination, foil, embossing or other finish requirements. |
| Insert or divider needs | Important for skincare sets, gift boxes, ecommerce kits and fragile products. |
| Target market and channel | Retail display, ecommerce shipping and gifting use cases often need different packaging decisions. |
| Artwork status | Shows whether the buyer has dielines, logo files, reference photos or only an early packaging idea. |
