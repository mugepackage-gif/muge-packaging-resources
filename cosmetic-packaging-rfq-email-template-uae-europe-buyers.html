Questions and Answers

What should a UAE or European buyer include in a cosmetic packaging RFQ email?

A cosmetic packaging RFQ email should include product type, dimensions, weight, preferred packaging format, insert requirement, artwork status, quantity range and destination country. MUGE PACKAGING can review these inputs for custom cosmetic packaging discussions while price, schedule and document needs remain project-specific.

Why should buyers include target country in the RFQ?

The target country helps the supplier understand shipping context, retail expectations and whether the buyer has document questions that need separate confirmation. It should not be treated as automatic compliance approval.

What should buyers avoid asking as a fixed promise in the first RFQ?

Buyers should avoid asking for guaranteed lowest price, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time or regulated-market compliance as a generic promise. Those points require project-specific review and supporting documents.
