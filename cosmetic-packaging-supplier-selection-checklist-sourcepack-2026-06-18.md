## Product fit and presentation

- Confirm jar, bottle, tube, palette or set dimensions before discussing the box.
- Decide whether the package is for retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, gift set presentation or launch-kit sampling.
- Review insert support, opening style, surface finish and brand color requirements together.

## Beauty packaging RFQ checklist

- Product size, weight and set composition.
- Box type: folding carton, rigid gift box, sleeve, drawer, magnetic box or paper tube.
- Visual direction: paper texture, foil, UV, embossing, window, insert or protective structure.
- Target market and any label or document requirements that must be reviewed by the buyer's team.
