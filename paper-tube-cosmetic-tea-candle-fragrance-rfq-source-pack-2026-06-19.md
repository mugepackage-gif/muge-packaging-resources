## RFQ preparation checklist

Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

- product diameter, height, packed weight and display direction
- preferred closure style, cap material direction or lid direction
- insert, liner, sleeve, label or internal protection requirement
- sales channel: retail shelf, gift set, e-commerce, distributor or promotional kit
- artwork status, finish direction and destination-market document questions
- target quantity range for review, without assuming fixed MOQ
