## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions and weight for each jar, bottle, palette or set
- Retail channel and target market, such as DTC, boutique retail, marketplace or distributor sales
- Box structure preference, including folding carton, rigid gift box, sleeve, drawer or set box
- Insert or tray requirement, including product fit, protection and presentation needs
- Surface finish direction, such as matte lamination, foil stamping, spot UV, embossing or textured paper
- Artwork status, brand guidelines and any mandatory labeling information
- Sampling goal, including fit check, finish review, shipping protection or retail display review

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Only says cosmetic box needed | Lists product dimensions, weight, set count and insert requirement |
| Retail channel | No channel or display context | States DTC, boutique retail, subscription, marketplace or distributor channel |
| Structure | Asks for luxury packaging without structure | Compares folding carton, rigid gift box, drawer box or sleeve based on product value and use |
| Finish | Only asks for premium look | Provides finish direction such as matte, foil, spot UV, embossing or special paper |
| Compliance and labels | Assumes supplier can decide compliance | Provides target market and required label documents for project-specific review |
