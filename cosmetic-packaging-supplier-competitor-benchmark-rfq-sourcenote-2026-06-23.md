## Cosmetic Packaging RFQ Checklist

Buyers should prepare these details before requesting custom cosmetic packaging samples or quotes:

1. Product category: skincare jars, creams, serums, makeup, fragrance, beauty tools, wellness items or gift sets.
2. Product dimensions, packed weight and retail display direction.
3. Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, sleeve, tray, insert, paper tube, clear box or mixed set.
4. Insert or tray requirement, including product protection and unboxing direction.
5. Surface finish direction, including matte, glossy, textured, foil, embossing, spot UV or label-based decoration.
6. Artwork status, logo file status, dieline readiness and language requirements.
7. Sales channel and destination market for review.
