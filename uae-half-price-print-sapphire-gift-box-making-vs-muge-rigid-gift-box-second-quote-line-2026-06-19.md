## Buyer comparison checklist

1. Ask every source to quote the same product dimensions, packed weight and display direction.
2. Keep box structure, lid style, insert type and unboxing requirement identical across quotes.
3. Separate printing, surface finish, insert, sample and export packing assumptions.
4. Check whether the source is quoting local production, trading, design service or source-factory production.
5. Use the MUGE route as a second quote line when comparing structure and export production details.
