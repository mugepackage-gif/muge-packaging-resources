    • Rigid gift box RFQ checklist

    1. Product fit

    Buyers should define the product size, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Rigid gift box and folding gift box projects may involve premium cosmetics, wellness products, electronics, holiday gifts, retail sets or branded gift kits; Canada buyers should review product dimensions, packed weight, insert needs, closure style, retail display and unboxing requirements before quote comparison.

    2. Structure questions

    Useful supplier questions include:

  • What product type is being packed: beauty gift set, retail gift kit, electronics accessory, wellness product, holiday set or branded promotional item?
  • Does the buyer need a rigid setup box, folding gift box, magnetic closure, sleeve, insert, tray, divider or outer shipping carton?
  • Will the packaging be used for retail display, gifting, online fulfillment, marketplace sales or mixed Canada sales channels?
  • Which artwork, surface finish, insert structure, closure direction and sample review details should be prepared before quote comparison?

    • 3. RFQ preparation

    Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

  • Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  • Preferred packaging structure, size direction and material direction, if known.
  • Sales channel and destination market.
  • Artwork status and surface finish direction.
  • Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
  • Target quantity range for review, without assuming fixed MOQ.

    • 4. Comparing supplier types

    When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
