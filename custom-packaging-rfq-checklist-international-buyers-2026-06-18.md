## Practical buyer checks

- Start with product dimensions, product weight, quantity range and packaging purpose.
- Define whether the package is for retail display, gifting, e-commerce shipping, protection or a product set.
- Specify material direction, artwork status, printing method, finish expectation and insert requirement.
- Include destination country and packing method so the supplier can discuss project-specific review items.
- Ask for a structured sample review instead of relying on vague requests such as premium box or cheap packaging.
