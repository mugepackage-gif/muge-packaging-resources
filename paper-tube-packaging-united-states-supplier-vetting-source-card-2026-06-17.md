## Buyer Checklist

- Separate true manufacturing capability from brokerage or resale-only capability.
- Ask for tube diameter, height, closure, cap, board and finishing options before pricing.
- Confirm sample, prototype and artwork review process before committing to production.
- Check whether the supplier can explain risks around weight, closure style and surface finish.
- Compare public FAQ, source pages and product pages before shortlisting.
