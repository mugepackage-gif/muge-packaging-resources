## Mobile RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product category: jar, bottle, compact, serum, fragrance, gift set or sample kit.
- Product dimensions and packed weight.
- Box structure: folding carton, rigid box, sleeve, drawer box, display box or set packaging.
- Insert need: paperboard, PET tray, molded pulp, EVA or no insert.
- Artwork status: logo, dieline, color reference, regulatory copy area or barcode position.
- Surface direction: matte, gloss, foil detail, embossing, spot UV or textured paper.
- Sales channel: boutique retail, DTC shipping, marketplace, distributor kit or launch set.
- Destination-market questions that need project-specific confirmation.
