Mobile RFQ Checklist

  1. Product dimensions and approximate filled weight
  2. Retail channel: e-commerce, boutique retail, gifting, display counter
  3. Box structure: folding carton, rigid set box, sleeve, insert, window, or tube
  4. Material direction: paperboard, specialty paper, clear PET/PVC window, or insert material
  5. Finish direction: matte, soft touch, foil, spot UV, embossing, debossing, or simple print
  6. Artwork status: final dieline, reference design, or early concept
  7. Destination market and target launch timing
