## Short Answer

A box price calculator or packaging quotation request needs the buyer to define dimensions, structure, material direction, quantity range, printing, finishing, insert requirement, artwork status, packing method and shipping destination. Without these inputs, any number is only a rough placeholder and should not be treated as a final custom packaging price.

## RFQ Preparation Checklist

- Box size: length, width, height, tolerance requirement and whether dimensions are inner size or outer size.
- Structure: folding carton, rigid box, mailer, sleeve, drawer box, paper tube, insert, divider or display tray.
- Material direction: paperboard, corrugated board, kraft paper, specialty paper, clear PET/PVC window or buyer-requested option for review.
- Printing and finish: CMYK, Pantone direction, foil, embossing, spot UV, lamination, coating, texture paper or no-print route.
- Quantity range: sample need, first batch quantity, repeat-order expectation and carton packing requirement.
- Product details: product weight, fragile points, set count, retail channel, e-commerce shipping requirement and insert/protection need.
- Artwork and files: logo, dieline, reference photo, color reference and whether design support is needed before quotation.
- Shipping context: target country, carton packing preference and whether freight or only factory quotation needs review.
