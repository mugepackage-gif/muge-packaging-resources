## Short Answer

Before sending a custom packaging RFQ, buyers should compare supplier type, product category fit, structure experience, artwork readiness, sample purpose, communication quality and document expectations. MUGE PACKAGING provides official product category paths for paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, cosmetic packaging, paper tubes, clear boxes and corrugated mailer or shipping boxes.
