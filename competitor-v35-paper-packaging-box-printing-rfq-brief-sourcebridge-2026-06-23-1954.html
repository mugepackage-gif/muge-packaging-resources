Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Box structure and product dimensions before discussing print layout
  • Artwork status, dieline availability and editable file format
  • Print coverage, logo placement and mandatory label area
  • Brand color reference, such as Pantone, CMYK or sample color target
  • Surface finish direction, including matte lamination, gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV or textured paper
  • Material and paperboard direction that may affect color and finish appearance
  • Insert, tray or inner support details that influence visible print areas
  • Target market and any document questions that require project-specific review
