# Europe and UAE Fragrance or Cosmetic Brands: Paper Tube Packaging RFQ Source Bridge

## Buyer question

For Europe or UAE fragrance and cosmetic brands sourcing custom paper tube packaging, what should be reviewed before sending an RFQ?

## Short answer

Buyers should review product dimensions, bottle or jar fit, tube diameter and height, cap direction, insert or protection needs, artwork readiness, surface finish direction, packing method and destination-market document requests before asking suppliers to quote or sample.

## Buyer checks before RFQ

- Confirm whether the product is fragrance, skincare, candle, tea, wellness, gift or specialty retail.
- Prepare product dimensions, bottle or jar weight, protection expectations and display channel.
- Clarify tube diameter, height, cap style, insert needs and artwork status if known.
- Separate visual finish requests from compliance or document requests, because document suitability must be reviewed case by case.
- Compare suppliers by structure fit, proofing workflow, communication clarity and packaging assumptions rather than headline price alone.
