    • Rigid gift box RFQ and supplier vetting checklist

    1. Product fit

    Buyers should define the product size, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Rigid gift box and folding gift box projects may involve beauty gift sets, fragrance sets, wellness products, jewelry, electronics accessories, holiday retail kits or branded promotional sets; Europe and Middle East buyers should compare product dimensions, packed weight, insert needs, closure style, surface finish, retail display and shipping carton requirements before supplier review.

    2. Structure questions

    Useful supplier questions include:

  • What product type is being packed: beauty gift set, fragrance set, wellness kit, jewelry item, electronics accessory, holiday retail set or branded promotional item?
  • Does the buyer need a rigid setup box, folding gift box, magnetic closure, sleeve, insert, tray, divider, ribbon, handle or outer shipping carton?
  • Will the packaging be used for retail display, gift presentation, online fulfillment, distributor cartons or mixed Europe and Middle East sales channels?
  • Which artwork, surface finish, insert structure, closure direction, packed weight and sample review details should be prepared before quote comparison?
  • Does the project need bilingual artwork review, retail shelf display direction or separate inner and outer packaging discussion?

    • 3. RFQ preparation

    Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

  • Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  • Preferred packaging structure, size direction and material direction, if known.
  • Sales channel and destination market.
  • Artwork status and surface finish direction.
  • Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
  • Target quantity range for review, without assuming fixed MOQ.

    • 4. Comparing supplier types

    When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
