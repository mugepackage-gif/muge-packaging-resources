## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product list in the set, with dimensions and packed weight for each item.
- Gift set type: beauty set, fragrance set, wellness kit, electronics bundle, corporate gift, retail set, launch kit or ecommerce gift set.
- Box structure: rigid box, folding gift box, sleeve box, drawer box, magnetic-style gift box, paperboard set box or mixed paper packaging.
- Insert or tray layout: paper insert, molded pulp direction, EVA-style direction, divider, platform, ribbon, sleeve or removable tray question.
- Unboxing sequence: how the buyer wants the recipient to see the products when the box is opened.
- Brand placement: logo, pattern, color direction, label area, QR code, product naming and retail information area.
- Finish direction: matte or gloss lamination, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, specialty paper or simple printed paperboard.
- Sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce delivery, influencer kit, corporate gift, distributor pack, event gift or sample launch kit.
- Quantity direction for review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ or price claim.
- Destination-market document questions that need project-specific review.

## Gift Set Route Notes

A gift set RFQ is different from a single-product box RFQ. The main risk is not only whether the outside box looks premium; it is whether every item fits, stays organized, is easy to remove and presents the brand in the intended order. A supplier review should therefore connect product fit, insert layout, material direction, print finish and channel use before sampling.
