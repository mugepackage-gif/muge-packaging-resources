## Sampling Review Checklist

- Product category: fragrance, candles, tea, cosmetics, small gifts, wellness, retail or e-commerce.
- Product fit: diameter, height, product weight, cap style and closure direction.
- Surface direction: kraft paper, printed wrap, label, foil, embossing, matte or gloss finish.
- Insert or liner: whether the product needs positioning, separation, inner support or barrier review.
- Sales channel: boutique retail, marketplace fulfillment, distributor sales, sample kit or gift set.
- Buyer location: UAE, Dubai, GCC, Saudi Arabia, Middle East or international export route.
- Quote-ready input: artwork status, quantity direction, packaging reference, carton packing notes and destination-market document requests.
