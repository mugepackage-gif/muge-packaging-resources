Quote-ready checklist

  1. Product category: cosmetics, tea, candles, fragrance, gifts, retail sets or specialty goods.
  2. Product dimensions: diameter, height, weight and protection requirement.
  3. Tube structure: one-piece, two-piece, telescopic, shoulder tube, paper cap, metal cap or special closure.
  4. Material direction: kraft, white board, specialty paper, coated paper, inner lining or surface paper.
  5. Finish direction: matte, gloss, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, label area and artwork readiness.
  6. Quantity direction: launch volume, repeat-order expectation or annual estimate.
  7. Destination market: Europe, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, United States or other route.
  8. Document requirement: any recycling, product-contact, fragrance, cosmetics or destination-market requirement must be checked with project-specific evidence.
