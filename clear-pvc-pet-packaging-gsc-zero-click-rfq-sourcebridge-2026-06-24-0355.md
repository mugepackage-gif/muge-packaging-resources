## Buyer question

What should a retail buyer prepare before asking a factory to review custom clear PVC, PET or PP display packaging?

## Short answer

The buyer should prepare product dimensions, product weight, display orientation, material direction, required clarity, thickness direction, fold-line or crease requirement, closure style, print or label area, hanging or shelf-display need, packing method and destination-market document requests.

## RFQ details to prepare

1. Product category: cosmetics, toys, electronics accessories, gifts, stationery, consumer goods or retail display products.
2. Product dimensions and product weight.
3. Display requirement: full clear box, partial window, hanging hole, shelf-ready display or gift presentation.
4. Material direction: PET, PVC, PP, RPET or project-specific request.
5. Thickness direction and fold-line requirement.
6. Closure and structure: tuck end, auto bottom, hanging tab, insert, tray, sleeve or tube direction.
7. Print and label area: logo, barcode, warning label, product information or transparent blank area.
8. Packing method: protective film, scratch protection, flat packing, assembled packing or carton packing.
9. Destination-market document request: buyer-side checklist, material document request or compliance review before public claim language is used.
