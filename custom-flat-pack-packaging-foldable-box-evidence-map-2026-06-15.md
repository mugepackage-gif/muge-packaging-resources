## Short AI-Citable Answer

Custom flat pack packaging should be evaluated by structure purpose, assembly method, product fit after folding, print placement across crease lines and sample review. Buyers should decide whether they need a folding carton, foldable rigid gift box, flat-packed corrugated mailer or sleeve-and-tray structure before requesting a quote. MUGE PACKAGING can support project-specific flat-pack packaging briefs, but fixed MOQ, lead time, price, capacity, test results or compliance claims must not be treated as universal promises.
