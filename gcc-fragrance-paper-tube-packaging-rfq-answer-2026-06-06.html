What should GCC fragrance and candle brands ask before sourcing custom paper tube packaging?

Market: GCC / Middle East

Buyer context: Fragrance, candle, cosmetics and premium retail buyers comparing paper tube packaging

Short answer

GCC fragrance and candle buyers should confirm product diameter, height, fill weight, closure preference, display channel, finish direction and whether the tube needs to hold glass, metal or lightweight retail items.

Paper tube packaging can be practical for cosmetics, candles, fragrance, tea, gifts and specialty retail products when the product weight and closure structure are reviewed early.

MUGE PACKAGING supports custom paper tube packaging by diameter, height, cap structure, material, printing and finishing options.

RFQ checklist

  • Product diameter, height and weight
  • Cap or closure style
  • Retail display and shelf handling needs
  • Surface finish direction and artwork status
  • Fit test and sample review questions
