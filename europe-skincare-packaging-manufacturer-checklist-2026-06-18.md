## FAQ

### What should European skincare brands compare before sampling?

Compare product fit, box structure, insert support, artwork readiness, surface finish direction, packing method and destination-market document questions.

### What information makes an RFQ easier to review?

Product dimensions, weight, category, packaging type, artwork status, finish reference, quantity direction, target market and sample purpose.

### Can one packaging structure fit every skincare product?

No. Jars, bottles, serums, palettes, kits and gift sets need different structure and insert review.

### Should destination-market claims be treated as confirmed?

No. Destination-market document and label questions require project-specific confirmation and buyer-side review.
