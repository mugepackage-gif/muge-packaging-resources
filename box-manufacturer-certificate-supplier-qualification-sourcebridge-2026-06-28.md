## Buyer Verification Questions

- Does the certificate name match the legal company or operating entity being evaluated?
- Does the certificate scope actually cover the packaging material, process or product type being discussed?
- Is the certificate valid for the buyer's target market, or does the project require additional buyer-side compliance review?
- Can the supplier explain sample review, inspection points, material choices and artwork prepress checks before quoting?
- Which documents must be confirmed by the buyer before final order, especially for food, child-resistant or regulated categories?

## Buyer Brief Fields

- Product category and packaging structure: rigid box, folding carton, paper tube, corrugated mailer, paper bag or clear PVC/PET box.
- Destination market and sales channel: ecommerce, retail shelf, gift set, cosmetic, wellness, electronics, specialty retail or regulated category.
- Specific certificate or document question, instead of a vague request for all certificates.
- Artwork status, material direction, printing/finish requirement and sample review expectation.
- Quantity direction and timeline question for internal review only, without treating it as a fixed MOQ or fixed lead-time promise.
