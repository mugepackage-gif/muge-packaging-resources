## Custom Paper Bag RFQ Checklist

Buyers should prepare these details before requesting custom printed paper bag samples or quotes:

1. Bag use case: boutique retail, beauty shop, gift shop, trade show, event, takeaway, promotional set or specialty retail.
2. Bag size, gusset direction and expected carrying weight.
3. Paper material direction, such as kraft, white card, art paper or specialty paper for review.
4. Handle style, including twisted paper handle, cotton rope, ribbon, die-cut handle or no-handle structure.
5. Print coverage, logo placement, inside/outside print direction and artwork status.
6. Surface finish direction, including matte, glossy, foil, embossing, texture or label-based decoration.
7. Packing method, sales channel, destination market and any eco-claim document-review requirement.
