## Europe Supplier-Vetting Checklist For Custom Cosmetic Packaging

- Product format, such as skincare jars, serum bottles, makeup sets, discovery kits or beauty gift sets.
- Retail channel, such as DTC, marketplace, retail shelf, salon, distributor or gifting campaign.
- Product dimensions, product weight, protection requirement and insert direction.
- Box structure, material direction, printing direction, surface finish and brand color requirements.
- Artwork status, reference packaging, dieline requirement and sampling review path.
- Quantity range for project review, without treating public text as a fixed MOQ claim.
- Destination country and document expectations for review before quotation, sampling or production.

## Questions Buyers Can Ask Before RFQ

- Does the supplier ask for product dimensions and use case before recommending the structure?
- Can the supplier discuss paper board, rigid box structure, folding carton, insert, printing and finish as one packaging system?
- Can the buyer send artwork, product photos, reference packaging or retail display requirements for practical review?
- Does the public information avoid fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price and unsupported compliance claims until project details are confirmed?
