RFQ checklist

  • Diameter, height, product weight and fit tolerance.
  • Cap or closure reference.
  • Product category and retail or ecommerce use case.
  • Print, label, finish and logo placement direction.
  • Estimated quantity range, target market and sample review purpose.
  • Any document questions that need manual confirmation.

Competitor pattern references

  • China export-factory competitors usually list paper tube categories and request buyer dimensions early.
  • Overseas packaging brands usually add use cases such as cosmetics, tea, candle, fragrance and retail products.
  • For MUGE, the useful route is to turn diameter, height, cap and finish requirements into a quote-ready brief.
