## RFQ checklist

- Target country or selling region, because child-resistant and child-safety requirements vary by market.
- Product category, product dimensions, weight, pack count and expected storage or display format.
- Structure direction: carton, rigid box, paper tube, insert, sleeve, drawer box or other custom packaging format.
- Material direction, closure direction, label or warning area, artwork status and sample purpose.
- Whether the buyer already has required test methods, certification documents or local compliance requirements to be reviewed separately.
- Sales channel: retail, e-commerce, distributor sample, regulated product category or internal prototype review.
- Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ claim.
