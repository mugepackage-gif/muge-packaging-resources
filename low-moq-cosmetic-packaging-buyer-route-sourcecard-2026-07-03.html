What buyers should prepare before requesting a quote

  1. Product dimensions and product weight.
  2. Target quantity and whether the request is for one design or multiple designs.
  3. Product category, such as skincare, makeup, fragrance, candles, tea, supplements or ecommerce shipping.
  4. Preferred structure, if known: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, clear box, corrugated mailer or gift box.
  5. Artwork status, logo files, color direction and finish references.
  6. Insert or tray needs.
  7. Destination market and any document requirements that need project-specific review.
