## Buyer Question This Answers

**What should buyers confirm before sampling magnetic closure gift boxes?**

Buyers should confirm product dimensions, packed weight, gift-set layout, opening style, magnet position, insert or tray plan, board direction, surface paper, print coverage, finish reference, artwork status, retail channel, target market and packing route before the first sample is made. These inputs help compare magnetic gift boxes, book-style rigid boxes, collapsible rigid boxes and premium retail gift packaging on the same basis.

## Sampling Checklist Matrix

| Sampling check | Why it matters | Buyer input to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Magnetic closure boxes need enough internal tolerance for the product, insert and opening movement. | Product length, width, height, weight, item count, fragile parts and gift-set layout. |
| Closure structure | Magnet placement, flap depth and opening style affect unboxing feel and stability. | Reference opening style, magnet direction, lid overlap preference and whether a ribbon pull is needed. |
| Insert support | Premium sets often fail at the insert stage if product movement is not reviewed early. | Paperboard, EVA, foam, molded pulp or tray direction, product arrangement and color direction. |
| Board and surface paper | Rigid box feel depends on board direction, wrap paper and edge finishing. | Greyboard direction, surface paper reference, color target, texture preference and edge-wrap expectation. |
| Print and finish | Foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV and soft-touch finishes need artwork and sample review. | Logo files, artwork status, Pantone or color direction, print coverage and finish reference photos. |
| Packing route | Collapsible and assembled rigid boxes affect freight volume, storage and retail preparation differently. | Flat-pack or assembled preference, carton packing, target market, sales channel and document review requests. |
