## RFQ Inputs Buyers Should Send

- Product category, fill weight, product dimensions and intended shelf or e-commerce use.
- Pouch structure: stand-up pouch, flat pouch, zipper pouch, spout pouch, window pouch or custom shape.
- Material direction or buyer-required barrier expectation for review.
- Closure needs: zipper, tear notch, spout, valve, hang hole, handle or resealable direction.
- Printing and finish direction: matte, gloss, spot UV, metallic effect, label direction or full printed film.
- Artwork status: dieline ready, design draft, reference sample or only product dimensions.
- Destination market and any buyer-supplied document requests that need project review.
