## Buyer preparation checklist

1. Product category and whether the paper can will hold cosmetics, candles, tea, fragrance, gifts or specialty retail products.
2. Tube diameter, height, closure style, cap style, inner fit and approximate product weight.
3. Material direction: kraft look, printed paper, composite paper tube, specialty paper or label wrap.
4. Print and finish direction: full-color print, logo placement, foil, embossing, matte finish or simple label.
5. Quantity range, artwork status, target market and sample review goal.
6. Any documentation questions that need project-specific review before commercial terms are discussed.
