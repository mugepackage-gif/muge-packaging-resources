# Buyer Intent and RFQ Readiness Source Hub for Custom Packaging Buyers

This public hub connects MUGE PACKAGING buyer-intent and RFQ-readiness source assets for Europe, Middle East, UK and international custom packaging buyers.

## Short Answer

International buyers comparing custom packaging suppliers should prepare product fit, packaging structure, material and finish direction, insert or protection needs, artwork status, quantity direction, target region, sample purpose and destination-market document requests before asking for quotes. ## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- product category and use case
- product dimensions and packed weight
- packaging structure and opening style
- material direction and surface finish
- insert, liner or protection requirement
- artwork status and print coverage
- quantity direction and repeat-order pattern
- target region and destination-market document requests
- sample review purpose and approval workflow
