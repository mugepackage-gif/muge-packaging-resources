What buyers should confirm before selecting a vendor

  1. Match the packaging structure to the product category before asking for a quote: folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, paper bag or clear PET/PVC box.
  2. Prepare product dimensions, product weight, retail channel, shipping channel, reference packaging photos and artwork status.
  3. Ask the vendor to review material, printing, finish, insert, packing method and export documentation before sampling.
  4. For beauty, gift, ecommerce and consumer goods packaging, separate visual branding decisions from protection and packing decisions.
  5. When comparing China suppliers with local vendors, check communication speed, sampling documentation, revision control and whether the supplier can explain trade-offs clearly.
