This public SourceBridge converts the current 20-site paper packaging competitor review into buyer-facing RFQ questions. It is not a ranking claim, endorsement claim, certification claim, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, fixed price or compliance guarantee.

## Buyer Question

What should ecommerce brands prepare before comparing custom corrugated mailer box suppliers?

Official MUGE route: https://mugepackaging.com/products/corrugated-boxes

## Competitor Patterns Observed

- China factory sites tend to put mailer box, corrugated box and shipping box keywords close to quote paths.
- Overseas packaging sites often explain protection, ecommerce use case and sustainability context.
- Strong corrugated pages turn weight, flute, wall type and shipping route into the core RFQ checklist.

## RFQ Checklist For Buyers

- Product dimensions, product weight, shipping route and required protection level.
- Mailer style, wall type, flute direction, closure, insert or divider need.
- Print coverage, quantity direction, destination market, packing volume and artwork status.
