## Supplier Comparison Scorecard

| Area | Buyer should compare | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product category, diameter, height, weight, closure direction and fit tolerance | Prevents sampling based on incomplete tube dimensions |
| Structure | Cap style, opening direction, insert or liner request, material direction and surface finish | Shows whether the supplier can discuss the actual packaging structure |
| Branding | Artwork status, print coverage, finish reference and brand color requirement | Helps buyers compare packaging presentation before sampling |
| Market route | Destination market, retail channel, e-commerce or distributor route | Keeps the RFQ tied to the buyer's sales context |
| Document request | Any document or compliance request that needs project review | Avoids treating public marketing text as a compliance guarantee |
| Communication quality | Clear RFQ response, project detail confirmation and realistic next-step questions | Helps buyers compare suppliers without relying only on broad claims |

## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

- Product category and product dimensions
- Tube diameter, height and closure direction
- Product weight and fit tolerance
- Cap style and opening direction
- Material direction, liner or barrier request if any
- Artwork status, print coverage and finish reference
- Quantity range and destination market
- Sales channel: retail, e-commerce, distributor or gifting
- Any document request that needs project review
