RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

  • Product type: skincare jar, serum bottle, makeup palette, fragrance item, beauty set or launch kit.
  • Primary container dimensions, filled weight, lid clearance and fragile-part notes.
  • Packaging structure direction: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, clear box, sleeve, insert tray or set packaging.
  • Artwork status, logo files, print coverage, barcode or label space and color references.
  • Finish direction such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, window or texture.
  • Retail channel, ecommerce shipment method, target market and project-specific document questions.
  • Quantity direction for internal review, without treating it as a fixed public MOQ.
