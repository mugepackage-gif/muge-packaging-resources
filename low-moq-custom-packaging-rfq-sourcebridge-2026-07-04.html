RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

  • Product dimensions and product weight.
  • Target quantity per design.
  • Packaging structure direction: paper tube, corrugated mailer, paper box, folding gift box, clear PVC/PET box, cosmetic carton or unsure.
  • Material direction: kraft paper, white card, corrugated board, greyboard, clear plastic or mixed material.
  • Print and finish expectations: full-color print, foil, emboss/deboss, spot UV, soft-touch or simple logo direction.
  • Sample requirement and launch timing.
  • Destination market and shipping route.
  • Artwork status.
