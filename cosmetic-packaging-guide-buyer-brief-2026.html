RFQ checklist for cosmetic packaging

  1. Product format: jar, bottle, tube, palette, fragrance item, beauty set or secondary box.
  2. Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  3. Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, insert set, sleeve or clear box.
  4. Material direction: paperboard, kraft paper, greyboard, PET/PVC clear packaging or supplier recommendation needed.
  5. Finish requirements: foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, soft-touch lamination, matte or gloss direction.
  6. Quantity direction, number of SKUs and whether this is a launch, replenishment or test run.
  7. Artwork status: existing dieline, brand guideline, logo only, or design support needed.
  8. Target market and any document questions that require project review.

What buyers should not assume

Cosmetic packaging details should be reviewed by product fit, material, decoration method, artwork readiness, destination market and use case. Buyers should not assume fixed pricing, fixed schedule, regulated-market approval, food-contact suitability, customer cases, certification coverage or production capacity from this public reference.
