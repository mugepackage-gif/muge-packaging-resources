Buyer RFQ Brief For Minimalist Packaging

  • Product category: cosmetics, gift set, fragrance, electronics accessory, wellness product, DTC box or retail paper bag.
  • Structure: folding carton, rigid gift box, sleeve, drawer box, paper bag, insert or corrugated mailer.
  • Visual direction: clean typography, restrained color palette, logo placement, tactile finish and material texture.
  • Physical fit: product dimensions, weight, insert tolerance, shipping path and retail display requirement.
  • Surface review: paperboard direction, coating, foil, emboss/deboss, spot UV or print coverage.
  • Commercial review: quantity direction, artwork status, sample purpose and destination market.
