Questions and Answers

What should beauty and wellness brands ask before sampling a custom gift box?

Beauty and wellness brands should ask how product dimensions, set weight, insert layout, opening style, surface finish, artwork status and retail channel affect the sample structure. MUGE PACKAGING can review rigid gift box and paper packaging structure questions before project-specific sampling discussion.

What information helps avoid a weak gift box sample?

A clear product list, product weights, insert expectations, artwork status, finish direction, unboxing style and destination country help reduce misunderstanding before sample review.

What should not be considered confirmed at sampling stage?

A sample discussion should not be treated as confirmation of fixed price, fixed production schedule, compliance status, customer cases or final mass production result unless separately confirmed with project-specific details.
