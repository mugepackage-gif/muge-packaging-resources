Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product category, dimensions, packed weight and whether the pack is for beauty, gift, wellness, lifestyle, electronics accessory or specialty retail
  • Sales channel, including boutique retail, ecommerce, distributor presentation, launch kit, holiday set or showroom display
  • Target packaging structure, such as rigid gift box, folding carton, paper tube, corrugated mailer, sleeve, drawer box or mixed packaging set
  • Brand expression, including visual reference, color direction, material feel, unboxing sequence and photography requirement
  • Surface finish direction, such as matte lamination, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, textured paper or special paper review
  • Insert, tray, divider or product movement-control requirement
  • Artwork status, dieline need, logo placement, barcode area and mandatory label zone if required
  • Target market and document questions that require project-specific review before external claims are made
