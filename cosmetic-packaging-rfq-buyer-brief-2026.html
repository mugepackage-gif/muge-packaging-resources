RFQ checklist for beauty and skincare buyers

  1. Product type: jar, bottle, tube, palette, compact, fragrance item, beauty set or other product format.
  2. Exact product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  3. Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, insert tray, sleeve, divider or clear box.
  4. Material direction: paperboard, kraft paper, greyboard, PET/PVC clear packaging or supplier recommendation needed.
  5. Surface finish: matte, gloss, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV, soft-touch lamination or other reference.
  6. Artwork status: final dieline, existing brand file, logo only or design support needed.
  7. Quantity direction, number of SKUs and whether the project is a launch, replenishment, gift set or sample run.
  8. Target market and any document questions that require project review.

Buyer fit notes

Cosmetic packaging projects often need structure review before quoting because product fit, insert tolerance, surface finish and retail presentation can affect both the sample and bulk production plan. Buyers should send product size, product photos, artwork status and target quantity direction as early as possible.
