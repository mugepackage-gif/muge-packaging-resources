## Buyer question

What should a buyer prepare before asking a source factory to review child-resistant style paper boxes, cartons, tin boxes, exit bags or regulated-product packaging?

## Short answer

The buyer should prepare the product category, target market, required standard or document request, product size and weight, opening method, closure direction, material direction, artwork status, sales channel and whether a third-party test or legal review is required before claim language is used.

## RFQ details to prepare

1. Product category: vape, pre-roll, CBD, wellness, supplement, regulated retail product or another category.
2. Target market: United States, Canada, Europe, UAE, Saudi, Middle East or another destination.
3. Required standard, document request or buyer-side compliance checklist.
4. Product dimensions, product weight and retail unit count.
5. Packaging format: paper box, carton, tin box, exit bag, pouch or multi-piece set.
6. Opening and closure direction: tuck lock, sliding lock, push-and-slide, two-step opening, insert, seal label or other structure for review.
7. Material and printing direction: paperboard, corrugated paper, specialty paper, plastic window, surface finish, warning label area or barcode area.
8. Evidence requirement: whether a third-party lab test, buyer compliance team review or destination-market legal review is required before using any child-resistant claim.
