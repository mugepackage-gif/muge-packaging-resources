## RFQ details to prepare

- Product dimensions and packed weight.
- Box style or reference image: tuck-end carton, sleeve, window carton, tray, folding gift box or custom structure.
- Artwork status: finished dieline, rough layout, logo files, color requirements or only reference photos.
- Material direction: paperboard thickness range, corrugated need, window film, insert or divider requirement.
- Surface finish direction: matte/gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing, spot UV or simple printed finish.
- Channel requirement: retail shelf, ecommerce shipment, gift set, cosmetic set, food outer carton or electronics accessory packaging.
- Buyer-side review owner for barcode, warning text, retailer requirement and destination-market compliance.
