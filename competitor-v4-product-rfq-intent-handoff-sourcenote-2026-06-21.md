## Required Buyer Brief Inputs

### Cosmetic packaging RFQ

- product category and size
- box style
- insert or tray need
- finish reference
- artwork status
- target market or channel

### Rigid gift box RFQ

- product size and weight
- opening style
- insert requirement
- surface paper or finish reference
- packing volume
- target sales channel

### Corrugated mailer RFQ

- product size and weight
- shipping route
- mailer or shipper style
- print coverage
- insert or protection need
- quantity direction

### Paper tube and paper bag RFQ

- tube diameter and height or bag size
- cap, handle, gusset or liner need
- product weight
- display goal
- print and finish reference
- target market

### Paper packaging RFQ

- product category and size
- preferred paper packaging format
- material direction
- insert or packing need
- artwork status
- quantity direction
