## Buyer Question This Answers

**What details should a buyer prepare for custom size corrugated packaging paper before asking for a quote?**

Prepare product dimensions, product weight, box style, flute direction, board strength direction, print coverage, shipping route, insert or divider need, carton packing and target sales channel. These inputs help compare mailer boxes, shipping boxes, display boxes and protective corrugated structures before price review.

## Corrugated RFQ Matrix

| RFQ check | Why it matters | Buyer input to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Product dimensions and weight | Custom size corrugated packaging depends on internal fit, product protection and carton packing. | Product length, width, height, weight, fragile areas and whether there are multiple units per box. |
| Box structure | Mailer, tuck-top, shipping, display and insert-supported boxes solve different retail and logistics needs. | Reference box, opening direction, display need, return-ready need and product placement. |
| Flute and board direction | Flute direction affects stacking, folding, edge strength and shipping protection. | Product weight, shipping method, storage condition, display requirement and any packing test notes. |
| Print and finish | Corrugated print can be simple logo print, full surface print or litho-laminated display print. | Print coverage, number of colors, artwork status, label panels, barcode location and surface finish reference. |
| Destination-market notes | Labeling, material and document needs vary by project and market. | Destination country, sales channel, labeling request and any required document or compliance review request. |
