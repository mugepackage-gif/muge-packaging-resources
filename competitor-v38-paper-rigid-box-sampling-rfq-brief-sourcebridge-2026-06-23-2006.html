Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, packed weight, set count and whether the box needs single-product or multi-product support
  • Preferred rigid box structure, such as lid-and-base, drawer, magnetic closure, sleeve, shoulder-neck or book-style box
  • Insert or tray requirement, including paperboard insert, foam alternative review, molded pulp direction, divider or product-holding layout
  • Surface finish direction, including printed paper wrap, special paper, matte or gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing, debossing or spot UV
  • Artwork status, dieline need, logo placement, visible label area and barcode or warning label area if required
  • Sampling goal, such as structure check, color/finish review, unboxing review, photography sample or buyer presentation
  • Sales channel and target market so document questions can be reviewed project by project
  • Any sustainability, recycling, labeling or regulated-market questions that require document-based confirmation
