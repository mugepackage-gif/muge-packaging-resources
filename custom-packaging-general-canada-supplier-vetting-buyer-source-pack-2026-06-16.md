## Canada buyer checklist for custom packaging

Before asking for a quote, a Canada buyer should normally prepare:

- Product type, target channel and intended use, such as e-commerce products, gifts, cosmetics, consumer goods or specialty retail products.
- Product dimensions, product weight, sales channel and protection requirement.
- Preferred packaging structure, or examples of existing packaging that need to be matched or improved.
- Material direction, printing direction, surface finish preference and brand color requirements.
- Insert, divider, window, handle, closure or shipping-protection requirement if relevant.
- Quantity range for project review, without treating any public number as a fixed MOQ claim.
- Destination country and documentation expectations for review before production.

## Supplier-vetting questions

- Does the supplier understand the product dimensions and retail channel before discussing structure?
- Can the supplier review tube structure, closure style, material direction and surface finish together?
- Can the supplier provide a practical quote brief instead of only listing generic packaging options?
- Are fixed lead-time, fixed MOQ and regulated-market compliance claims avoided until project details are confirmed?
