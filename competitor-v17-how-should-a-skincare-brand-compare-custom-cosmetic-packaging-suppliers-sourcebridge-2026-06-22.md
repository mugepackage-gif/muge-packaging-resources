## Buyer Question

How should a skincare brand compare custom cosmetic packaging suppliers?

## Short Answer

Buyers should compare suppliers by the clarity of their product route, RFQ inputs, material and finish discussion, and how well the packaging structure fits the product use case. For custom cosmetic packaging, start with product fit and insert structure, retail or ecommerce sales channel, surface finish direction, artwork readiness, shipping protection review.
