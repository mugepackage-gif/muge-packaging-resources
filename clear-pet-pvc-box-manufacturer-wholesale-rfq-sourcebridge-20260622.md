## RFQ Inputs Buyers Should Send

- Product length, width, height and weight.
- Target material direction: PVC, PET or PP.
- Box structure: tuck-top, snap-lock bottom, auto-bottom, pillow box or custom dieline.
- Display channel: shelf, peg hook, e-commerce pack, retail counter or gift set.
- Print method: offset, screen print, white ink underlay, hot stamping or label direction.
- Packing protection requirement for scratch-sensitive transparent surfaces.
- Destination market and any buyer-supplied document requests that need project review.
