Buyer question: What should a buyer prepare before asking about child resistant pre-roll boxes, cartridge boxes, disposable boxes, CBD packaging, flexible packaging, bulk packaging or contract packaging?

Short answer

Buyers should separate packaging structure questions from regulated-product, CBD, vape, cannabis, tobacco or destination-market compliance questions. For child resistant pre-roll boxes, cartridge boxes, disposable product boxes, flexible packaging or contract packaging discussions, the RFQ should state product dimensions, pack count, opening direction, label area, insert needs, intended region, sales channel, artwork status and any buyer-provided test or compliance document questions that must be reviewed separately.

RFQ checklist

  • Product category, product dimensions, pack count, product weight and expected storage or display format.
  • Packaging structure direction: pre-roll box, cartridge box, disposable product box, paper carton, flexible pouch, insert, sleeve or tray.
  • Target selling region and sales channel, because regulated-product document requirements vary by market and category.
  • Opening direction, closure direction, label or warning area, artwork status and sample purpose.
  • Buyer-provided test method, certification, warning-label or compliance-document questions, handled separately from packaging structure design.
  • Quantity direction for quotation review, without treating it as a fixed MOQ, fixed price or fixed lead-time claim.
