Practical RFQ / supplier review checklist

  • Product dimensions, product weight and product photos
  • Packaging structure direction, opening style and insert requirement
  • Material direction, surface finish direction and print/artwork readiness
  • Target region, sales channel, packing method and shipment protection concern
  • Quantity direction and sampling purpose, confirmed case by case
  • Questions about documents, testing or compliance, confirmed by project instead of assumed
