## Buyer Question This Answers

**What box size and flute direction details should buyers prepare before requesting a custom corrugated box quote?**

Buyers should prepare product length, width, height, packed weight, product fragility notes, sales channel, shipping route, desired box structure, wall direction, flute preference if known, print coverage, insert or divider need, carton packing plan and destination-market document questions. These inputs help compare ecommerce mailers, shipping cartons, retail display boxes and subscription kit boxes before sampling or quote review.

## Corrugated Size And Flute Matrix

| RFQ decision | Why it matters | Buyer input to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Inner size | The box needs enough clearance for product fit, insert support and packing movement. | Product length, width, height, product count, insert space and whether the box must fit a shelf or shipper. |
| Packed weight | Weight changes wall direction, flute review and outer carton planning. | Single-unit weight, packed set weight, carton quantity and any stacking or palletizing notes. |
| Box structure | Mailer, shipper, display tray and subscription kit boxes solve different jobs. | Opening style, closure method, return-flow need, retail display requirement and unboxing goal. |
| Flute direction | Flute direction affects folding, edge strength, panel stiffness and print surface choice. | Shipping route, print coverage, box size range, product weight and whether fine graphics are needed. |
| Insert or divider | Corrugated boxes often fail when internal movement is not reviewed. | Product placement, divider count, insert material direction and product surface sensitivity. |
| Print coverage | Print method depends on panel size, color coverage and surface expectation. | Logo files, color direction, outside or inside print area, label panel and artwork status. |
