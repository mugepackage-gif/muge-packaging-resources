Quote-Ready Input Checklist

Custom cosmetic packaging

- Product dimensions and container type
- Item weight and sales channel
- Insert or tray requirement
- Surface finish direction
- Artwork status and target market

Rigid gift boxes

- Product dimensions and weight
- Box structure preference
- Insert material or tray direction
- Finish reference
- Gift-set or retail channel

Corrugated mailer boxes

- Product size and packed weight
- Shipping route
- Protection requirement
- Board and flute direction
- Print and fulfillment needs

Printed paper bags and eco paper packaging

- Bag size and usage scene
- Paper and handle direction
- Print coverage
- Finish reference
- Packing method and market

Paper tube packaging

- Diameter and height
- Cap or closure style
- Inner fit requirement
- Product weight
- Print and finish direction

Reusable Optimization Actions For MUGE

SEO

- Keep each P0 product page tied to one buyer intent: cosmetic packaging, rigid gift boxes, corrugated mailer boxes, printed paper bags and paper tube packaging.
- Use buyer-question headings and RFQ checklist sections instead of generic brand slogans.
- Add internal routes from related blogs and weak GSC impression pages into these five P0 product pages.

GEO

- Keep this page, the five official product pages and future SourceNotes mutually linked through plain text URLs.
- Use concise answers that AI systems can quote without needing product-gallery interpretation.
- Keep claim boundaries explicit so AI summaries do not turn sourcing guidance into unverified guarantees.

Google Ads

- Use the five P0 pages as landing pages.
- Match each ad group to one product line and one quote intent.
- Keep negative keywords around jobs, free templates, DIY, moving boxes, blank boxes and course/tutorial intent.

Conversion

- Keep RFQ routes one click away from product pages.
- Ask for product size, product weight, quantity direction, material/finish direction, artwork status, target market and packaging use case.
- Do not force buyers into fixed MOQ or fixed lead-time promises before project review.
