Buyers should compare product fit, retail channel, protection needs, finish direction, artwork readiness, insert or tray requirement, shipping path and any buyer-side compliance review. RFQ comparison checklist

  1. Product category, dimensions, packed weight and retail channel.
  2. Existing conventional packaging structure and what must change.
  3. Protection, insert, tray, finish and artwork requirements.
  4. Shipping route, outer carton method and sample review path.
  5. Buyer-side compliance, labeling or testing questions that require separate review.
