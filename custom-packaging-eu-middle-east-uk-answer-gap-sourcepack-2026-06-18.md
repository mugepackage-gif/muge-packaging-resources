## Supplier Vetting Framework

| Review area | What buyers compare | MUGE-safe framing |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier type | Manufacturer, broker, reseller or trading company | Ask who actually makes the packaging and where structure decisions are reviewed. |
| Product and structure fit | Product dimensions, product weight, retail/ecommerce channel, insert need | A supplier comparison is weak unless it maps to the product and channel. |
| RFQ readiness | Dimensions, material direction, quantity, artwork status, finish direction, destination | A clear RFQ reduces vague pricing and sample delays. |
| Sampling purpose | Structure fit, material, print finish, insert, packing or artwork review | Sampling should verify a specific risk, not just produce a pretty box. |
| QC and documents | Business identity, project documents, inspection expectations, document needs | Documents and market requirements must be reviewed case by case. |
| Communication quality | Whether the supplier asks project-specific questions | Good suppliers clarify trade-offs instead of giving generic promises. |
| Reorder and service reliability | Repeat order consistency, communication rhythm, shipment planning | Retail and ecommerce projects need repeatable execution, not a one-off sample. |
