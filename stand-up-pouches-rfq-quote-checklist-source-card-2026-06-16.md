## RFQ Fields Buyers Should Prepare

| Field | Buyer Input | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product type, product dimensions, target fill weight or volume, use scenario. | Defines pouch size, gusset, sealing area and product-fit review. |
| Pouch structure | Stand up pouch, spout pouch, zipper pouch, side-gusset variation or hang-hole requirement. | Different structures change tooling, artwork layout, closure choice and sample review. |
| Closure and dispensing | Zipper, tear notch, spout, cap, valve, resealable need or one-time-use requirement. | Closure choice affects user experience, filling method and quote review. |
| Material and barrier expectation | Product sensitivity, shelf/display condition, flexibility, opacity, window and finish preference. | Material selection should be reviewed by product use and buyer requirements instead of generic claims. |
| Printing and finish | Artwork status, color count, finish direction, matte/gloss, metallic effect or label strategy. | Print and finish details influence proofing, sample appearance and production review. |
| Sample and artwork review | Need for blank sample, printed sample, dieline review, artwork proof and approval process. | Helps avoid structure mismatch, artwork distortion or unclear sign-off. |
| Packing and shipping context | Inner pack, master carton, e-commerce shipment, retail display or destination-market needs. | Shipment and retail context can influence pouch thickness, cartonization and handling review. |
| Commercial review | Expected order range, target launch timing, quote currency, delivery destination and sample urgency. | Allows supplier to review feasibility without promising fixed MOQ, fixed lead time or fixed price. |

## Supplier Comparison Checks

- Ask suppliers to explain structure and material assumptions, not only unit price.
- Check whether quoted pouch size matches actual fill volume and product behavior.
- Confirm whether artwork, sample, plate/tooling or shipping assumptions are separated in the quote.
- Record any unclear claims for human confirmation before ordering.
