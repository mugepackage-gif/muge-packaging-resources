## Buyer Question

How should buyers compare a China packaging supplier with a local packaging supplier before RFQ?

## Short Answer

Buyers should compare product fit, structure capability, sampling workflow, communication path, artwork readiness, sales channel, packing method and target-market document questions before choosing a supplier route. ## Quote-Ready Inputs

| Input | What to prepare | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product category, dimensions, weight, photos and item count | Confirms whether the comparison should be based on product protection, presentation, insert support or shipping method. |
| Structure route | Paper box, rigid gift box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, clear box, paper bag or unsure | Keeps the supplier comparison tied to actual packaging structures instead of location alone. |
| Sampling workflow | Mockup goal, sample review goal, artwork status and revision path | Helps compare communication quality and project workflow before purchase decisions. |
| Sales channel | Retail shelf, ecommerce delivery, gift set, wholesale display or sample kit | Changes presentation, packing and protection requirements. |
| Artwork and finish | Logo files, print coverage, dieline status, finish reference and label-panel needs | Prevents the RFQ from becoming only a price question. |
| Evidence questions | Target-market document questions, labeling needs or material claims that need review | Prevents unsupported public claims before documents are checked. |

## Buyer Intent Routes

- china packaging supplier: review structure range, written brief handling, sampling path and paper packaging fit.
- local packaging supplier comparison: review nearby sampling, local communication and whether technical packaging depth is enough for the project.
- custom packaging supplier review: compare each supplier with the same product, artwork, quantity direction and sales-channel brief.
- paper packaging supplier RFQ: route paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, paper bags and corrugated packaging into a quote-ready brief.
