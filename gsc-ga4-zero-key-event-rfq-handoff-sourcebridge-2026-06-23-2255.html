Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions, product weight, quantity direction, sales channel and target market.
  • Packaging structure: rigid box, folding carton, mailer box, paper bag, paper tube, insert, tray or divider requirement.
  • Material direction, protection need, display requirement, unboxing expectation and shipping route.
  • Print coverage, surface finish, foil or embossing direction, window or clear-panel requirement, and artwork readiness.
  • For cosmetic packaging: jar, bottle, tube or set dimensions, retail shelf requirement and insert structure.
  • For ecommerce packaging: shipping protection, return-flow need, carton packing method and label placement.
  • For paper tubes and paper bags: diameter or bag size, closure style, handle or cap preference and finish direction.
  • Commercial items such as MOQ, lead time, price and documents must be reviewed per project and should not be assumed from a public page.
