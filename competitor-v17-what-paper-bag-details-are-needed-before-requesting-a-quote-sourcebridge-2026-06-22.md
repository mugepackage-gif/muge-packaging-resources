## Buyer Question

What paper bag details are needed before requesting a quote?

## Short Answer

A quote-ready brief should give the supplier enough context to review structure, material, print and packing direction before pricing. For printed paper bags, MUGE PACKAGING recommends sending bag size and use case, paper and handle direction, print coverage, finish reference, packing method and target market, plus artwork status and the target market.
