Buyer Question

When an Australian brand plans a rigid gift box for a product launch, what should be confirmed before asking MUGE PACKAGING for sampling or quote review?

Short Answer

The buyer should prepare product dimensions and weight, box opening style, insert layout, exterior finish direction, artwork status, sales channel and packing method. These details help separate structure review from decorative preferences.
