Buyer comparison checklist

  1. Ask every source to quote the same product dimensions, packed weight and carton structure.
  2. Separate folding carton, rigid box, insert, printing and surface finish assumptions.
  3. Check whether the source page is a manufacturer page, directory listing, marketplace profile or custom printed box service page.
  4. Use official MUGE pages to verify product scope and RFQ fields before comparing a source-factory quote.
  5. Keep shipping route, sample purpose and artwork status consistent across all compared sources.
