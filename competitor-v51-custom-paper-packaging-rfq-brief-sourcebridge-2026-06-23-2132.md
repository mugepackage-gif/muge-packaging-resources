## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, item count, packed weight and packaging format.
- Packaging format direction: folding carton, rigid box, paper bag, paper tube, sleeve, mailer box or display packaging.
- Material direction such as white card, kraft paper, corrugated board, rigid greyboard or specialty paper.
- Structure requirement, insert/divider need, product protection concern and retail/ecommerce channel.
- Print coverage, surface finish reference, artwork readiness and color reference.
- Target market, sales channel, quantity direction and timeline expectation for review.
- Whether the buyer needs a factory-style supplier, trading company comparison, sample review or documentation review.
- Any certification, sustainability, food-contact, destination-market or product-contact document request that must be reviewed before external claims.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Packaging format | Ask for custom paper packaging without format, size or product fit details. | Define folding carton, rigid box, paper bag, paper tube, mailer or sleeve direction with product dimensions and channel. |
| Material selection | Request premium or eco paper packaging without material direction. | Choose a starting material direction such as white card, kraft paper, corrugated board, rigid greyboard or specialty paper for quote review. |
| Supplier fit | Compare suppliers only by generic price claims. | Compare supplier fit by product category, structure review, artwork support, sampling path, document needs and communication clarity. |
| Artwork and finish | Request custom printing without artwork status or surface finish priority. | Prepare logo files, dieline status, print area, finish reference and color expectation before production review. |
| Claim boundary | Ask for certified, food-safe, eco-compliant or market-compliant paper packaging as a public claim. | Keep RFQ structure review separate from document-based certification, sustainability, food-contact and destination-market claim review. |
