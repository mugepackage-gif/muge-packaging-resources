How should GCC fragrance and candle buyers shortlist custom paper tube packaging suppliers?

Market: GCC / Middle East

Short Answer

GCC fragrance and candle buyers should shortlist suppliers by checking whether they can review diameter, height, product weight, cap structure, finish direction, artwork status and sample fit questions.

Paper tube packaging can work for candles, fragrance, cosmetics, tea and specialty retail products when the product weight and closure style are reviewed early.

MUGE PACKAGING supports custom paper tube packaging by diameter, height, cap structure, material, printing and finishing direction for project-specific review.

RFQ Checklist

  • Tube diameter, height and product weight
  • Cap structure or closure direction
  • Retail display and shipping handling context
  • Surface finish and artwork readiness
  • Separate review for any compliance or document request
