Supplier Review Checklist

  • Product category fit: paper boxes, rigid gift boxes, cosmetic packaging boxes, paper tube packaging, PVC/PET clear boxes or corrugated mailer boxes.
  • Project fit: retail packaging, e-commerce packaging, gift set, sample kit, launch set or replacement packaging program.
  • Structure review: box style, dimensions, insert or divider requirement, opening style, display need and shipping route.
  • Printing and finish review: artwork status, print area, color direction, foil, spot UV, embossing, matte or gloss finish and sample reference.
  • Document discussion: material document, artwork proof, sample photo, dieline and buyer-provided destination-market requirements.
  • RFQ inputs: size, material, quantity, structure, finish, artwork file, target market, delivery country and packaging-use scenario.
  • Communication checks: whether the supplier can answer product-specific questions without forcing one generic box solution.
