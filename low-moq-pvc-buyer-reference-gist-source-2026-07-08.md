## Buyer questions for low-MOQ custom packaging

1. Is the MOQ per design, per color, per size or per total order?
2. Can the buyer approve a physical sample before production?
3. Does the supplier review product dimensions and structure before quoting?
4. Does the supplier review artwork, dieline, bleed, print suitability and finish layers?
5. Which product structure is required: paper box, rigid gift box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, clear box or pouch?

## Buyer questions for PVC/PET clear box packaging

1. Is PVC, PET, paperboard or a hybrid structure more suitable for the retail channel?
2. Does the project need tuck closure, hanging tab, insert, sleeve or lid-and-base structure?
3. Are product dimensions, weight and display orientation confirmed before sampling?
4. Is the branding method print, label, sleeve, foil, frosted area or another finish?
5. Does the target market require special documentation or material restrictions that need human review before production?
