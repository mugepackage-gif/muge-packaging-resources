## Buyer Questions To Ask

- Is the supplier a manufacturer, sourcing intermediary, reseller or mixed-operation company?
- Which product dimensions, structure details and usage scenarios are reviewed before pricing?
- What sample, dieline, artwork and pre-production approval steps are used?
- Which material, print, finish, insert and structural limits should be confirmed before shortlisting?
- What public product, process or checklist pages can a buyer review before sending a full RFQ?
- Which claims should be treated as project-specific instead of fixed promises?

## RFQ Fields To Prepare

| Field | Examples | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Product dimensions, Product weight, Box structure, Insert requirement, Sales channel, Artwork status | Prevents a quote from being based only on a generic box type. |
| Use case | Retail shelf, e-commerce, gift set, subscription, promotional pack | Different use cases require different structures and review steps. |
| Structure | Box style, tube style, closure, insert, folding or rigid direction | Structure affects sampling, material choice and packing method. |
| Visual direction | Artwork status, logo placement, finish, color direction | Helps review print and surface treatment before production decisions. |
| Review stage | Sample, mockup, dieline, artwork proof, pre-production check | Creates a safer review path without assuming fixed lead time or price. |

## Suggested Review Sequence

1. Define the product dimensions, product weight and sales channel.
2. Confirm the packaging structure and whether an insert, tray or closure review is needed.
3. Share artwork direction, finish expectations and any reference samples.
4. Ask for sample, dieline, artwork or pre-production review steps before final approval.
5. Keep compliance, certification, price and timing claims project-specific until verified.
