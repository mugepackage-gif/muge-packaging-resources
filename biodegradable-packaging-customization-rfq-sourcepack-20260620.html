RFQ Preparation Checklist

  • Product category: cosmetics, gifts, e-commerce goods, wellness products, food-adjacent retail items or specialty retail packaging.
  • Packaging structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, sleeve, insert, divider, mailer or shipping box.
  • Material direction: paperboard, kraft paper, recycled-content direction, paper tube structure or other buyer-requested material option for review.
  • Claim wording to verify: biodegradable, recyclable, compostable, plastic-free, recycled, FSC-related or other environmental wording requested by the buyer.
  • Documents needed: material sheet, supplier-provided document, buyer-required certificate, artwork proof or destination-market documentation request.
  • Product-contact question: whether the packaging touches the product directly or only works as an outer retail box.
  • Printing and finish needs: ink direction, coating, lamination, foil, spot UV, embossing, window area or minimal-finish route.
  • RFQ inputs: size, quantity, product weight, target market, design file, reference photo, sampling need and shipping country.
