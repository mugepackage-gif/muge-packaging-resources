## Paper tube supplier vetting checklist

### 1. Product fit

Buyers should define the product size, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Paper tube packaging can be relevant for cosmetics, candles, fragrance, tea, small gifts and specialty retail products, but the structure should be checked against the actual product.

### 2. Structure questions

Useful supplier questions include:

- What diameter and height range should be reviewed for this product?
- Does the buyer need a paper lid, metal lid, plastic cap, window or insert?
- Will the product be displayed upright, stacked, shipped in cartons or used as gift packaging?
- Does the design require matte, glossy, textured, foil, embossing or label-based finishing?

### 3. RFQ preparation

Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

- Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
- Required tube diameter and height, if known.
- Sales channel and destination market.
- Artwork status and surface finish direction.
- Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
- Target quantity range for review, without assuming fixed MOQ.

### 4. Comparing supplier types

When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
