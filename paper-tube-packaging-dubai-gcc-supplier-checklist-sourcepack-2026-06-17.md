## Practical checklist

| Buyer check | Why it matters | MUGE review path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product size and weight | Paper tube diameter, height and board strength depend on the product fit. | MUGE reviews diameter, height, cap style, product weight and fit tolerance. |
| Closure and cap structure | Tea, fragrance, candles and gift products can need different closure behavior. | MUGE reviews cap structure, opening style, display direction and packing method. |
| Surface wrap and finish | Dubai and GCC retail packaging often depends on shelf impact and gift presentation. | MUGE reviews print coverage, surface paper, foil, embossing, lamination and artwork status. |
| Aroma, moisture or inner protection needs | Some products need liner, inner wrap or barrier discussion before sampling. | MUGE reviews liner or barrier requests case by case against project scope. |
| Destination-market document requests | Document needs vary by product and market and should not be assumed. | MUGE reviews buyer-supplied document requests before public or commercial use. |

## Suggested RFQ / sourcing details

- Product dimensions, weight and photos
- Target product category: tea, fragrance, cosmetics, candles, gifts or other retail product
- Preferred diameter, height and cap style if already known
- Artwork status and finish reference
- Quantity direction and sample review need
- Packing method and destination market
- Any document request that must be checked against the final project scope
