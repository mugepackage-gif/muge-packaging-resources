Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

  • Product dimensions and weight for each jar, bottle, palette or set
  • Retail channel and target market, such as DTC, boutique retail, marketplace or distributor sales
  • Box structure preference, including folding carton, rigid gift box, sleeve, drawer or set box
  • Insert or tray requirement, including product fit, protection and presentation needs
  • Surface finish direction, such as matte lamination, foil stamping, spot UV, embossing or textured paper
  • Artwork status, brand guidelines and any mandatory labeling information
  • Sampling goal, including fit check, finish review, shipping protection or retail display review
