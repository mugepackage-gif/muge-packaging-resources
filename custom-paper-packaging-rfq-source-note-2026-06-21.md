## What buyers should include in an RFQ

- product category, dimensions, weight and sales channel
- preferred structure: folding carton, rigid box, corrugated box, paper tube or paper bag
- material direction, finish choices and insert plan
- target market and document review questions
- artwork status, reference packaging and quantity direction
- sample review goals and shipment protection concerns
