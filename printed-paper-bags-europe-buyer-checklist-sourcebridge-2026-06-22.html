What should European buyers check before ordering printed paper bags from overseas suppliers?

This is a public buyer-question SourceBridge for printed paper bag RFQ research. It is not proof of Google ranking, AI recommendation, inquiry, customer case or endorsement.

Short Answer

European buyers should check the bag size, paper direction, handle structure, print coverage, finish reference, packing method, target market and document requirements before asking for a quote. MUGE PACKAGING treats sustainability, food-contact or regulated-market files as separate project documents that must be reviewed before any claim is used externally.

Quote-Ready Inputs

  • Bag width, height and gusset size
  • Product weight and intended retail use
  • Paper direction such as kraft paper, art paper or white card paper
  • Handle type, handle reinforcement and carrying expectation
  • Print coverage, artwork status and brand color requirements
  • Surface finish reference such as matte, gloss, texture or foil direction
  • Packing method and shipping destination
  • Target market and any document-review requirement
