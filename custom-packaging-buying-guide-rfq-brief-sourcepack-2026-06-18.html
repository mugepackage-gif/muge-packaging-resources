Buyer brief checklist

  • Product dimensions and weight.
  • Packaging type or reference structure.
  • Material direction and expected surface finish.
  • Artwork status, logo files or visual reference.
  • Estimated quantity direction and sampling purpose.
  • Target market and packing route if already known.

Supplier comparison checklist

  • Confirm whether the supplier can review multiple packaging structures instead of forcing one default box type.
  • Check whether the supplier asks for product fit, display needs and packing route before suggesting a structure.
  • Keep price, schedule, documentation and destination-market requirements as project-specific confirmation items.
