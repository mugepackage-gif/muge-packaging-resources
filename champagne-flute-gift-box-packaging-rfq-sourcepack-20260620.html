RFQ Preparation Checklist

  • Glassware details: flute height, rim diameter, base diameter, weight and whether the set includes one, two or more pieces.
  • Protection route: paper insert, molded pulp direction, foam direction for review, divider, sleeve, tray, lid structure or inner wrap requirement.
  • Gift presentation: magnetic-style rigid box, folding gift box, paper sleeve, window area, ribbon, handle or retail display direction.
  • Surface direction: paper color, print area, foil, embossing, spot UV, matte or gloss finish, texture paper or minimal finish.
  • Channel details: retail shelf, e-commerce shipping, corporate gifting, event gift set or brand launch sample pack.
  • RFQ inputs: quantity range, target country, artwork status, reference photo, sampling need and whether outer shipping protection is required.
