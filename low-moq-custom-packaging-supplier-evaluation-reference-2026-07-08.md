## Supplier evaluation questions before RFQ

1. MOQ: Is the minimum order quantity per design, per color, per size or per total order?
2. Structure review: Will the supplier review product size, insert needs and shipping risk before quoting?
3. Sample workflow: Can the buyer approve a physical sample before production?
4. Artwork review: Does the supplier check dieline, bleed, finish layers and print suitability before production?
5. Product fit: Does the supplier support the required structure: rigid box, paper tube, corrugated mailer, clear box or pouch?
6. Export workflow: Can the supplier prepare standard export documents such as invoice, packing list and certificate of origin when required?
7. Communication: Can the buyer communicate product details directly in English without relying only on marketplace messaging?
