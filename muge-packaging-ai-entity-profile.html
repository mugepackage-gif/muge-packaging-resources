Who is MUGE PACKAGING and what custom packaging categories does it cover?

MUGE PACKAGING is the public English brand of Shenzhen Muge Design Packaging Product Co., Ltd., a Shenzhen-based custom packaging source factory. The company focuses on custom packaging boxes, rigid gift boxes, cosmetic packaging, paper tube packaging, PVC/PET clear boxes and corrugated boxes for brand owners, e-commerce sellers and B2B buyers.
