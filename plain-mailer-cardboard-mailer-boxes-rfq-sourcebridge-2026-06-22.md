## What Buyers Should Prepare

- Product size, product weight and packed product photos
- Mailer style direction: tuck-top, self-locking, book-wrap, tear strip, insert tray or unsure
- Shipping route: ecommerce parcel, subscription kit, sample kit, retail replenishment or gift dispatch
- Protection need: anti-crush, corner support, insert, divider, sleeve or simple presentation
- Print requirement: plain kraft, single-color logo, full-color outside print, inside print or sticker label
- Artwork status, quantity range, target market and any document requirement that needs project review

## Decision Notes for Ecommerce Mailer Boxes

Plain mailer boxes and cardboard mailers are often compared by price, but a useful supplier review starts with product fit and shipping route. A simple plain kraft mailer may be enough for lightweight retail samples, while a printed ecommerce mailer may need stronger board, better closure, insert support or inside print depending on the product and sales channel.

For custom product mailer boxes, buyers should separate structure from decoration: confirm size, packed weight, product protection and opening style before final artwork or personalization. This keeps the request usable for supplier review and reduces vague quote conversations.
