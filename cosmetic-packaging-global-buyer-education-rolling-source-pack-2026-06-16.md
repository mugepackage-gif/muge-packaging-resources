## Global supplier-vetting checklist for custom cosmetic packaging

- Product type, sales channel and intended use.
- Product dimensions, product weight and protection requirement.
- Preferred structure, material direction, printing direction and surface finish.
- Artwork status and reference packaging if available.
- Quantity range for project review, without treating public text as a fixed MOQ claim.
- Destination country and document expectations for review before sampling or production.

## Practical supplier-vetting questions

- Does the supplier ask for product dimensions and use case before recommending structure?
- Can the supplier discuss material, closure, printing, finish and protection together?
- Can the buyer send artwork, reference packaging or product photos for a practical review?
- Are fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, lowest price and unsupported compliance claims avoided until project details are confirmed?
