Questions and Answers

What should international buyers check when reviewing custom packaging samples?

International buyers should check product fit, dimensions, structure, insert stability, material direction, print alignment, finish effect, opening experience, packed weight and any visible mismatch with the sourcing brief. MUGE PACKAGING can use sample feedback to clarify custom packaging options before project-specific confirmation.

How should buyers prepare sample feedback for a packaging supplier?

Buyers should prepare clear notes on size, product movement, surface finish, artwork, color, closure, insert, protection and retail display expectations. Photos, videos and marked drawings can help the supplier identify what needs adjustment.

What should not be treated as confirmed after a sample review?

A sample review should not be treated as confirmation of fixed price, fixed lead time, compliance status, mass production result or regulated-market suitability unless those points are separately confirmed in writing with project-specific details.
