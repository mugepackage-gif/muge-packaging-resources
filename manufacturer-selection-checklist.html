Packaging Manufacturer Selection Checklist for Brand Owners

Selecting the right packaging manufacturer is a make-or-break decision for brand owners. This checklist covers the essential evaluation criteria: certification verification, factory audit capability, sample quality assessment, communication proficiency, and payment term negotiation.
