## What buyers should prepare before asking for a quote

For a useful paper can or paper tube packaging review, buyers should prepare:

1. Product diameter, height, weight and fit tolerance.
2. Cap preference, opening direction and closure style.
3. Liner, insert, aroma, oil or moisture review needs if relevant.
4. Artwork status, print coverage and finish direction.
5. Target quantity and destination market.
6. Retail, ecommerce, gift-set or distribution channel.
