## Supplier fit

- Confirm whether the supplier handles the required packaging type: folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, clear PVC/PET boxes, corrugated boxes or mixed packaging sets.
- Check whether the supplier can review product dimensions, quantity direction, material preference, structure, artwork status and target market before suggesting a package.
- Ask for a project-specific review instead of assuming one standard structure fits every product.

## RFQ information buyers should prepare

- Product dimensions and weight.
- Packaging structure or reference photo.
- Material direction, print method and finish preference.
- Estimated quantity and sample expectation.
- Destination market and packing or shipping route if known.
