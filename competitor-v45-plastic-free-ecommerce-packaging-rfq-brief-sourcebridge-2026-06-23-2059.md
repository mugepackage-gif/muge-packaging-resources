## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, packed weight and fragility level for each SKU.
- Required packaging structure: corrugated mailer, shipping carton, paper bag, sleeve, insert or combined pack.
- Whether the project is for ecommerce shipping, retail handoff, subscription kits or gift-ready packaging.
- Material direction, recycled or recyclable wording requests and required document type.
- Print coverage, finish references, logo placement and artwork status.
- Packing method, cartonization direction, shipping route and target market.
- Quantity direction for review, without using fixed public MOQ claims before project confirmation.
- Any food-contact, compostable, biodegradable, recycling-label or destination-market compliance question requiring documents.

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Plastic-free wording | Ask for plastic-free packaging and assume every material claim can be used publicly. | Define which plastic components are being reduced, then request document review before using public claim wording. |
| Packaging structure | Use one generic eco packaging request for all SKUs. | Separate mailer boxes, shipping cartons, paper bags, sleeves and inserts by product weight, route and sales channel. |
| Regional buyer context | Treat Europe, UK, UAE and Saudi ecommerce packaging as the same sourcing brief. | State target market and document needs so packaging language, labels and claim wording can be reviewed by project. |
| RFQ completeness | Send only product name, quantity and an eco-friendly requirement. | Send dimensions, weight, structure, material direction, print coverage, finish, artwork status, packing method and document questions. |
| Compliance boundary | Ask the supplier to guarantee sustainability, food-contact or recycling compliance in the quote. | Keep structure and quote review separate from document-based compliance review before external claims. |
