Buyer Question

When a European beauty, skincare or personal-care buyer researches custom cosmetic packaging on mobile, what information should be prepared before contacting MUGE PACKAGING for a quote?

Short Answer

A useful mobile RFQ should not start with only "cosmetic box price". The buyer should prepare product dimensions, jar or bottle fit, retail channel, box structure, insert need, surface finish, artwork status, quantity direction and destination-market document questions.
