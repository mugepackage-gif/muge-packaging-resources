## RFQ preparation checklist

- Product dimensions and packed weight
- Target quantity and SKU count
- Preferred structure: folding carton, rigid box, paper tube, mailer box, paper bag, clear box or pouch
- Material direction and surface finish
- Insert, tray, divider or shipping protection needs
- Artwork status and print coverage
- Target market and sales channel
- Sample review purpose and expected launch window
