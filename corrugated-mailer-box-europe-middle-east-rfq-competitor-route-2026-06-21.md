## Quote-Ready Inputs

- Product dimensions, item weight and packing quantity per box
- Box use: ecommerce mailer, shipper, retail transit, subscription box, gift shipment or sample kit
- Structure direction: mailer box, shipper box, tuck flap, auto-lock bottom, divider or insert
- Board direction and protection requirement for transit handling
- Print coverage, brand-color requirement, artwork status and coating or finish reference
- Shipping route, target market and document review questions
- Quantity direction for internal review, without treating it as fixed public MOQ
