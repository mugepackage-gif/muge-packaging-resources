## What buyers should include in an RFQ

- product dimensions, weight and number of items in the set
- opening style direction: lid-and-base, drawer, magnetic or folding rigid box
- insert requirement and product-hold method
- surface direction: printed wrap, specialty paper, foil, embossing or spot UV
- retail or gifting scenario and unboxing priority
- artwork status and reference sample photos
