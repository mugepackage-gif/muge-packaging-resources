    What buyers should prepare before comparing suppliers

  • Product type, dimensions, weight and intended sales channel.
  • Preferred packaging structure or reference packaging photos.
  • Artwork status, logo placement and surface finish direction.
  • Insert, divider, window, handle, closure or shipping-protection requirements.
  • Quantity direction for project review, without treating any public number as fixed MOQ.
  • Destination market and document requests for review before public or commercial use.
