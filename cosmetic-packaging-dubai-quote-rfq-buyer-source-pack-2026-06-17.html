    • Cosmetic packaging RFQ checklist

    1. Product fit

    Buyers should define the product size, packed weight, display direction, closure preference and protection needs before asking for samples. Cosmetic packaging projects may involve skincare jars, serum boxes, makeup sets, perfume sleeves, beauty gift sets, paper tubes, rigid boxes, folding cartons or clear display packaging; the right structure should be reviewed against product dimensions, surface finish, protection needs, retail display and brand presentation.

    2. Structure questions

    Useful supplier questions include:

  • What product type is being packed: skincare jar, serum bottle, makeup item, perfume item, gift set or retail kit?
  • Does the buyer need a rigid box, folding carton, paper tube, clear PVC/PET box, insert, tray, sleeve or e-commerce outer carton?
  • Will the packaging be used for retail display, beauty gifting, online fulfillment, marketplace sales or mixed Dubai and Middle East sales channels?
  • Which artwork, surface finish, window direction, insert structure and sample review details should be prepared before quote comparison?

    • 3. RFQ preparation

    Before requesting a quote, buyers should prepare:

  • Product dimensions and approximate packed weight.
  • Preferred packaging structure, size direction and material direction, if known.
  • Sales channel and destination market.
  • Artwork status and surface finish direction.
  • Closure style, insert requirement and packing method.
  • Target quantity range for review, without assuming fixed MOQ.

    • 4. Comparing supplier types

    When comparing a source factory, trading company or platform seller, buyers should check whether the supplier can discuss structure, material direction, finish, sample review and export communication together. The correct choice depends on project complexity, design readiness and review requirements.
