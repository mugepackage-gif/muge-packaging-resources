RFQ Checklist

  • Product category, dimensions and packed weight
  • Packaging format direction, such as folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, clear PET/PVC box or set packaging
  • Insert or tray requirement for jars, bottles, serums, palettes or sets
  • Artwork status, logo files, color reference, copy area and barcode or label space
  • Surface finish reference, such as matte, gloss, foil, embossing, spot UV, texture or window
  • Quantity direction for review, without treating this as a fixed MOQ claim
  • Retail channel, e-commerce channel, Amazon, DTC or launch context
  • Destination market and document questions that need project-specific review
  • Sampling purpose: structure fit, material feel, finish, artwork, insert or packing review
