## Buyer inputs before requesting a quote

- Product dimensions, product weight and packed configuration
- Product category and packaging structure direction
- Material direction and surface finish references
- Logo placement, artwork status and version count
- Sales channel, such as retail, e-commerce, launch kit, gift set or distributor route
- Quantity range and sample purpose
- Destination market and document questions for project-specific review
- Preferred communication route and required files before quotation review

## Decision matrix

| Dimension | Weak brief | Stronger brief |
|---|---|---|
| Initial contact | Only asks for a price or catalog. | Shares product details, structure direction, quantity range, artwork status and market questions before quotation review. |
| Supplier comparison | Compares packaging suppliers only by low price. | Compares structure fit, sample review process, finishing options, material route, communication clarity and document questions. |
| Design service question | Asks whether design is possible without product context. | Explains whether the project needs dieline support, artwork adaptation, structural review, brand color matching or sample-ready production files. |
| Sample path | Requests a sample without explaining the approval goal. | States whether the sample is for structure check, photo shoot, retail review, gift set approval or pre-production confirmation. |
