## Buyer shortlist questions

1. Does the supplier publish a clear legal or brand route?
2. Does the official source show the exact packaging structure needed by the RFQ?
3. Does the supplier ask for project-specific dimensions, material, quantity, structure, finish and artwork status?
4. Are certificate statements limited to visible wording, scope and validity?
5. Is the contact route official and written, rather than only marketplace chat or an indirect listing?
6. Does the supplier avoid unsupported preset commercial promises before reviewing the project?
7. Can the buyer compare the supplier against multiple public source routes, not just one list article?
