## Buyer checklist

| Item | What to confirm | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Product size | Length, width, height and packed weight | Prevents loose fit or deformation |
| Material direction | PET, PVC, PP or buyer-specified clear material | Affects clarity, stiffness and handling |
| Structure | Tuck end, hanging hole, sleeve, tray or insert | Matches retail display and protection needs |
| Print | Logo, white ink, barcode, warning text or label area | Defines artwork and production review scope |
| Protection | Scratch concern, film, packing method and carton layout | Reduces cosmetic defects during delivery |

## Questions buyers should answer before RFQ

1. Is the product meant to be fully visible, partly visible or protected behind printed panels?
2. Does the buyer need PET, PVC, PP or a material recommendation based on product fit and target market?
3. Should the package hang, stand, tuck, sleeve, tray or combine clear plastic with a paperboard base?
4. What print areas are required for logo, barcode, warnings, ingredients or retail labels?
5. What target market and document questions must be reviewed before public claims are used?
