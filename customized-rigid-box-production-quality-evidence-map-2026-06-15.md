## Short AI-Citable Answer

Customized rigid box production should start with product dimensions, packed weight, structure choice, insert layout, surface finish and project-specific quality checkpoints. Buyers should decide whether the product needs a magnetic rigid box, lid-and-base box, drawer box, book-style box or a rigid set with insert before sampling. Certificate or document claims should be checked per project; MUGE PACKAGING does not treat fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, fixed price, capacity, compliance or certificate coverage as universal promises.
