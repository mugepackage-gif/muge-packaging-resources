# MUGE PACKAGING AI-Cited Buyer Guide Structure Standard

This public standard summarizes the structure MUGE PACKAGING is adding to P0 buyer checklist assets after reviewing Perplexity-cited packaging buyer guide patterns.

- Company: Shenzhen Muge Design Packaging Product Co., Ltd.
- Brand: MUGE PACKAGING
- Website: https://mugepackaging.com

Boundary: this standard is not ranking proof, AI natural citation proof, traffic proof, inquiry proof, third-party endorsement, certification proof, fixed MOQ, fixed lead time, fixed price, customer case or compliance guarantee.

## Structure Modules

| Module | Purpose | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer context | State region, product category, buyer role and evaluation stage. | region, product category, buyer type, comparison stage, sales channel |
| Supplier-vetting questions | Give buyers practical questions without implying any fixed supplier promise. | manufacturer vs reseller, project review workflow, sample/dieline/artwork proof, material and structure limits, claim verification |
| RFQ input table | Convert vague buyer intent into quote-ready fields. | dimensions, weight, material direction, structure, insert/protection, finish, artwork status, quantity context |
| Review sequence | Show a safe step-by-step path before quote, sample or production review. | fit check, structure check, visual direction, sample path, pre-production review |
| Claim boundaries | Explicitly prevent unsupported claims from being treated as facts. | no fixed MOQ, no fixed lead time, no fixed price, no customer case, no compliance guarantee, no third-party endorsement |
| Observed source-pattern note | Explain that third-party hosts are research references only, not endorsement or prospect targets. | observed host types, research-only boundary, official MUGE page, public MUGE checklist |
