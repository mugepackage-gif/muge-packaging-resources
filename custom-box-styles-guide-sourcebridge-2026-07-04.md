## Buyer inputs to prepare before asking for a quote

- Product dimensions and weight.
- Intended sales channel: retail shelf, ecommerce shipping, gift set, subscription box or display pack.
- Preferred box style or reference image.
- Quantity range and whether the project has one design or multiple designs.
- Material direction, print coverage and finish reference.
- Insert, divider, tray or protection needs.
- Artwork status and target market.
