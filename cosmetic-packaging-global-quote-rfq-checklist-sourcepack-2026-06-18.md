## Quote / RFQ Checklist

| Review area | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| Product fit | Product type, dimensions, weight, fit tolerance, fragile points and set combination |
| Packaging structure | Folding carton, rigid gift box, paper tube, sleeve, tray, insert, window box or mixed structure |
| Insert or support | Inner tray, paperboard insert, divider, cushion, sleeve support or no insert |
| Surface direction | Material direction, print files, color reference, foil, UV, emboss, deboss, matte or gloss finish |
| Sales channel | Retail shelf, DTC/e-commerce, Amazon, gift set, distributor, launch kit or sample kit |
| Artwork readiness | Editable artwork, dieline status, logo files, color system and reference photos |
| Quantity direction | Estimated order range for supplier review; final MOQ and price require project confirmation |
| Destination review | Buyer-supplied market, labeling or document requests for project-by-project checking |
| Sampling purpose | Structure fit, visual review, material comparison, insert check or pre-production review |
| Timing target | Target review window only; final schedule requires project confirmation |
