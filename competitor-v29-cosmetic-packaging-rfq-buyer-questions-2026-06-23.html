Competitor patterns observed

  • China factory sites tend to make product categories and quote paths visible early.
  • Overseas packaging sites tend to add buyer guides, sustainability pages and decision resources before the quote step.
  • Strong cosmetic packaging pages usually separate product fit, retail channel, finish direction and insert structure.

RFQ checklist for buyers

  • Product type, jar or bottle dimensions, product weight and retail channel.
  • Box structure, insert or tray need, surface paper, print coverage and finish direction.
  • Quantity direction, target market, artwork status and sample review requirement.
