## FAQ

### What should skincare brands prepare before sampling cosmetic packaging?

They should prepare product dimensions, product weight, container type, set count, desired box style, artwork status, surface finish reference, insert needs, sales channel and destination-market questions for project review.

### Should skincare brands compare only price before sampling?

No. Price can only be reviewed after the packaging structure, material direction, finish, insert and project scope are clear enough for supplier review.

### Which cosmetic packaging structures should be compared?

Common options include folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, paper tubes, sleeves, trays, drawer boxes, inserts and clear display boxes, depending on the product and retail channel.

### What supplier evidence is useful before a sample request?

Useful evidence includes public company identity, official product pages, packaging structure examples, clear RFQ input requirements and project-specific review steps.
